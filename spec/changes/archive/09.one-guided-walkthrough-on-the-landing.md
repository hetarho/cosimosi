# 09. Change: One guided walkthrough on the landing

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/81.landing-page](../plan/81.landing-page.md).
> Filled by /cosimosi:create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /cosimosi:create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## As-is

The shipped landing (job 120, since evolved) renders **eight** sections as a literal tuple — `hero`,
`playground`, `demo-cta-top`, `feature-tour`, `mirror`, `theory`, `blog`, `closing-cta` — and the page's
argument is split across three of them that do not talk to each other:

- **`playground`** (`ui/sections/LandingPlayground.tsx`, `model/playground.ts`,
  `ui/LandingPlaygroundScene.tsx`) is a free-form interaction: the visitor types one line, picks one of five
  moods, launches it as a single real star, drags a 365-day time slider to watch it fade, and recalls it. It
  is the page's only interaction, and it demonstrates just three of the product's behaviors (launch, fade,
  recall) on one star — no split, no accumulation, no sky color.
- **`feature-tour`** (`LandingFeatureTour.tsx`) is five static copy cards (`write` / `constellate` / `fade` /
  `revive` / `color`) with no canvas — the explanation lives below the interaction instead of inside it, so
  the visitor reads about splitting and sky color instead of watching them.
- **`mirror`** (`LandingMirror.tsx`) is a separate copy section carrying the [M5] definition
  (`landing_mirror_title`: "하늘은 평균이 아니라 거울이에요") beside two static swatch rows
  (averaged vs. re-read-weighted).

Three meta-disclaimers hedge these sections:

- `landing_play_note` — "이 별은 이 페이지에만 잠시 떠 있어요. 아무것도 저장되지 않아요."
- `landing_mirror_illustration_note` — "지어낸 기록으로 만든 예시예요. 누군가의 실제 데이터가 아니에요."
- `landing_cta_demo_note` — "가입 없이 볼 수 있어요. 아무것도 저장되지 않아요."

And the demo CTA label is `landing_cta_demo` — "움직이는 걸 보기" — a plain descriptive phrase that reads
utilitarian next to the rest of the page's voice, propped up by the note beneath it.

Everything the playground shows already runs through the shipped production functions
(`arousalToInitialStrength`, `effectiveBrightness`, seed-from-text) — the landing already mounts a real star
via its own scene, beyond plan 81's original "sky + latent field only" hero. The hero itself is untouched by
this change.

## To-be

**One authored walkthrough section replaces `playground`, `feature-tour` and `mirror`.** Instead of one
free-form toy plus two card decks of prose, the visitor steps through the product's whole arc on a single
canvas, watching each claim happen instead of reading it. The walkthrough is **fully hardcoded**: an authored,
well-written diary (fixture content, both locales), precomputed split, deterministic visuals. The visitor's
only inputs are each step's single action button and step navigation (next / restart) — no free-text input,
no mood picker, no time slider.

The six steps, in fixed order (each: a short caption + one action, named with the product's real UI verbs):

1. **쓰면 쪼개진다** — the authored diary sits on screen; pressing **별 쪼개기** (the product's verb,
   `writing_flow_split_action`) reveals its precomputed scenes.
2. **쪼개진 장면이 별이 된다** — pressing **별 띄우기** launches the scenes as stars on the walkthrough
   canvas.
3. **우주가 내가 담는 색으로 물든다** — more authored entries accumulate and the sky visibly tints toward
   the emotions the writer keeps adding.
4. **다시 찾지 않으면 어두워진다** — time passes; unvisited stars dim and their words begin to erode.
5. **떠올리면 돌아온다** — recalling one restores its brightness — and it comes back slightly changed.
6. **자주 떠올릴수록 하늘이 그쪽으로 기운다** — the [M5] mirror, absorbed from the retired `mirror`
   section: the sky is not the average of what was written but the mirror of what is re-read. This step
   carries the [M5] definition sentence; it is the walkthrough's final state, not a separate card.

Contract details:

- **The [M5] guard moves, it does not weaken.** The section tuple shrinks to
  `hero · walkthrough · demo-cta-top · theory · blog · closing-cta`; the compile-time guarantee that the
  mirror cannot be dropped now lives one level down — the walkthrough's **step tuple is a fixed six-member
  literal tuple ending in the mirror step**, so removing or reordering it fails `tsc` exactly as the section
  tuple does today.
- **Every visual is the shipped behavior**, not an animation of it: split reveal, star channels, sky tint,
  `effectiveBrightness` dimming, decay-stage word loss, recall recovery and reshape all run through the
  production functions over the authored fixture, as the playground already does.
- **All three meta-disclaimers are removed** (`landing_play_note`, `landing_mirror_illustration_note`,
  `landing_cta_demo_note`). The landing is self-evidently a marketing page; nobody mistakes its example for
  their data. [I12] honesty is untouched — it forbids overclaiming, not confidence; the theory section's
  denial sentence and the copy-honesty test remain the gate.
- **The demo CTA becomes one refined label with no note** — the register of "우주 미리보기": a quiet,
  self-sufficient invitation, top and bottom, still demo-before-signup, still targeting `/demo`.
- **All new copy** is `landing_*` in both catalogues, in the 토스-blog 해요체 voice; the copy-honesty test's
  exact-sentence allowlist (`REVIEWED_DENIALS`) is updated wherever a reviewed denial sentence changes.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope** — the walkthrough section (model + scene + fixture content), the retirement of the
`playground` / `feature-tour` / `mirror` sections and their message keys, the section tuple and its tests,
the demo-CTA copy, the removed disclaimer keys, the copy-honesty allowlist update, and a one-line amendment
to PRD §5.11 [K1] recording that steps 3 (fun-first tour) and 4 (the [M5] definition) are now delivered as
one interactive section — their content, order and required presence survive.

**Must not change:**

- **The route and the gate** — `/` public, `/universe`, `gateDecision`'s arms, `requiresSignIn` and its four
  consumers are untouched.
- **The hero** — the empty two-layer sky, its poster fallback and reduced-motion behavior stay as shipped.
- **[K1]'s surviving order** — hero before the walkthrough, the walkthrough before the theory cards, the
  [M5] definition present and ahead of theory, demo CTA top and bottom, demo-before-signup in the closing
  block ([K3]) — all still compile-time-enforced.
- **[I12]/[K2]** — no brain-equivalence, therapeutic, coordinates-as-anatomy, absent-mechanics or citation
  claim appears; the copy-honesty test keeps gating both catalogues and the shell; theory cards keep their
  citation-free type and five pillar anchors.
- **The public-page import closure ([U1])** — no transport reachable from `pages/landing`, zero API calls on
  load; the renderer ban restatement stays.
- **Locale** — auto-detect + the manual switch, `landing_*` key parity across `en.json`/`ko.json`,
  `lint:raw-strings`.
- **SEO root** — `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, the shell's tags and `noscript` block (the `noscript` [M5]
  sentence may be re-voiced with the new copy, but its presence is required).
- **`spec/values.yaml`** — no key added; walkthrough step content is content, timings come from design
  tokens.

**Non-goals:** the demo page (change 10 owns it); the theory/blog/closing sections' structure; the signup
flow; mobile (web-only waiver stands); prerendering; any BE surface.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The landing renders one walkthrough section where `playground`, `feature-tour` and `mirror` used to be;
   the retired sections' components and message keys are gone, and the section tuple is
   `hero · walkthrough · demo-cta-top · theory · blog · closing-cta`, still a compile-time literal tuple.
2. The walkthrough has exactly six steps in the fixed order — split, launch, sky-takes-your-color, fade,
   recall, mirror — as a fixed-length literal tuple ending in the mirror step; removing, reordering or adding
   a step fails `pnpm typecheck`.
3. The walkthrough is hardcoded and advance-only: an authored diary and precomputed split (both locales), no
   free-text input, no mood picker, no time slider; the visitor's only inputs are each step's single action
   and step navigation, and every run is deterministic.
4. Step actions use the product's real UI verbs where one exists (별 쪼개기 · 별 띄우기 · 회고 register),
   and each step's visual change happens on the walkthrough canvas through the shipped production functions —
   split reveal, star launch, sky tint, `effectiveBrightness` dimming with decay-stage word loss, recall
   recovery with a changed form, and the strength-weighted mirror tilt.
5. The mirror step states the [M5] definition — the sky mirrors the emotions you return to, not their
   average — in both locales; no standalone mirror section remains, and the definition cannot be dropped
   without a `tsc` failure.
6. No meta-disclaimer remains on the page: `landing_play_note`, `landing_mirror_illustration_note` and
   `landing_cta_demo_note` are removed from both catalogues and nothing equivalent replaces them.
7. Both demo CTAs are a single refined label in the "우주 미리보기" register with no explanatory note, top
   and bottom; the closing block still hardcodes demo-before-signup; targets stay `/demo` and `/signup`.
8. All copy is `landing_*` in both catalogues with identical key sets, in the 해요체 voice with no 문어체 or
   번역투; `pnpm gen:messages` output is committed, `pnpm lint:raw-strings` passes, and
   `copy-honesty.test.ts` passes with its `REVIEWED_DENIALS` allowlist updated for any re-voiced denial.
9. The public-page import closure holds: no transport is reachable from `pages/landing`, the page issues zero
   `apps/api` calls, and the hero's two-layer scene and poster fallback are unchanged.
10. PRD §5.11 [K1] carries the recorded amendment (tour + mirror delivered as one interactive section), and
    `spec/policy/ux/public-copy.md` + `spec/tech/landing-page.md` describe the as-built six-section IA.
11. `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm test:guards`, `pnpm lint:fsd:layout` and the web build
    pass; no new `steiger` exemption block is added.
