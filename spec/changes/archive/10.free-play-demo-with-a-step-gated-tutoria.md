# 10. Change: Free-play demo with a step-gated tutorial

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/79.demo-page](../plan/79.demo-page.md).
> Filled by /cosimosi:create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /cosimosi:create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

## As-is

The shipped `/demo` (job 118) is a **one-pass trailer, not a sandbox**, and its chrome does not enforce its
own script:

- **Every control is always pressable.** `DemoControlRail` renders 쪼개기 · 띄우기 · 일기 두 편 더 ·
  시간 밀기 · 회고하기 · 내 우주 시작하기 as a flat button stack; during the tour the sequence engine
  highlights the current beat's anchor, but nothing disables or de-emphasizes the others (only 회고하기 is
  `disabled` until launch). A visitor can press beat-7's button during beat 2, fire signals out of order, or
  wander off-script entirely — the "tour" is a suggestion, not a state.
- **The run state is a set of one-shot flags.** `useDemoRun`'s `DemoRunState` is
  `splitRevealed / launchedDiaryIds / recalled (boolean) / gistStage / skyFilled` over exactly one
  three-diary `DemoDiarySet`. Once the ten beats are done there is nothing left to do: no fourth diary
  exists, recall is a single boolean on one scripted memory, gist rises on one scripted memory, and the only
  exit is the signup CTA. The page is a corridor that ends at a door.
- **Time is one opaque nudge.** A single "시간 밀기" button advances the clock by
  `demo.time_travel_step_days`; the passage of time surfaces only as a small text line
  (`demo_clock_label`: "우주 시간 {date}") — nothing resembling how the product presents universe time.
- **Adding diaries is a batch cheat.** "일기 두 편 더" dumps the set's remaining two diaries at once — no
  diary appears the way the product shows one, no per-diary split/launch.
- **The copy is functional, not voiced.** "시간 밀기", "일기 두 편 더", "쪼개기", "띄우기" — terse labels
  that neither match the product's verbs (별 쪼개기 / 별 띄우기) nor the 토스-blog 해요체 register the rest
  of the product speaks.

The isolation architecture around all of this is sound and stays: the import-ban closure, no `isDemo` in
shared code, the demo-local clock, `resetUniverseUserState()` on mount, nothing persisted on leave.

## To-be

**The demo becomes a free-play simulation room with a tutorial in front of it** — the visitor first walks
the scripted beats (or skips), then stays and plays: keeps writing, launching, traveling, recalling,
watching forgetting and semanticization happen, for as long as they like. Two deltas:

### 1. The tutorial gates the chrome (FSM)

- **One explicit state machine owns the run's phase** (on the shipped XState foundation, beside plan 78's
  step engine): `tutorial(step) → free-play`, entered as `free-play` permanently once the visitor completes
  the last beat **or presses skip**. Every control's interactivity is **derived from the machine's state**,
  not from scattered booleans — during `tutorial(step)`, only the control the current beat anchors is
  interactive; in `free-play`, everything is.
- **Visual gating matches the interaction gating**: while the tutorial runs, every non-current control (and
  the taster rail, the time controls, anything not yet introduced) is dimmed **and blurred** so the eye
  cannot confuse what to press; the current control renders at full clarity with the existing highlight.
  Skip stays fully visible and interactive at all times ([O4]).
- Pressing a gated control does nothing — not a no-op handler that half-runs, but an unpressable control:
  off-script actions are unrepresentable while the tutorial owns the page.

### 2. Free play is real play

- **Writing a diary looks like the product.** A control with the product's verb (일기 쓰기 register)
  presents a **randomly drawn, prepared diary** from the fixture pool through the same UI shapes as the real
  writing flow — the diary appears as an entry, **별 쪼개기** reveals its precomputed split, **별 띄우기**
  launches it — repeatable indefinitely (draws cycle through the pool; no real write, no LLM, ever [Z1]).
  "일기 두 편 더" is retired; the tutorial's constellation beat uses the same draw-write-launch flow.
- **The fixture pool grows to sustain play.** The current three-diaries-per-set corpus is enough for a
  corridor, not a room; the pool is extended (authored diaries with precomputed splits, both locales) so a
  visitor can keep launching well past the tutorial without immediate repetition. Fixture ownership stays
  with [plan/77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](../plan/77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md) — this change extends that
  corpus and its integrity tests as a consequence.
- **Time travel becomes three legible jumps**: **+1일 / +1주 / +1달** replace "시간 밀기". Advancing time is
  presented the way the product presents universe time passing (the demo renders its own equivalent of the
  universe-time presentation — `UniverseTimeOverlay` itself stays on the never-mount list), so the visitor
  sees time arrive, not a date string change.
- **Every mechanic is experienceable, repeatedly, per star:**
  - **망각** — as time advances, unrecalled stars dim (`effectiveBrightness`) and their words erode by decay
    stage; **opening a launched star's entry shows the eroded text** (`currentDecayText`), so forgetting is
    readable, not just visible at a distance. The diary body itself stays verbatim ([I2]) — what erodes is
    the memory's current text, as in the product.
  - **회고** — recall is per-star and repeatable: each 회고하기 re-brightens that star, bumps its
    `recallCount`, reshapes its seed and restores its words — free, no cost surface ([Z2][Z8]).
  - **요지화** — each memory's gist timer runs on the demo clock; long-unrecalled memories climb the
    semantic stages and their gist bodies rise into the neocortex band as time is advanced — per memory, not
    on one scripted id.
  - **우주 색** — the sky keeps re-mirroring the strength-weighted emotions as the universe grows ([M5]).
- **The run state generalizes from one-shot flags to per-memory stored facts** (which diaries launched, each
  memory's recall history and timers, the clock) so the mechanics compose in any order in free play — the
  derived quantities keep coming from the shipped production functions only.
- The ornament taster and the signup CTA stay; the CTA remains the only exit, but no longer the only thing
  left to do.

### 3. The copy is re-voiced

All `demo_*` copy moves to the 토스-blog 해요체 register, using the product's real verbs (별 쪼개기 · 별
띄우기 · 회고하기): no "시간 밀기", no "일기 두 편 더", no terse imperative labels; captions stay literary
and [I12]-honest, beat 8 keeps the [M5] sentence.

## Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope** — the run FSM and the gating it derives (interactivity + dim/blur treatment), the free-play
loop (draw-write-split-launch, per-star recall, per-memory gist, the three time jumps and the time
presentation), the fixture-pool extension in `@cosimosi/demo` (plan 77's corpus + integrity tests), the
generalized demo-local run state, the re-scripted tutorial beats' anchors/signals where controls changed,
all `demo_*` copy in both locales, and `spec/values.yaml`'s `demo` group (below).

**Must not change:**

- **[Z1] / [I13] — the isolation closure.** Zero `apps/api` RPCs, DB writes and LLM calls; the demo-scoped
  import-ban block (transports, `*/react` entrypoints, `widgets/features/entities`, `@cosimosi/store`,
  restated renderer ban) stays; **no `isDemo` flag, prop or branch appears in `packages/*`, `features/*` or
  `entities/*`** — the FSM, the pool cursor and every timer live in `pages/demo` (and authored data in
  `@cosimosi/demo`).
- **[Z7] — nothing survives.** No `localStorage`, no URL param, no server write; `resetUniverseUserState()`
  on mount, full cleanup on unmount; signup still starts from an empty universe.
- **[Z8] — no money.** No currency, price, cost, quote, purchase, achievement or payment surface; recall and
  time travel stay free with no balance rendered.
- **[I10] — the real clock.** The demo clock stays a demo-local date passed down as `universeTime`; the real
  clock store and `syncUniverseClock` stay untouched.
- **The never-mount list** — `UniverseCanvasWidget`, `StardustOverlay`, `UniverseTimeOverlay`,
  `WritingFlowSheet`, `RecallFlowSheet`, `DeletionFlowSheet`, `DetailPanel` reads: the demo keeps rendering
  its own look-alikes, never the product widgets.
- **[Z5] determinism** — every drawn diary's split is precomputed and identical for the same diary; only the
  draw order varies.
- **Golden parity** — no formula is implemented locally; every derived quantity keeps coming from
  `@cosimosi/memory-logic` / `packages/emotion` / `packages/universe` / `packages/force-sim`.
- **[I2]/[I8]** — diary bodies render verbatim; a recall moves stored facts and the seed only.
- **[O4]** — skip is always visible and always works, including mid-tutorial.
- **The route** — `/demo` public, no `beforeLoad`, web-only waiver stands.

**Non-goals:** the landing walkthrough (change 09); the onboarding tour (plan 80 — the engine's other host,
untouched); the product writing/recall flows themselves; any new ornament kind; persistence of demo progress
of any sort.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. While the tutorial runs, only the current beat's anchored control is interactive; every other control is
   non-interactive **and** rendered dimmed + blurred; the skip affordance stays fully visible and interactive
   throughout ([O4]).
2. Completing the last beat or pressing skip moves the run to free play permanently: all controls become
   interactive and undimmed, and no tutorial chrome lingers.
3. The gating derives from one explicit finite-state machine that owns the run's phase (tutorial step ↔
   free play); a control's availability cannot be set anywhere else, and pressing a gated control does
   nothing — a state-machine test walks the script and asserts per-state availability.
4. In free play, a control with the product's writing verb draws a random prepared diary from the fixture
   pool and presents it through the same UI shapes as the product's writing flow: the entry appears, 별
   쪼개기 reveals its precomputed split, 별 띄우기 launches it — repeatable at least as many times as the
   pool is deep, with no real write and no LLM call ([Z1][Z4][Z5]).
5. The fixture pool is extended (authored, both locales, precomputed splits) and its integrity tests cover
   every added diary; the same diary always splits identically ([Z5]).
6. Time travel is three controls — +1일 · +1주 · +1달 — advancing the demo clock by one day, seven days and
   one month respectively (month length per `spec/values.yaml`), and the advance is presented like the
   product's universe-time passing, not as a bare date-string swap; `UniverseTimeOverlay` itself is still
   never mounted.
7. Forgetting is experienceable: as the clock advances, unrecalled stars dim by `effectiveBrightness` and
   their words erode by decay stage, and opening a launched star's entry shows the eroded current text while
   the diary body stays verbatim ([I2]).
8. Recall is per-star and repeatable: each 회고 re-brightens that star, increments its recall count,
   reshapes its seed and restores its words — with no cost, balance or spend surface anywhere ([Z2][Z8]).
9. Semanticization is experienceable per memory: memories left unrecalled while time advances climb the
   semantic stages and their gist bodies rise into the neocortex band; the sky keeps mirroring the
   strength-weighted emotions as the universe grows ([M5]).
10. The isolation boundary is unchanged: a full session issues zero RPCs, DB writes and LLM calls; the
    demo-scoped lint block still bans every transport path; no `isDemo` appears in shared code; leaving
    `/demo` clears everything and nothing persists ([Z1][Z7][I13]).
11. All `demo_*` copy is re-voiced in 해요체 with the product's verbs — "시간 밀기" and "일기 두 편 더" no
    longer exist in either catalogue — key sets match across `en.json`/`ko.json`, `pnpm gen:messages` output
    is committed, `lint:raw-strings` passes, and every sentence stays [I12]-honest with beat 8 carrying the
    [M5] definition.
12. `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm test:guards`, `pnpm lint:fsd:layout` and the web build
    pass; the demo adds no `steiger` exemption and no new shared-code seam.
