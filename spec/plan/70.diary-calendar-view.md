# 70. diary calendar view

> Diary calendar view — a month grid on the shipped `/diary` reader that marks every written day with **that day's**
> representative mood color and clicks through to the diary ([D12]). It is a **view toggle on the existing page, not a new
> route**; it consumes [68](68.diary-search-and-filter.md)'s `GetDiaryCalendar`† read and [17](17.emotion-model.md)/
> [51](51.palette-customization.md)'s `moodColor` palette seam, computes the per-day representative with the shipped
> `toEmotionSlices`, and stays free and time-frozen like the rest of the archive ([D11]). Ships web + mobile.
> Scope: FE | Status: planning
>
> Layer P. Home: `apps/{web,mobile}/src/widgets/diary-calendar/` (month grid, RN-forked `ui`) + the month/mark pure
> functions in `packages/universe/src/diary-calendar.ts`, mounted by the existing `pages/diary-reader` as a two-option
> view toggle. It fills the last [D6]–[D12] presentation seam and leaves every read, filter and cursor to [68], every
> list behavior to [69](69.diary-list-completion.md).

## Purpose

The archive is a list, and a list answers "what did I write?" one entry at a time. It cannot answer the question a
diarist actually asks of a year — _when was I writing, and how did those weeks feel?_ [D12] asks for exactly that: a
month calendar where each written day carries a mark in **that day's representative mood color**, and where clicking a
mark lands on that day's writing. It is the only surface in the product where the passage of _real_ calendar time is
legible at all; the universe deliberately shows universe time ([T3]) and the list shows a cursor.

The shipped reader ([47.diary-reader-page](47.diary-reader-page.md)) already owns the route, the free `GetDiaries`
archive read, the immutable body, the split chips and the one paid action. [69](69.diary-list-completion.md) completes
its list (title-less rows, sort toggle, infinite scroll, the filter/search UI) and [68](68.diary-search-and-filter.md)
owns the reads behind all of it, including the new `GetDiaryCalendar`†. What is missing is the second _shape_ of the same
archive. Making it a **route** would split the archive's identity in two and duplicate the filter state, the deep-link
consumer and the deletion mount; making it a **view toggle** keeps one page, one read model, one set of filters.

Two questions this unit must answer that the list never had to. First, the **per-DAY** representative mood is a distinct
question from the per-DIARY one — `diaries` has no `UNIQUE (user_id, diary_date)`, so one day may hold several diaries
and ~10 moods across them, and [D6]'s answer (render every memory's dot, no representative at all) does not scale to a
6×7 grid where each cell can hold exactly one color. Second, a day can be **written but hold no live mood** — a
past-dated diary that launched nothing ([T1]), or one whose memories were all let go ([X4]) — and asserting `NEUTRAL`'s
color there would claim an emotion the user never recorded ([M3]). Both are answered by
[68.diary-search-and-filter](68.diary-search-and-filter.md), which owns the read; this plan follows those answers and
adds no third rule.

## Scope / Non-goals

**In scope**

- **`widgets/diary-calendar`** on both apps ([D12]) — a month grid of the current user's written days, each written day
  marked with its representative mood color, each mark clickable. Read-only; no action slot.
- **The view toggle in `widgets/diary-reader`** ([D12][D6]) — one two-option control (목록 / 달력) in
  `DiaryReaderBlock`'s existing header (as-built, the header lives in the widget, not the page); the widget composes
  either the list ([69]) or the calendar under the **same** header, restore section, deletion mount and
  deep-link consumer. **No new route, no new screen** on either app.
- **The per-day representative mood projection** ([D12][M2][M3][M4]) — pure functions in `packages/universe`:
  the month grid (weeks × days over plain calendar dates) and `dayRepresentativeMood`, which is the **top slice of
  `toEmotionSlices`** over that day's `(mood, weight)` rows, inheriting its `weight desc,
mood.localeCompare` tie-break and its drop-≤0 normalization.
- **The neutral outline mark** for a written day with no live mood — a border-token outline, never
  `NEUTRAL`'s hue ([M3]).
- **Click-through** ([D12]) — selecting a marked day applies `{from: date, to: date}` to [69]'s date-range filter
  state (the wire contract is [68]'s `GetDiaries` `from`/`to`) and
  switches the view back to the list, so a day holding N diaries shows all N.
- **URL-backed view + month on web** (`view`, `month`) added to the `/diary` `validateSearch` schema [69] owns;
  mobile keeps both in the screen's local state.
- **Copy via i18n** under the `calendar_*` prefix, mood names through `packages/emotion`'s `moodLabel` — no
  `mood_*` keys, no raw strings.
- **Free and time-frozen** ([D11][T3][G4]) — entering the calendar, stepping months and selecting a day spend no
  Twinkle and advance no clock.
- **Web + mobile parity** ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4's matrix grants this unit no waiver) — same widget
  slice and same pure package module; only the grid `ui` forks, each app
  hand-writing its own `DiaryCalendar.tsx` (no app slice carries a `*.native.tsx` sibling — the as-built convention).

**Non-goals**

- **The `GetDiaryCalendar`† RPC, its sqlc query, its migration, its cursor and its `DiaryDayMood` noun** — all
  [68.diary-search-and-filter](68.diary-search-and-filter.md). This unit consumes the read and states the row-set
  requirement the neutral-outline rule places on it; it writes no proto, no SQL, no UL row.
- **The list, its rows, sort toggle, infinite scroll, search/filter UI, and the free-vs-paid visual boundary** —
  [69.diary-list-completion](69.diary-list-completion.md). The calendar _drives_ [69]'s date filter; it does not own it.
- **The `/diary` route, the page shell, the deletion mount, the star-detail deep link, and the paid jump** —
  [47.diary-reader-page](47.diary-reader-page.md). This unit adds a view to that page and touches none of them.
- **The mood→color table, the palette registry, palette switching** — [17.emotion-model](17.emotion-model.md) /
  [51.palette-customization](51.palette-customization.md) / (v2) [72.decorate-usecase](72.decorate-usecase.md). The
  calendar calls `moodColor` and holds no color of its own.
- **`EffectiveStrength` / `EffectiveBrightness` math** — [37.forgetting-decay](37.forgetting-decay.md) /
  [32.reconsolidation-rules](32.reconsolidation-rules.md), mirrored in `packages/memory-logic`. The calendar consumes the
  weight; it computes no strength.
- **A `Calendar`/`DatePicker` `packages/ui` primitive** — the month grid is widget-local layout. The
  view toggle reuses the `SegmentedControl` primitive pair [69] promotes into `packages/ui` (the reserved
  design-system name; no new primitive here — AC 17 holds).
- **A year/heat-map view, streak counts, or any per-day statistic.** [A1a] forbids real-time streaks and [D2] keeps the
  archive supporting; a written-day mark is the whole ask of [D12].

## Design

### Placement (FSD §3.1 / §3.5)

```
packages/universe/src/diary-calendar.ts        pure: month grid + per-day representative mood (+ .test.ts)
packages/universe/src/react/use-diary-calendar.ts   the GetDiaryCalendar month query hook — beside
                                               use-diary-archive.ts, its shipped precedent (an app-local model/
                                               copy in both apps would be byte-identical and fail R4)
packages/universe/src/{index.ts,react.ts}      re-exports (the package's two entry points — "." and "./react")

apps/{web,mobile}/src/widgets/diary-calendar/
├── ui/DiaryCalendar.tsx                       the month grid — the only fork: web CSS grid,
│                                              mobile's own RN file (View rows), no *.native.tsx sibling
└── index.ts                                   public API: { DiaryCalendar }

apps/{web,mobile}/src/widgets/diary-reader/ui/DiaryReaderBlock.tsx   + the view toggle and view branch (the header,
                                               restore section and deep-link consumer live HERE, not on the page)
apps/web/src/app/routes/route-tree.tsx                            + `view` / `month` in /diary validateSearch
apps/web/steiger.config.js                                        + scoped 'fsd/insignificant-slice': 'off'
```

- The **pure** month/mark logic lives in `packages/universe`, not in an app-local `lib/` segment. Reason (not taste):
  both apps need it byte-identically, and `scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` R4 **fails** a byte-identical pure module
  present under both apps' `lib`/`model` paths until it is promoted to its owning package. `packages/universe` already
  owns the reader's shared read model (`diary-store.ts`, `use-diary-archive.ts`, `diary-reader.machine.ts`), so the
  calendar's projection belongs beside them.
- The **widget** is mounted by the page, not by the list feature: the page owns the view mode, and a widget that a page
  swaps in is exactly §3.1's "a big chunk a page drops in". The calendar imports `entities`/`packages` only — never
  `features/read-diary-list` (same-layer-adjacent coupling) and never the router (`pages`/`widgets` may not import
  `app/routes`; the shipped reader's `onExit` callback seam is the precedent).
- **No new `packages/ui` primitive.** The grid is widget-local layout; the toggle reuses [69]'s pair.

### The read it consumes (owned by [68])

`memory.v1.MemoryService.GetDiaryCalendar`† — unary, `NO_SIDE_EFFECTS`, per-user-scoped (no `user_id` on the wire),
keyset-cursor paginated, classified **exactly once** in
`packages/client-cache/src/http-policy.ts` with `userScopedUnaryReadPolicy`. This unit adds **no second** classification
entry — a duplicate hard-fails the production transport before I/O (§2.7), which is the guard.

The wire shape is [68](68.diary-search-and-filter.md)'s, one message per written day with its mood inputs nested:

```proto
message GetDiaryCalendarResponse { repeated DiaryDayDto days = 1; string next_page_token = 2; }
message DiaryDayDto     { string diary_date = 1; repeated DiaryDayMoodDto moods = 2; }
message DiaryDayMoodDto { string mood = 1; float weight = 2; }
```

The client mapper flattens it into the triple `DiaryDayMood { diaryDate, mood, weight }` — the FE mirror of
[68]'s Go domain type — which is what the pure functions below take.

Two consumption rules follow, and they are requirements on [68]'s query rather than new decisions:

1. **Every written day in the requested range yields a `DiaryDayDto`.** A day whose diaries launched nothing ([T1]) or
   whose memories were all let go ([X4]) arrives with an **empty `moods` list** — otherwise the neutral
   outline is unsatisfiable, because the day would be indistinguishable from a day never written. The client treats an
   empty list, an empty `mood` or an unrecognized `mood` as "written, no live mood" and coerces it to the outline
   rather than to a color (mirroring `resolvePaletteById`'s unknown→default discipline).
2. **`weight` is consumed, never recomputed.** The weight is `EffectiveStrength`-derived ([M4]/[V3]); the
   calendar sums nothing and applies no decay. It normalizes only through `toEmotionSlices`.

The response carries **no diary id, no body, no count** — so the calendar cannot deep-link to a specific entry and
cannot render text. That absence is what makes click-through a _filter_, below.

### The per-day representative mood and the mark

`packages/universe/src/diary-calendar.ts` exports three pure functions and one type:

| Export                             | Shape                                                             | Rule                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `type DayMark`                     | `{ mood: Mood \| null }`                                          | `null` = written, no live mood → outline. There is no `color` field: color is resolved at the render edge only (§3.4)             |
| `dayRepresentativeMood(rows)`      | `readonly DiaryDayMood[] → Mood \| null`                          | `toEmotionSlices(Map<mood, weight>)[0]?.mood ?? null` — the **top slice**, inheriting its tie-break and drop-≤0 normalization     |
| `monthMarks(rows)`                 | `readonly DiaryDayMood[] → ReadonlyMap<string /*date*/, DayMark>` | one entry per **written** day; unwritten days are absent from the map, never present-with-null                                    |
| `buildMonthGrid(month, weekStart)` | `('YYYY-MM', 0..6) → readonly (readonly MonthCell[])[]`           | 4–6 weeks × 7 cells; each cell = `{ date: 'YYYY-MM-DD', inMonth: boolean }`. Leading/trailing cells come from the adjacent months |

- **Dates never become `Date` objects.** The grid is computed from integer `(year, month, day)` arithmetic and emits
  `YYYY-MM-DD` strings; marks are keyed and looked up as strings. `diary_date` is a user-entered **local** calendar date
  ([W5]) with no time component, so parsing it through `new Date(iso)` would apply a UTC shift and could move a mark a
  day for any user west of UTC. This is a correctness rule, and it is also why the calendar needs **no** `User.Timezone`
  dependency ([60](60.account-profile-model.md) is not a prerequisite).
- **Zero color in the pure layer.** `DayMark` carries a `Mood`; `DiaryCalendar` calls `moodColor(mood)` at render time.
  A palette swap therefore recolors the calendar with no code change ([M6]) and the pure module stays palette-agnostic.
- The mark is a filled disc for a mood day and a 1px outline disc for a mood-less day; both sit under the day number so
  the grid stays legible at mobile widths. Nothing animates — there is no reduced-motion contract to honor here.

### The view toggle, the month, and the URL

- **Two views, one widget.** As-built, `DiaryReaderPage` renders only `<DiaryReaderBlock/>` + `<DeletionFlowSheet/>`;
  the header (title, back), the restore section and the star-detail **deep-link consumer** (which actively drives
  `loadMore()` to find its target) all live in `widgets/diary-reader/ui/DiaryReaderBlock.tsx`. So the view toggle and
  the list↔calendar branch land **in the widget**: `DiaryReaderBlock` keeps its header + restore section mounted and
  swaps **either** [69]'s list **or** `<DiaryCalendar/>` beneath them; the deep-link consumer and the page-level
  deletion mount stay unconditional, so entering the calendar never drops them.
- **Web: URL-backed.** `view: 'list' | 'calendar'` (default `list`) and `month: 'YYYY-MM'` (optional) join the `/diary`
  `validateSearch` schema that **[69] owns** (69's `sort` field created it; 68 is BE-only and touches no route file);
  `route-screens.tsx` injects them as props, so the page never imports the
  router. **[69] owns the schema; this unit adds exactly these two keys** — the same single-owner discipline
  [00.overview](00.overview.md) §5.6 applies to migrations. **Mobile: screen-local state.**
- **No XState machine.** §3.2 puts exclusive _in-page phases_ in a machine; the view mode's authority on web is the URL,
  and a machine would be a second source of truth for one two-valued routed field that would then need syncing back. The
  displayed month is a **query input**, i.e. data (§3.2), not control state. The reader's existing
  `diary-reader.machine.ts` (the paid-jump phases) is untouched.
- **The initial month is resolved, not stored.** With no `month` param: the month of the **newest diary already in the
  archive cache** (the list is the default view, so `useDiaryArchive`'s first page is always fetched first), else the
  current month from the device-local date. The resolved value is **not** written back into the URL, so mounting the
  calendar adds no history entry; stepping does.
- **Stepping is prev/next only** — one month at a time, unbounded, with the month label between them. There is no
  "today" button and no month picker: the URL `month` param plus browser back already return the user, and the archive's
  voice is restrained (a permanently visible extra control earns its place or it does not ship). A month with no entries
  renders an empty grid with a quiet line rather than an error.

### Click-through ([D12]) — a filter, not a navigation

Selecting a marked day calls the widget's only outward prop, `onSelectDay(date)`. The page then sets [68]'s date-range
filter to `{from: date, to: date}` **and** switches `view` to `list`. A day holding several diaries therefore lands on
all of them, in [69]'s rows, with the existing free-read affordances. The calendar itself performs no navigation, opens
no entry, and cannot: its DTO carries no diary id. Consequences, stated:

- **No auto-expand of a sole entry.** Row open/close is [69]'s behavior; the calendar stops at "the list, filtered to
  that day". One rule for one and for many is simpler than two.
- An unwritten cell is inert — not a button, no hover affordance, no `aria` role — so the grid announces only real days.

### Freshness (§3.2 — invalidation, never polling)

The month query's key includes the month, so stepping is a new key and a natural fetch. Beyond that:

- The month query declares `staleTime = diary_reader.calendar_stale_ms`, so **entering the calendar refetches** rather
  than painting a stale month from the shared `clientCache.default_stale_ms` window.
- This unit widens the three existing archive invalidation hooks in `packages/universe/src/react/` to include the
  calendar key: `use-release.ts`, `use-restore.ts` (both already invalidate the `GetDiaries` keys) and **`use-let-go.ts`,
  which invalidates only `GetUniverse` today** — letting go removes a memory and therefore can remove a day's last live
  mood, flipping the mark to an outline. A new `useInvalidateDiaryCalendar()` beside `use-invalidate-universe.ts` keeps
  the key in one place.
- A `Recall` can shift _which_ mood is a day's top slice (it raises `EffectiveStrength`), but it can never add or remove
  a mark. That shift is left to the entry refetch above — decided, not overlooked: wiring the calendar key into every
  recall site would couple a toggled view to the paid-action paths for a color nudge the user is not looking at.
- **A month renders only when fully loaded.** With per-`(day, mood)` rows a month can exceed one page; the hook pages
  until `next_page_token` is empty before handing marks to the grid, because a half-paged month would compute a _wrong_
  representative (a missing high-weight mood silently promotes the runner-up). `diary_reader.calendar_month_page_size`
  is sized so a typical month resolves in one round trip.

### Copy (i18n) — literary and restrained

All strings resolve through the seam under the `calendar_*` prefix, present in **both** `en.json` and `ko.json`; mood
names come from `packages/emotion`'s `moodLabel` (no `mood_*` keys). Keys: `calendar_view_action` · `calendar_list_view_action`
· `calendar_month_label` (`{year}`/`{month}`) · `calendar_prev_month_action` · `calendar_next_month_action` ·
`calendar_weekday_sun`…`calendar_weekday_sat` · `calendar_day_mood_hint` (`{date}`/`{mood}`, the cell's accessible name)
· `calendar_day_unmarked_hint` · `calendar_loading` · `calendar_error` · `calendar_empty_month`. The empty month reads
**"이 달엔 아무것도 적지 않았어요."** (해요체 — the shipped reader voice, e.g. `diary_reader_empty`) and a mood-less
written day **"색이 남지 않은 날"** — plain, unhurried, and honest
about absence rather than filling it in. `pnpm lint:raw-strings` covers every new `aria-*`/`accessibilityLabel`.

### Golden-parity stance

**No pure function here is golden-parity with Go, and none should be.** `buildMonthGrid`, `monthMarks` and
`dayRepresentativeMood` are presentation projections with no server counterpart — the server never renders a calendar,
and `toEmotionSlices`, which they build on, is already presentation-only and unmirrored by rule ([M6]: color is a
frontend concern). The one number that _is_ parity-pinned, `EffectiveStrength`, is **consumed as `weight` from the wire**
and never recomputed here, so this unit adds no drift surface and no fixture. Adding a Go twin for the grid would create
a second implementation of calendar arithmetic to keep in step for zero benefit (ARCHITECTURE §2.9 #2).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `/diary` (web) and the diary-reader screen (mobile) offer a **two-option view toggle** (목록 / 달력) that swaps the
   list for a month calendar **within the same page** — no new route, no new screen, and the deletion mount plus the
   star-detail deep-link consumer stay mounted across the switch ([D12]).
2. The calendar renders a **month grid** of 4–6 weeks × 7 days for the displayed month, with leading/trailing cells from
   the adjacent months, and marks **every day the user wrote on** ([D12]).
3. A marked day whose diaries hold at least one live `EpisodicMemory` is marked with **that day's representative mood
   color**, computed as the **top slice of `toEmotionSlices`** over the day's `(mood, weight)` rows — inheriting its
   `weight desc, mood.localeCompare` tie-break and drop-≤0 normalization ([D12][M4]).
4. A day that was **written but holds no live mood** (launched nothing, or all memories let go) is marked with a
   **neutral outline**, never `NEUTRAL`'s color; it is still distinguishable from an unwritten day ([M3][I1][X4]).
5. **No color crosses the wire.** The consumed DTO carries `mood` names and weights only; every hue in the calendar comes
   from `moodColor`, and swapping the palette recolors the calendar with no code change ([M3][M6][I3]).
6. The `weight` is **consumed, never recomputed**: the calendar applies no `EffectiveStrength`, no decay and no
   aggregation beyond `toEmotionSlices`' normalization ([M4][V3]).
7. **Selecting a marked day** applies the date-range filter `{from: date, to: date}` and switches to the list view, so a
   day holding several diaries shows all of them ([D12][D8]); an unwritten cell is inert and exposes no control.
8. Entering the calendar, stepping months and selecting a day **spend no Twinkle and advance no universe time**; the
   consumed read is `NO_SIDE_EFFECTS`, carries no `SpendGate` and no clock port, and the calendar exposes **no
   action-injection prop** through which a paid affordance could be mounted ([D11][T3][G4]).
9. The calendar renders **no diary text of any kind** — no body, no preview, no `CurrentText`, no snippet — and its
   request carries no search `query` field ([D10][D4][I2]).
10. A **released** diary's day loses its mark and a **restored** one regains it, via [68]'s `release_groups` anti-join;
    no `diaries.deleted_at` column is introduced or read ([X2][I1]).
11. **Dates never pass through a `Date` UTC conversion**: the grid and the mark lookup operate on `YYYY-MM-DD` strings
    and integer `(y, m, d)` arithmetic, so no mark shifts a day for a user in a negative UTC offset ([W5]).
12. A month is rendered **only when fully paged** (`next_page_token` empty); a partially loaded month shows the loading
    state rather than a representative computed from an incomplete row set.
13. **On web the view and month are URL-backed** (`view`, `month` on the `/diary` `validateSearch` schema, injected as
    props — the page and widget never import the router); **on mobile both are screen-local state**. Mounting the
    calendar adds no history entry; stepping does.
14. The calendar declares **no XState machine**; the month is a query input and the view mode is routed/local state
    (ARCHITECTURE §3.2), and the reader's existing paid-jump machine is unchanged.
15. Letting go, releasing and restoring **invalidate the calendar key** (`use-let-go.ts`, `use-release.ts`,
    `use-restore.ts`); there is **no polling** and no interval refetch anywhere in this unit (§3.2).
16. **All** user-visible copy resolves through the i18n seam under `calendar_*`, present in both locales, with mood names
    from `moodLabel` and no `mood_*` keys; `pnpm lint:raw-strings` passes.
17. **Web + mobile parity**: one `widgets/diary-calendar` slice name and one shared
    `packages/universe/src/diary-calendar.ts` on both apps; the **only** fork is the grid `ui`, each app owning its own
    `DiaryCalendar.tsx` with no app-slice `*.native.tsx` sibling.
    No new `packages/ui` primitive is added; `pnpm lint:fsd:layout` and steiger pass with the scoped
    `'fsd/insignificant-slice'` block registered.
18. Every tuning number this unit reads comes from generated config (`@cosimosi/config`); no page size, staleness,
    prefetch count or week-start value is hardcoded in FE code.

## Policy / Values Impact

- **policy / ux:** the rules below **will be true once implemented** and are appended as a 달력 뷰 section to the
  existing [policy/ux/diary-reader.md](../policy/ux/diary-reader.md) — the reader's UX policy, extended rather than
  duplicated. Written at `/implement-job`; **do not pre-create or edit any policy file.**
  - **The archive has two shapes and one identity.** The calendar is a view of `/diary`, not a destination; the list is
    the default, the toggle is the only way in, and every other reader affordance survives the switch.
  - **A day's color is the top slice of that day's strength-weighted emotions** — the same blend the universe paints
    ([M4]), collapsed to one mood. A day may hold several diaries and many moods; the mark asserts the loudest, never an
    average, and never a per-diary representative (which [D6] deliberately does not have either).
  - **A written day always shows.** With no live mood it shows as an outline, because the writing happened even when the
    remembering did not survive; `NEUTRAL`'s color is never used to stand in for absence.
  - **The calendar is free and time-frozen.** Entering it, stepping months and selecting a day cost nothing and move no
    clock; the calendar holds no paid affordance at all, so the free/paid boundary needs no visual warning here.
  - **Clicking a day lands on that day's writing, filtered** — never on a guessed single entry.
