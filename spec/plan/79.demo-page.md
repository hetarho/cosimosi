# 79. demo page

> Demo page — the public, non-auth `/demo` route that runs the fixed ten beats of [Z3] as a **step-gated tutorial**
> over the **real** render layers, then stays open as a **free-play room**: a diary splits, stars launch, a shared
> neuron pulls a constellation together, time is shoved forward until the stars dim and lose words, a recall reshapes
> one, more time lifts gists into the neocortex band, the sky takes the universe's emotion, a few ornaments are tried
> on, and the visitor is invited to sign up — or keeps writing, launching, traveling and recalling for as long as they
> like. It is a frontend-only sandbox: no auth, no RPC, no DB write, no LLM ([Z1]); free time travel and no spend gate
> at all ([Z2]); no price, purchase, achievement or payment surface anywhere ([Z8]); nothing survives leaving the page
> ([Z7]). It leaves the step engine to a sibling and the fixtures to another. Scope: FE | Status: built
> ([job 118](../jobs/archive/118.demo-page.md); free play + gating: change 10, job 128)
>
> Layer P. Home: `apps/web/src/pages/demo/{index.ts, ui/, model/}` on a public route under `rootRoute`.
> It fills the last seam of Epic P — the surface that binds
> [77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md)'s data to [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md)'s
> steps — and deliberately opens **no** seam into the real code path.

## Purpose

The demo is not a product, it is a trailer ([PRD.md](../PRD.md) §5.12 preamble). A visitor arriving from the landing
page ([81.landing-page](81.landing-page.md), [K3]) has to decide "is this worth an account?" in tens of seconds. What
makes cosimosi worth an account is precisely what a first session cannot show: a star only dims after days of not being
remembered, a gist only rises after the timer runs, the sky only takes on a color once enough memories accumulate. So
the demo's whole reason to exist is compressing days into seconds — which is why [Z2] hands it free time travel and
lifts every cost, and why [I13] exempts it from the invariants outright.

That exemption is the engineering problem. An exempt sandbox is safe only while it is **unreachable from the real code
path**: one `if (isDemo)` inside `packages/memory-logic`, or one demo-shaped bypass on the universe clock, and [I10]
stops holding for real accounts too — which is exactly the reason [I13] gives for demanding isolation. This unit is
therefore two things at once: a presentation surface, and the boundary that makes the exemption harmless. This plan
owns where the demo lives and what it may import, makes that boundary mechanical rather than a review habit, and
names the one mechanism that closes it completely.

The third job is honesty. `/demo` is a public surface, so [I12] governs every sentence on it: "inspired by engram
theory", never "works like a brain"; the 3D positions are an emergent visualization, never the brain's coordinates; no
therapeutic claim. And because a free trial that smells of money is worse than no trial, [Z8] keeps every price,
purchase, achievement and payment affordance off the page — the ornament taster ([Z3]-9) shows _what it looks like_,
never _what it costs_. Finally [Z7]: nothing made here carries into an account, so [U2]'s "your first universe is
empty" survives the funnel.

## Grounding

- Constitution ([I1]–[I13]): [00.overview](00.overview.md) §3 (_The constitution_), owned by [PRD.md](../PRD.md) §7.

  _is_ the invariant work**, and [I12] applies in full because `/demo` is public. Structural guards
  ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §3, the v2 risk register's Epic-P rows):
  - **[I13] — no demo bypass in real code.** Two guards, both structural. (a) **Transport starvation:** every RPC in
    this codebase is issued through a function whose first parameter is an `ApiTransport`
    (`requestLaunchStars`, `requestRecallDiaryStars`, `requestViewSemantic`, `requestSyncStatus`, `writeMoodColor`, …)
    or through a hook that calls `useTransport()` from `@connectrpc/connect-query`. A demo-scoped
    `no-restricted-imports` block that bans `@connectrpc/*`, `@cosimosi/api-client`, `@cosimosi/client-cache` and the
    `*/react` hook entrypoints leaves `pages/demo` with **no way to obtain a transport**, so no server call is
    expressible regardless of which barrel export drifts in. (b) **No flag:** unlimited stardust and free time travel
    live only in `pages/demo/model`; no `isDemo` prop, flag or branch exists in `packages/*`, `features/*` or
    `entities/*` — the shared code does not know the demo exists.

    `CanLaunchAt` are **not** mirrored and the demo never writes `useUniverseClockStore` or calls
    `syncUniverseClock`, so the real clock gains no reversible path.

    nothing is persisted.
  - **[I4]/[I6] no memory↔memory edge.** The fixture's synapses are canonically ordered neuron pairs and

  - **[I2] the `Diary` is immutable.** Beat 6 changes `currentText` and the seed; the fixture's diary body is rendered

  - **[I11]/[V10] decoration cannot touch meaning.** The taster drives only the sky effect, the star-variant key and

  - **[I12] honest public copy.** Every string is an i18n message reviewed as a deliverable of this plan.

├── ui/DemoWritingSheet.tsx      the write flow in the product's dialog shapes, read-only (see below)
├── ui/DemoDecorationSheet.tsx   beat 9 — the product decoration panel's shapes, with no price anywhere
├── ui/DemoStarPanel.tsx         a picked star: the product star-detail panel's shapes, read-only
├── ui/DemoRecallSheet.tsx       회고하기 in the product recall flow's shapes, with no price or consent
├── ui/DemoEntryReader.tsx       one opened diary, verbatim, in the archive entry's shapes
├── ui/DemoTimePassing.tsx       the universe-time look-alike: sweep driver + the [T6] clock
├── ui/DemoTutorialMask.tsx      ONE covering layer, a hole cut at the current beat's controls
├── model/anchors.ts             DemoAnchor · DemoSignal — the two host-owned unions [78] is generic over
├── model/run-machine.ts         the run FSM: tutorial(beat) ↔ freePlay, and the one gating derivation
├── model/script.ts              the ten beats as a SequenceScript: caption accessor + anchor + signal
└── model/use-demo-run.ts        the whole sandbox in one piece of page state (facts, clock, taste)

**As built, the sandbox is ONE piece of page state, generalized to per-memory stored facts**
(change 10). `useDemoRun` holds the drawn set, the resolved fixture, the write flow's draft, the
draw cursor, the growing launched list, each launched memory's facts — recall count, last-recall
anchor, seed, current words, gist-timer anchor with its [C7] floor — the clock and the taste; and
`projectScene` derives the scene the layers receive from exactly those facts (gist stages via the
production `semanticize` ∘ `gistUnitsElapsed` over the demo clock — per memory, not per scripted
id). That shape is what makes the [I13] half of this plan structural rather than reviewed: free
time travel, no cost gate and mechanics that compose in any order are expressible because they are
page state, and there is no shared package, feature or entity with a demo flag or a bypass to
offer.

**The run's phase is one explicit FSM** (`model/run-machine.ts`, on plan 07's XState 5 foundation,
beside plan 78's step engine — not inside it): states `tutorial(beat id)` → `freePlay`, entered
permanently on last-beat completion or skip. It exposes the single derivation every control's
interactivity comes from — during a tutorial step only that beat's controls are pressable (the
neuron-reuse beat opens the whole write flow; per-memory controls narrow to the beat's own star);
in free play everything is open. The visual half is ONE page-level
mask (`ui/DemoTutorialMask.tsx`): four heavily dimmed, lightly blurred strips covering everything
except the union box of the controls the current beat opens (`tutorialInteractiveAnchors`, measured
page-side through the engine's own anchor registry) — the HUD itself stays plain and only
disables; the mask paints below the sequence chrome and disappears for anchor-less beats (the sky
beat plays unmasked) and in free play. **The mask also LIFTS (fades to transparent, without
unmounting) for the moments whose payoff plays on the covered canvas** — a launch's awaken, every
time sweep, and a completed recall's reshaped star — returning `demo.mask_lift_linger_ms` after the
presentation ends; pressability
never rides the lift, and the next beat's caption and ring are **held back through it** (the page
hands the guide `null` while lifted), so the scene speaks first and the guidance arrives with the
covering's return. The caption's placement is the guide's own breakpoint rule (plan 78): on a wide
screen the line always takes the **bottom band**, because the surfaces that interrupt there are
centred modals; on a phone the page states the style per surface — `'center'` floats it just above
the middle with nothing open, a step staged inside an open surface switches to `'attached'` (the line
glues just below the highlighted control, or just above it near the bottom edge), and a surface the
beat merely stands in (the decoration sheet, the recall walk, a diary being read) pins `'top'`. The
neuron-reuse and decorating beats' rings
**walk with the visitor** — once the drawn diary is on screen the page overrides the guide's rect
to 쪼개기, then 띄우기; once the catalog is open it overrides to a catalog row, then stands the ring
down for the trip back out — because those beats' work is the whole round trip, not the
already-pressed button that opened it. **The camera is free play's**: `CameraControls` stays unmounted while the tour runs, so a
drag cannot point the ring and the hole at a scene the caption is not describing. **The signup CTA sits outside
the tour**: top-right under the skip's corner, never highlighted, never gated, no `DemoAnchor`
member — the closing beat anchors nothing, its caption names the corner over an already-open room
(`TUTORIAL_INTERACTIVE.signup_cta = null`), and the dwell then only retires the chrome. A
**start-over control** sits beside the CTA, equally un-anchored: it re-keys the run component, so
"reset" and "arrive" are literally one code path (fresh draw, tour from beat 1, module stores
cleared by the same unmount cleanup). Skip is
above the gate and never gated ([O4]). `syncDemoRunMachine` is the one seam that walks the phase
machine in step with the engine's advances, so the two machines cannot disagree — and the page
re-measures the highlight whenever the phase, the draft or the sheet moves the layout, because the
engine's only native re-measure signal is an anchor mounting or unmounting.

**The write flow renders in the product's own shapes** (`ui/DemoWritingSheet.tsx`): the same
`Dialog`, the same field labels (`writing_flow_*` keys), the same 별 쪼개기 → proposal rows (name,
mood chip, source passage, neuron chips) → 별 띄우기 walk. It is a deliberate **read-only twin** of
`widgets/writing-flow`, not a reuse: the shipped widget is fused to the transport (`useTransport`,
the split/revise/launch RPCs, the GetUniverse cache) which the isolation closure bans, and an
`isDemo` prop on the shipped widget is the flag [I13] forbids — so the twin drops exactly the
writable surface (fields read-only, no revise, no error arm) and keeps the shapes — the same
centered `Dialog` included. A launch closes the dialog before the memory goes up, so the birth
always plays on an unobstructed sky. Its dismiss is gated on "could the write control reopen it",
so a tutorial beat staged inside the sheet cannot be stranded by its close button.

**The HUD is the home screen's arrangement** (`ui/DemoHud.tsx`), for the same reason the write flow
wears the product's dialog: a trailer that teaches a screen the product does not ship teaches the
wrong screen. `UniverseHomePage`'s layout, twinned — the clock centred over the sky as bare type out
of flow ([T6], `sm` and up; on a phone it keeps left, because the chrome's always-visible skip owns
the top edge's right half), a borderless `drop-shadow-md` icon column against the right edge for the
ways out of the canvas, and 일기 쓰기 alone at the bottom as one outlined `lg` control. Three
divergences, each because the sandbox cannot honestly carry the product's thing: **no balance**
([Z8]), **no archive icon** (there is no archive route — a star's diary opens from the star), and the
**time controls**, which no signed-in screen has, sit under the clock they move as quiet text
buttons. The right column is held clear of the top edge so the skip never overlaps it.

**A picked star opens the product's reading surfaces**, all read-only twins for the same reason the
write flow is: `ui/DemoStarPanel.tsx` is `widgets/star-detail`'s `Dialog` — the star rendered in its
own framed sky (`StarPreview` at the demo clock, wearing the tasted shape), the meta rows read from
the shipped read-time functions (`effectiveStrength` · `effectiveBrightness` · `effectiveElapsedDays`
· `currentDecayStage`), and the current words through `currentDecaySpans` so the forgetting is
readable rather than only visible at a distance. The shipped panel's five actions become two,
labelled and in the open — 회고하기, and 원본 일기 보기 beside it — because a menu holding two things
is a menu for its own sake. From there `ui/DemoRecallSheet.tsx` walks `widgets/recall-flow`'s steps
(the faded words, the sentence that goes back, what came back) with the paid half absent: no quote,
no consent modal, no in-flight fence, nothing that can fail. Its rewrite field is **read-only and
prefilled** with the fixture's reconsolidated reading ([Z5]) — a memory the set authored none for
sends its current words back and comes back reinforced — because a box whose contents were silently
discarded is the one dishonest shape a read-only twin can take. `ui/DemoEntryReader.tsx` is the
archive's opened entry (`features/read-diary-list`'s `DiaryEntry`): the body verbatim and the
memories it split into as mood chips, in a `Dialog` because the sandbox has no archive route to
navigate to. Each of the three opens by closing the one it came from, so the canvas is never under
two scrims at once — the product's own rule where its deletion flow clears the selection first.
The same DOM renders every width, so no anchor id ever registers twice.

**One rule the fourth beat turned out to need.** An edge is drawn only once its two neurons have
**co-fired in a memory that has actually launched** — the server's own formation rule, applied to the
launched subset. Filtering on "both endpoints exist" instead was wrong and also flattened the beat: a
single diary can easily touch every neuron in a set, so every authored edge would already be drawn
before the second and third diaries arrived, and the beat that the whole fixture design exists to serve
would have had nothing to add. It is asserted per set, because the visitor meets each one a third of
the time.

There is **no `apps/demo`**: a second app would duplicate the `@cosimosi/3d-renderer` host wiring, the Vite/worker/build
config and a second root the FSD and language gates must be taught, for a page whose value is the real renderer.
`route-tree.tsx` gains a `demoRoute` under `rootRoute` at `/demo` with no `beforeLoad`; `route-screens.tsx` gains a
`DemoRoute` wrapper supplying `onSignUp` — the page never imports the router, matching the shipped
`UniverseRoute`/`LoginRoute` seam. Deployment needs **no change**: the Worker's
`assets.not_found_handling: "single-page-application"` already serves any SPA deep link, on the live origin recorded in
[DEPLOY.md](../../DEPLOY.md) §1.

`apps/web/steiger.config.js` needs **no** new exemption block — every shipped block targets `entities|features|widgets`
slices and no `pages/*` slice carries one (plan 81 states the same rule; a page slice is not "insignificant").
**Mobile parity is explicitly waived** — the demo is a Visitor surface reached from the landing page, and
`00.overview` §0 gives the Visitor web-funnel behaviors only ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4). The waiver follows
the admin-console precedent (`spec/policy/ops/admin.md` §6: "the web↔mobile parity rule is deliberately waived for it"), stated here because
`scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` does not enforce web↔mobile page peering — an unstated waiver is silently broken.

### The isolation boundary ([I13]) — the enforcement mechanism

an allowlist**:

Because **every** RPC-issuing function in `packages/*` takes an `ApiTransport` as its first argument, and every hook
that hides one calls `useTransport()`, a `pages/demo` starved of both cannot issue a call even if a server-calling
barrel export is imported by accident. That is why the rule needs no maintained allowlist of forbidden symbols.

Two mechanical notes, so the rule actually lands: ESLint flat config **replaces** rule options per matching file, so the
demo block must **restate** the existing `three` / `@react-three/fiber` ban (`src/pages/**` already matches it) or the
renderer ban would be lost for demo files. And the corresponding positive rule — **no `isDemo` flag, prop or branch in
`packages/*`, `features/*`, `entities/*`** — is discharged by this plan adding none, and by
[77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md) defining no pure function locally; `lint:fsd:layout`
R4 catches a copy-pasted mirror.

not product calls and are out of scope — they run above every route, including this one.

### The demo clock and "unlimited stardust" ([Z2])

The clock is a mutable ISO date in `pages/demo/model` with **three controls** — +1일 · +1주 · +1달 — advancing one
day, seven days, and `demo.time_travel_month_days` respectively (day and week are fixed calendar units, not tuning
numbers; the month is the one tuned scalar). There is no monotonicity check on the jumps, no launch precondition, no
sync-consent modal and no diary-date constraint — [Z2] exempts all of it, and none of that exemption is expressible
in shared code because the demo simply passes its date down as the `universeTime` prop the layers already accept.
(A launch does land the clock on the diary's date and never rewinds it — not as a guard but because that is the
server's own launch behaviour, mirrored so free play reads true.)

**The advance is presented like the product's passing time, demo-locally.** `UniverseTimeOverlay` stays on the
never-mount list (it is wired to the advance-announcement and consent stores), so `ui/DemoTimePassing.tsx` renders
the demo's own equivalent from the same pure seams: `advanceSweepFrame` samples the displayed date previous →
current, `advanceSkyRate` (the ref `SkySphere` already accepts) makes the backdrop flow, and the layers project at
the sampled date — so the stars dim in front of the viewer and time arrives as something that happens to the place,
never a date-string swap. A launch that moves the clock forward plays the same sweep. During the tutorial the
covering mask lifts for the sweep (and for a same-day launch's awaken) and returns `demo.mask_lift_linger_ms`
after it ends, so the presentation is never watched through the covering.

**"무제한 별가루" is discharged by absence, not by a large number.** No balance, no cost, no quote and no spend gate is
rendered or reachable: `features/twinkle-balance-hud`, `features/spend-cost-display` and `widgets/stardust` are all
banned by the import rule. A currency figure on a page that never charges anything would be noise at best and a payment
smell at worst — exactly what [Z8] forbids.
