# 79. demo page

> Demo page — the public, non-auth `/demo` route that runs the fixed ten beats of [Z3] over the **real** render layers:
> a diary splits, stars launch, a shared neuron pulls a constellation together, time is shoved forward until the stars
> dim and lose words, a recall reshapes one, more time lifts gists into the neocortex band, the sky takes the universe's
> emotion, a few ornaments are tried on, and the visitor is invited to sign up. It is a frontend-only sandbox: no auth,
> no RPC, no DB write, no LLM ([Z1]); free time travel and no spend gate at all ([Z2]); no price, purchase, achievement
> or payment surface anywhere ([Z8]); nothing survives leaving the page ([Z7]). It leaves the step engine to a sibling
> and the fixtures to another. Scope: FE | Status: planning
>
> Layer P. Home: `apps/web/src/pages/demo/{index.ts, ui/, model/}` on a public route under `rootRoute`.
> It fills the last seam of Epic P — the surface that binds
> [77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md)'s data to [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md)'s
> steps — and deliberately opens **no** seam into the real code path.

## Purpose

The demo is not a product, it is a trailer ([PRD.md](../PRD.md) §5.12 preamble). A visitor arriving from the landing
page ([81.landing-page](81.landing-page.md), [K3]) has to decide "is this worth an account?" in tens of seconds. What
makes cosimosi worth an account is precisely what a first session cannot show: a star only dims after days of not being
remembered, a gist only rises after the timer runs, the sky only takes on a color once enough memories accumulate. So
the demo's whole reason to exist is compressing days into seconds — which is why [Z2] hands it free time travel and
lifts every cost, and why [I13] exempts it from the invariants outright.

That exemption is the engineering problem. An exempt sandbox is safe only while it is **unreachable from the real code
path**: one `if (isDemo)` inside `packages/memory-logic`, or one demo-shaped bypass on the universe clock, and [I10]
stops holding for real accounts too — which is exactly the reason [I13] gives for demanding isolation. This unit is
therefore two things at once: a presentation surface, and the boundary that makes the exemption harmless. This plan
owns where the demo lives and what it may import, makes that boundary mechanical rather than a review habit, and
names the one mechanism that closes it completely.

The third job is honesty. `/demo` is a public surface, so [I12] governs every sentence on it: "inspired by engram
theory", never "works like a brain"; the 3D positions are an emergent visualization, never the brain's coordinates; no
therapeutic claim. And because a free trial that smells of money is worse than no trial, [Z8] keeps every price,
purchase, achievement and payment affordance off the page — the ornament taster ([Z3]-9) shows _what it looks like_,
never _what it costs_. Finally [Z7]: nothing made here carries into an account, so [U2]'s "your first universe is
empty" survives the funnel.

## Design

### Placement (FSD §3.1)

```
apps/web/src/pages/demo/
├── index.ts                     public API = DemoPage (props: onSignUp)
├── ui/DemoPage.tsx              the page: canvas host + control rail + the sequence chrome
├── ui/DemoUniverseScene.tsx     the demo canvas host (the EngramUniverseCanvas blueprint)
├── ui/DemoControlRail.tsx       the demo-local controls the tour highlights ([O2])
├── model/anchors.ts             DemoAnchor · DemoSignal — the two host-owned unions [78] is generic over
└── model/                       the demo clock · the beat handlers · the taster selection
```

There is **no `apps/demo`**: a second app would duplicate the `@cosimosi/3d-renderer` host wiring, the Vite/worker/build
config and a second root the FSD and language gates must be taught, for a page whose value is the real renderer.
`route-tree.tsx` gains a `demoRoute` under `rootRoute` at `/demo` with no `beforeLoad`; `route-screens.tsx` gains a
`DemoRoute` wrapper supplying `onSignUp` — the page never imports the router, matching the shipped
`UniverseRoute`/`LoginRoute` seam. Deployment needs **no change**: the Worker's
`assets.not_found_handling: "single-page-application"` already serves any SPA deep link, on the live origin recorded in
[DEPLOY.md](../../DEPLOY.md) §1.

`apps/web/steiger.config.js` gains one scoped `'fsd/insignificant-slice': 'off'` block for `./src/pages/demo/**`.
**Mobile parity is explicitly waived** — the demo is a Visitor surface reached from the landing page, and
`00.overview` §0 gives the Visitor web-funnel behaviors only ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4). The waiver follows
the admin-console precedent (`spec/policy/ops/admin.md` §6: "the web↔mobile parity rule is deliberately waived for it"), stated here because
`scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` does not enforce web↔mobile page peering — an unstated waiver is silently broken.

### The isolation boundary ([I13]) — the enforcement mechanism

One ESLint block scoped to `src/pages/demo/**` in `apps/web/eslint.config.js`, and it must be read as a **closure, not
an allowlist**:

| Banned from `pages/demo`                                                                                                                                                                       | Why it closes something                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `@connectrpc/*`                                                                                                                                                                                | the only source of `useTransport()`; without it no hook can reach the wire                                               |
| `@cosimosi/api-client`, `@cosimosi/client-cache`                                                                                                                                               | the generated clients and the query/cache seam                                                                           |
| `@cosimosi/universe/react`, `@cosimosi/twinkle/react`, `@cosimosi/memory/react`, `@cosimosi/emotion/react`'s `changePalette` · `useChangePalette` · `readPalettePreference` (by `importNames`) | the server-backed read mirrors and the one palette write that hits `AccountService`                                      |
| path patterns matching `widgets/*`, `features/*`, `entities/*`                                                                                                                                 | narrows the normally-legal `pages → widgets` edge: the demo composes `packages/*` directly, like `pages/test` does today |

Because **every** RPC-issuing function in `packages/*` takes an `ApiTransport` as its first argument, and every hook
that hides one calls `useTransport()`, a `pages/demo` starved of both cannot issue a call even if a server-calling
barrel export is imported by accident. That is why the rule needs no maintained allowlist of forbidden symbols.

Two mechanical notes, so the rule actually lands: ESLint flat config **replaces** rule options per matching file, so the
demo block must **restate** the existing `three` / `@react-three/fiber` ban (`src/pages/**` already matches it) or the
renderer ban would be lost for demo files. And the corresponding positive rule — **no `isDemo` flag, prop or branch in
`packages/*`, `features/*`, `entities/*`** — is discharged by this plan adding none, and by
[77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md) defining no pure function locally; `lint:fsd:layout`
R4 catches a copy-pasted mirror.

**The precise reading of [Z1]**, recorded here: "서버 호출 없음" = no `apps/api` RPC, no DB write, no LLM
(`Extractor` / `Semanticizer` / `Embedder`, and `spec/ubiquitous-language.md` §5's standing rule that the demo uses no
AI port). Platform telemetry (`WebObservabilityProvider`) and the app-shell auth-session bootstrap in `App.tsx` are
not product calls and are out of scope — they run above every route, including this one.

**Never mounted on `/demo`**: `UniverseCanvasWidget` (its `useUniverse()` throws unauthenticated),
`StardustOverlay`, `UniverseTimeOverlay`, `WritingFlowSheet` (its split is an LLM call), `RecallFlowSheet`,
`DeletionFlowSheet`, and `DetailPanel`'s provenance / gist reads.

### The demo canvas host

`DemoUniverseScene` follows the shipped `EngramUniverseCanvas` blueprint exactly: it owns the three read-model
singletons while mounted (`useEpisodicMemoryStore` / `useNeuronStore` / `useSynapseStore` `setAll`), mounts
`<SkinProvider defaultSkin={resolveActiveSkin(VALUES.rendering.activeSkin)}>`, and composes inside one
`<UniverseCanvas>`: `SkySphere` · `StarField` · `NebulaField` · `LatentStarField` · `CellStarLayer` · `StarLayer` ·
`FilamentLayer` · `BandFog` · `GistStarLayer` · `AwakenNeuron` · `CameraControls` · `FrameTick` · `PostFX`. Positions
come from `createUniverseSimBridge(null)` (the inline, main-thread bridge — no worker) fed by
`buildUniverseGraph(snapshot)` over a locally constructed `UniverseSnapshot`, pumped by
`<FrameTick onFrame={pump}/>`. Bloom and `camera.fov` stay the fixed skin's, never taster-controlled
([71.ornament-catalog-model](71.ornament-catalog-model.md) keeps them outside the catalog).

Fixtures are written as **domain** shapes (`EpisodicMemory` / `Neuron` / `Synapse` from `packages/memory`) straight into
the stores, skipping the proto/DTO mappers — so no `bigint`↔`int64` handling is involved and no api-client type
is imported.

### The ten beats ([Z3])

Each beat is a `SequenceStep<DemoAnchor, DemoSignal>` from [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md): a caption, an
optional anchor naming a control to highlight, and how the step advances. `SequenceStep` is generic over **host-owned
string-literal unions** (78), so this page declares `DemoAnchor` and `DemoSignal` in `pages/demo/model/anchors.ts` —
naming only the demo-local controls in `DemoControlRail` and the demo-local outcomes they produce, exactly as
[80.onboarding-tour](80.onboarding-tour.md) declares its own pair. A typo in the scenario is then a compile error, and
a control the demo does not own has no member to name. The step carries no handler: the handler this page binds is
keyed off the beat id, because 78's step model has no action or effect field. The visitor **acts** —
[O2] forbids video or slides.

Two mechanics worth pinning because they are easy to get wrong. First, the **gist timer anchor** the FE
`EpisodicMemory` mirror does not carry lives in `pages/demo/model` as demo-local state — **no field is added to the
shared type** (that would be a demo bypass in shared code). Second, `useAwakenRegistryStore` is a module-level
idempotency registry that survives remounts, so a second run in the same tab would skip the awaken; the demo calls
`resetUniverseUserState()` on **mount** (which resets the registry, the latent-consumed store and the three read
models) so every entry and every replay starts from a genuinely clean sandbox ([O3]).

### The demo clock and "unlimited stardust" ([Z2])

The clock is a mutable ISO date in `pages/demo/model` with a single control: advance by
`demo.time_travel_step_days`. There is no monotonicity check, no launch precondition, no sync-consent modal and no
diary-date constraint — [Z2] exempts all of it, and none of that exemption is expressible in shared code because the
demo simply passes its date down as the `universeTime` prop the layers already accept. A beat that needs a deeper jump
repeats the step; the repeat count is scenario content, not a tuning number.

**"무제한 별가루" is discharged by absence, not by a large number.** No balance, no cost, no quote and no spend gate is
rendered or reachable: `features/twinkle-balance-hud`, `features/spend-cost-display` and `widgets/stardust` are all
banned by the import rule. A currency figure on a page that never charges anything would be noise at best and a payment
smell at worst — exactly what [Z8] forbids.

### Beat 9: the ornament taster ([Z3]-9, [Z8])

The taster shows a handful of items per kind and **imports catalog ids only** — never the price table, never the
ownership or selection model, never `Decorate` ([73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md) owns the preview and
the save flow). Concretely:

- **`PALETTE`** → `applyPalette(id)` from `@cosimosi/emotion/react` (`setMoodPalette` + the zustand mirror; server-free).
  `changePalette` is banned by `importNames` because it calls `AccountService`.
- **`BACKGROUND`** → the demo owns its canvas, so it sets `SkySphere`'s `effect` prop directly from its own state, the
  way `/test` does today. It does **not** call `setSkinKey` — a whole-skin swap also moves bloom and `camera.fov`, which
  [71.ornament-catalog-model](71.ornament-catalog-model.md) places outside the catalog. (`setSkinKey` is
  [73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md)'s seam on the
  _product_ canvas, which the demo never mounts.)
- **`STAR_SHADER`** → a `StarVariantKey` in demo state, passed through
  [71.ornament-catalog-model](71.ornament-catalog-model.md)'s `createStarBodySource(variant)` plumbing and remounted via
  the existing `key={`star-${…}`}` pattern.

The demo does **not** use [73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md)'s preview store: that store is coupled to the
save flow and the unowned total, i.e. to a price. Keeping the taster's selection in `pages/demo/model` keeps [Z8]
structural (there is no code path from the taster to a total) and keeps `packages/store` ignorant of the demo.

### Nothing carries over ([Z7])

On unmount the page clears everything it touched: `resetUniverseUserState()` (the three read models, the clock store,
the awaken registry, the latent-consumed store), `resetMoodPalette()` + `usePalettePreferenceStore.getState().reset()`
so a tasted palette cannot bleed into the landing page or a later session, and its own `model` state dies with the

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `/demo` is reachable **without authentication**, registered under `rootRoute` with **no `beforeLoad`**, outside the
   authenticated subtree, so neither the auth guard nor `PaletteBootstrap` runs on it ([Z1]).
2. Loading, running and completing the whole sequence issues **zero** `apps/api` RPCs, **zero** DB writes and **zero**
   LLM calls; no AI port is referenced ([Z1], UL §5).

   `@cosimosi/api-client`, `@cosimosi/client-cache`, the `*/react` hook entrypoints, `changePalette` /
   `useChangePalette` / `readPalettePreference`, and the `widgets`/`features`/`entities` path patterns — while still
   restating the `three` / `@react-three/fiber` ban ([I13], [00.overview](00.overview.md) §3).

   defines no pure function of its own ([I13][Z6]).

   bridge, `<FrameTick/>`); `UniverseCanvasWidget`, `StardustOverlay`, `UniverseTimeOverlay`, `WritingFlowSheet`,
   `RecallFlowSheet`, `DeletionFlowSheet` and `DetailPanel`'s provenance/gist reads are never mounted ([Z1]).

   shared neuron, dimming + word loss, recall with a seed change, gist rise into the neocortex band, the sky taking the

   [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md) with a caption, a highlighted control and an always-visible skip
   ([Z3][O2][O4]).
7. Entering `/demo` starts the sequence **every time**, and a replay in the same tab plays the awaken choreography again
   (the awaken registry and the read models are reset at mount) ([O3]).
8. One `DemoDiarySet` is chosen at random per run and every split result is identical for the same set — only _which
   set_ varies between runs ([Z4][Z5]).

   monotonicity check, no consent modal and no cost, and no such bypass exists in shared code ([Z2][I10][I13]).
