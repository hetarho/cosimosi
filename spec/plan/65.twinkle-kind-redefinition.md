# 65. twinkle kind redefinition

> Twinkle kind redefinition — the shipped two tiers stop meaning _how the Twinkle was obtained_ and start meaning
> _what it may buy_: `TwinkleKind.SMALL` (recall-only, refilled on the user's own calendar day) and
> `TwinkleKind.GENERAL` (universal, permanent). One unit performs the whole rename across domain, contract and FE
> mirror; makes the day boundary the user's timezone instead of UTC; and makes "SMALL bought an ornament"
> unrepresentable in the plan function. The ledger's earn/spend orchestration, the purchase reason, the ledger CHECK
> and every UI surface belong to siblings. Scope: BE | Status: planning
>
> Layer D. Home: the existing core context `apps/api/internal/twinkle` (pure domain in `ledger.go`) plus its
> golden-parity mirror `packages/twinkle-logic`. It fills the vocabulary seam [43.stardust-ledger](43.stardust-ledger.md)
> left ("basic = the daily one, additional = the charged one") and leaves the earn/spend surface that _uses_ the new
> vocabulary to [66.earn-and-purchase-spend](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md).

## Purpose

v1 shipped a two-tier balance named after provenance. `Balance.Basic` meant "the tier the daily grant fills";
`Balance.Additional` meant "the tier a payment fills". v2 deletes the payment path entirely (payment moves to v3)
and adds a second thing to buy — ornaments ([P9]) — which makes the shipped names actively wrong: `Additional` is no longer "additional
to" anything, and `Basic` is no longer "the basic way you get Twinkle", it is _the tier you may only spend on
remembering_. [G2a] promotes Twinkle to the product's single currency and states plainly that 작은 별가루 "는 별개
화폐가 아니라 용도 제한된 하위 종류" — a **purpose** restriction, not an origin story. This unit renames the concept
to what it now means, in one commit, at every layer, because a concept renamed in three epics is exactly the drift
ARCHITECTURE §2.9 #1 forbids.

The purpose restriction is load-bearing, not cosmetic. [G5] is the reason the two kinds exist at all: "꾸미기 지출이
회상을 잠식하지 않는 것이 이 구조의 목적" — a decoration purchase must be structurally incapable of eating today's
recall budget. Once [P9] exists, the shipped `PlanSpend` is a live bug: it takes no purpose, drains the daily tier
first unconditionally, and would happily pay for a background out of the user's 회상 allowance. This unit gives the
plan function the purpose it was missing and makes the recall-only rule a property of a closed switch rather than of
a caller's discipline.

The day boundary is the second correction. [G2] says the SMALL refill boundary is `User.Timezone`, and [U7] names
that timezone as the authority for three separate things. The shipped derivation hard-codes UTC (`utcDay` in
`apps/api/internal/twinkle/ledger.go`, mirrored in `packages/twinkle-logic/src/stardust-ledger.ts`), so a Seoul user's
"today" refills at 09:00 local — the one place in the product where the economy misreads the user's own day.
[60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md) lands `users.timezone` first
([00.overview](00.overview.md) §5.6); this unit is the first
consumer of it.

## Scope / Non-goals

**In scope**

- **The domain rename** in `apps/api/internal/twinkle/ledger.go` ([G2][G2a]): `Balance{Small, General}`,
  `BalanceRecord{General, SmallSpentThisWindow, SmallResetWindow}`, `SpendPlan{FromSmall, FromGeneral, OK}`,
  `SmallRemaining`, and a `TwinkleKind` enum with `SMALL`/`GENERAL` naming the two tiers as UL and PRD §3.2 already
  spell them.
- **The user-timezone day boundary** ([G2][U7]): a pure `ResetWindowOf(now, zone)` that replaces the private
  `utcDay`, a consumer-owned zone port on the twinkle context, and the composition-root adapter that binds it to
  `account`'s published profile read.
- **Purpose-aware spending** ([G5][P9]): the Go `SpendKind` (the rename of the shipped `QuoteKind`, aligned with
  the proto enum that already carries that name), a closed `SmallEligible(kind)` predicate, `PlanSpend(smallRemaining,
general, cost, kind)`, and the tier-aware `ShortfallFor(...)` that replaces the wrong shortfall arithmetic in
  `QuoteSpend`.
- **The contract rename**: `twinkle.v1.GetBalanceResponse{small = 1; general = 2; total = 3}` and
  `admin.v1.AdminUser.small/general`, plus `admin.Balance{Small, General}` and the `cmd/api/admin.go` adapter.
- **The golden-parity mirror**: `packages/twinkle-logic/src/stardust-ledger.ts` renamed function-for-function, given
  the zone and kind parameters, and `apps/api/internal/twinkle/testdata/stardust-ledger-golden.json` regenerated with
  new case keys, zone cases and per-kind `plan_spend` cases.
- **The FE domain mirror rename** ([§3.5] shared-package rule): `TwinkleBalance{small, general}` in
  `packages/twinkle/src/twinkle-balance-store.ts`, the `setBalance` call in `packages/twinkle/src/react/balance.ts`,
  and the mechanical field-name follow-through in `apps/{web,mobile}/src/features/twinkle-balance-hud/ui/TwinkleBalanceHud.tsx`.
- **The values key rename**: `twinkle.basic_daily_amount` → `twinkle.small_daily_amount`, with the comment corrected
  from "UTC calendar day" to the user's local calendar day.
- **The ubiquitous-language gate refresh**: `scripts/lint-ubiquitous-language.mjs`'s `forbiddenSynonyms` set
  is rebuilt against the current UL and gains per-rule path scoping plus the retired-tier patterns.

**Non-goals**

  `basic_spent_this_window`, `basic_reset_window` and `twinkle_ledger_entries.from_basic`/`from_additional` keep their

## Design

### Placement (domain-first Go — no new context, no new package)

The redefinition is a **change of meaning inside an existing core context**, so per ARCHITECTURE §2.3 nothing moves:
the pure types and functions stay in `apps/api/internal/twinkle/ledger.go`, the use-case call sites in
`service.go`, the row mapper in `pg/store.go`, the proto mapper in `rpc/server.go`. No `domain/` or `app/` split is
earned by a rename (§2.9 #2). The TS mirror stays `packages/twinkle-logic`; the FE balance mirror stays
`packages/twinkle`. The one genuinely new structural element is a port (below), which lives in the file that already
holds this context's consumer-owned ports, `apps/api/internal/twinkle/ports.go`.

### The two kinds, redefined by purpose

`TwinkleKind` becomes a real domain type — a TEXT-style closed set like `EntryKind`, not a PG enum, since it is never
persisted as a column value:

```
TwinkleKind ∈ { SMALL, GENERAL }
Balance         = { Small int, General int }                 // Total() = Small + General
BalanceRecord   = { General, SmallSpentThisWindow int, SmallResetWindow time }
SpendPlan       = { FromSmall, FromGeneral int, OK bool }
Balance.Of(kind TwinkleKind) int                             // the per-tier accessor
```

`SMALL` is a **derivation, never a stored counter and never earned** — unchanged from
[43.stardust-ledger](43.stardust-ledger.md) and reaffirmed here: no cron, no `daily_grant` row, no scheduled
refill. `GENERAL` is the stored carrying counter every earn path credits. The store already rejects a negative
`smallSpentDelta` (`ErrDeltaOutOfRange`), which is what makes "SMALL was earned" and "SMALL was refunded"
unrepresentable in persistence.

### The day boundary — the user's calendar day, resolved through a port

One pure function owns the boundary rule, and it is the only place a zone is interpreted:

```
ResetWindowOf(now time, zone *Location) date       // the user's LOCAL calendar date
SmallRemaining(now time, zone *Location, resetWindow date, spentThisWindow int) int
DeriveBalance(now time, zone *Location, record BalanceRecord) Balance
```

- **The zone-free variants cease to exist.** There is no `SmallRemaining` overload without a zone and no surviving
  `utcDay`, so every stale call site is a compile error rather than a silent UTC read. That is the structural guard
  for [U7] here.
- **An unresolvable zone resolves to UTC, deterministically, on both sides, and never errors.** An empty string, an
  unknown IANA name, or a user with no profile row yet all derive exactly today's shipped behavior. This direction is
  chosen deliberately: [G5] says the daily grant must never be blocked, so a missing zone may not deny a refill.
- **The zone arrives as a scalar through a consumer-owned port** (§2.2/§2.4):
  `twinkle.UserZoneReader { ZoneFor(ctx, scope) (string, error) }` declared in `twinkle/ports.go`, returning an IANA
  name. The concrete adapter lives in `apps/api/cmd/api/twinkle.go` over `account`'s published profile read; `twinkle`
  never imports `account` and never queries `users`. `NewService` **requires** it (`ErrZoneReaderRequired`), like
  `Ledger` and `Signals` today — production cannot boot with the boundary unbound, and no permissive UTC adapter is
  exported from the package for a composition root to bind by accident (the same fail-closed discipline
  [75.achievement-tracking-usecase](75.achievement-tracking-usecase.md) applies to its recorder port). The adapter
  maps "no profile row" to the empty string, which the derivation then reads as UTC.
- **`time.LoadLocation` is made hermetic** by importing `_ "time/tzdata"` in `apps/api/cmd/api`. The API image is
  `gcr.io/distroless/static-debian12`; embedding the zone database removes any dependence on the base image shipping
  `/usr/share/zoneinfo`.
- **The anchor is monotone, so a timezone change can never mint a grant.** `twinkle_balances.basic_reset_window`
  advances through `GREATEST(basic_reset_window, $reset_window)` in `db/queries/twinkle/ledger.sql`, and
  `SmallRemaining` derives the conservative `grant − spent` branch whenever the local date is at or before the anchor.
  A user toggling zones westward keeps the current window; toggling eastward can bring the _next_ refill forward by at
  most the 26-hour offset span, once, and never yields two refills for one calendar date. No rate limit on the `/me`
  timezone control is needed, and none is specified.
- The service passes `ResetWindowOf(now, zone)` — not `now` — as `ApplyBalanceDelta`'s `resetWindow`, and the
  `basic_grant` query argument becomes `values.TwinkleSmallDailyAmount`. The port signature is otherwise unchanged.

### Purpose-aware spending — [P9] as a closed switch

The proto already declares `twinkle.v1.SpendKind{SPEND_KIND_RECALL, SPEND_KIND_GIST_VIEW, SPEND_KIND_DIARY_RECALL}`
while the Go side calls the same concept `QuoteKind`. That is a §2.9 #1 violation already on disk, so this unit
**renames the Go type to `SpendKind`** rather than adding a third enum, and adds the purpose the domain was missing:

```
SpendKind ∈ { RECALL, GIST_VIEW, DIARY_RECALL, PURCHASE }
SmallEligible(kind SpendKind) bool                 // closed switch; recall family true, everything else false
PlanSpend(smallRemaining, general, cost int, kind SpendKind) SpendPlan
ShortfallFor(smallRemaining, general, cost int, kind SpendKind) int
```

- `SmallEligible` enumerates `RECALL`, `GIST_VIEW`, `DIARY_RECALL` and returns **false in the default arm**. A kind
  added later is ineligible until someone deliberately lists it — the fail-closed direction [P9] needs. [G2]'s
  "회상에만" is thereby named as exactly those three kinds, which is not a new judgment: it is the shipped paid-read set
  (`memory.PaidActionKind{recall, diary_recall, view_semantic}`, `memory_paid_action_receipts.action_kind`), and [G4]
  prices the [D3] diary jump as the sum of its per-memory recalls, so a diary recall _is_ a recall.
- `PlanSpend` returns `FromSmall: 0` whenever `SmallEligible(kind)` is false, then draws `FromGeneral = cost` and sets
  `OK = FromGeneral ≤ general`. For the recall family the shipped SMALL→GENERAL order is unchanged ([G2]).
- **Ineligibility is not an error.** There is no `ErrSmallNotEligible`: a purchase simply draws zero from SMALL. The
  only denial remains `ErrInsufficientTwinkle`, and the item-naming `STORE_INSUFFICIENT_TWINKLE` variant is plan 72's.
- `ShortfallFor` replaces `apps/api/internal/twinkle/service.go`'s `shortfall = cost - balance.Basic -
balance.Additional`, which over-reports coverage the moment a non-recall kind exists. `QuoteSpend`'s signature takes
  `SpendKind`; its `quoteCost` switch gains **no `PURCHASE` arm**, and the proto enum gains **no
  `SPEND_KIND_PURCHASE` value**, so no client can ask the recall pricer to price an ornament — the Go enum is
  deliberately a superset of the wire enum, and a purchase reaches the economy only through plan 72's internal
  `store.SpendGate` port.
- `SpendKind` and `EntryReason` stay distinct and this plan touches only the former: `SpendKind` is the _purpose_ a
  spend is planned against; `EntryReason` is the persisted ledger vocabulary whose closed set plan 66 alone owns.
  A `DIARY_RECALL` spend still writes one `recall` row per member memory.

### What persistence keeps, and the one bilingual file

No column is renamed and no migration is written. `twinkle_balances.additional`, `basic_spent_this_window`,
`basic_reset_window`, `twinkle_ledger_entries.from_basic` and `from_additional` keep their spelling; a rename
migration on an append-only ledger buys nothing and risks the shipped CHECK constraints. The mapping
`small ↔ basic*`, `general ↔ additional` is documented once, in `spec/tech/twinkle-economy.md`, and
`apps/api/internal/twinkle/pg/store.go` is the **only** file permitted to speak both spellings — which is precisely
the role §2.4 gives the row↔domain mapper. The DDL comment's now-wrong "UTC calendar day of the current basic window"
is corrected in the tech doc and in the values comment; `00007_twinkle_ledger.sql` is a shipped migration and is not
edited.

### The contract surface (renames only, no new RPC)

- `proto/cosimosi/twinkle/v1/twinkle.proto`: `GetBalanceResponse{int64 small = 1; int64 general = 2; int64 total = 3}`.
- `proto/cosimosi/admin/v1/admin.proto`: `AdminUser.small = 6`, `AdminUser.general = 7` (numbers unchanged).
- `apps/api/internal/admin/types.go` `admin.Balance{Small, General, Total}` and the adapter in
  `apps/api/cmd/api/admin.go`. The admin console FE renders only `total`, so no admin UI change follows.
- **No RPC is added or removed**, so `packages/client-cache/src/http-policy.ts` needs no new classification and the
  `NO_SIDE_EFFECTS` inventory is untouched. `pnpm gen` regenerates Go + TS clients; `pnpm check:gen` gates
  freshness. `buf lint STANDARD` is satisfied (field names are lower_snake_case); `buf breaking` is configured in
  `proto/buf.yaml` but is invoked by no script or workflow, so the field rename has no gate to clear.

### The FE mirrors

- `packages/twinkle-logic/src/stardust-ledger.ts` mirrors the Go names function for function: `smallRemaining`,
  `resetWindowOf`, `planSpend(smallRemaining, general, cost, kind)`, `smallEligible`, `shortfallFor`,
  `SpendPlan{fromSmall, fromGeneral, ok}`, and a `SpendKind` string union. `resetWindowOf` resolves the IANA zone with
  `Intl.DateTimeFormat` inside a try/catch that falls back to UTC, so an unknown zone — or a runtime whose `Intl`
  lacks `timeZone` support — degrades to today's behavior instead of throwing. This is safe to ship to React Native
  because **no production FE path calls the derivation**: the tier is read from `GetBalance` and displayed, exactly as
  [43.stardust-ledger](43.stardust-ledger.md) pinned (server-authoritative balance, FE prices only). If plan 67's
  refill marker needs the local-day grouping, it calls this same function rather than writing a second one.
- `packages/twinkle/src/twinkle-balance-store.ts`: `TwinkleBalance{small, general}`, `setBalance(small, general)`,
  `clear()`, and `twinkleTotal`. `packages/twinkle/src/react/balance.ts` syncs `data.small`/`data.general`.
- `apps/{web,mobile}/src/features/twinkle-balance-hud/ui/TwinkleBalanceHud.tsx` follow the store fields through. This
  is the rename's mechanical tail, not a presentation change: **no copy, no layout, no i18n key moves here** — the HUD
  keeps calling the existing label keys until plan 67 renames them. That ordering is deliberate: the key rename
  and the deletion of 기본 별가루 / 추가 별가루 are one atomic edit, and it belongs to the plan that owns the balance
  surfaces.

### The ubiquitous-language gate refresh

`scripts/lint-ubiquitous-language.mjs`'s `forbiddenSynonyms` currently enforces a vocabulary that does not exist —
`MemoryCell → "use EngramCell"`, `MemoryRecord`, `Memory → "use Engram"` — while UL and PRD §3.2 canonicalize
`EpisodicMemory`/`SemanticMemory`/`Neuron`, and the rules are inert only because `\bMemory\b` never matches a real
identifier. This unit rebuilds the set:

1. Each rule gains an optional path **scope** (include roots + exclude prefixes); rules without one keep scanning
   everything, so the existing rendering-term and memory-edge checks are unaffected.
2. The three stale memory rules are replaced by rules that name the real canonical targets
   (`MemoryCell`/`MemoryRecord`/a standalone `Memory` → `EpisodicMemory` or `SemanticMemory`, chosen per layer). The
   set must be verified inert against the current tree before it lands — a gate that fails on shipped code is a gate
   nobody keeps.
3. The retired-tier rules land scoped to `apps/*/src`, `packages/*/src`, `apps/api/internal` and `proto`, **excluding
   `apps/api/db/**` and `apps/api/internal/twinkle/pg/**`** — the two places the persisted `basic*`/`additional`
   spelling is correct. Patterns: `\bBasicBalance\b`, `\bAdditionalBalance\b`, `\bbasicRemaining\b` (case-insensitive,
   catching `BasicRemaining`), `\bFromBasic\b`/`\bFromAdditional\b` (case-insensitive; `from_basic` does not match),
   `basic Twinkle`, `additional Twinkle`, and the retired user-facing `기본 별가루` / `추가 별가루`. The gate runs as
   `pnpm lint:language`.
4. The `inventory|equip|장착|보관함` rule lands scoped to `apps/*/src` +
   `packages/*/src` — verified to have zero current hits — because it is the only mechanical enforcement [P7] will
   ever get.
5. The Korean patterns bite in **source** files only, because the scan's extension set is

### Golden-parity stance

**Yes — every pure function in this unit mirrors TS↔Go and the mirror is pinned.** `SmallRemaining`,
`ResetWindowOf`, `PlanSpend`, `SmallEligible`, `ShortfallFor`, `RecallCost` and `GistViewCost` live once in
`apps/api/internal/twinkle/ledger.go` and once in `packages/twinkle-logic/src/stardust-ledger.ts`, read the same

`apps/api/internal/twinkle/testdata/stardust-ledger-golden.json` (regenerated with `UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`). The fixture's
`values` block key becomes `small_daily_amount`; the case keys become `small_remaining`, `from_small`, `from_general`;
`small_remaining` cases gain a `zone` input covering `Asia/Seoul`, a UTC+14 zone, a UTC−11 zone, an empty zone and an
unknown zone; `plan_spend` cases gain a `kind` input with at least one eligible and one ineligible branch at the same

not a mirror** — the FE reads `GetBalance` and never advances a tier ([43.stardust-ledger](43.stardust-ledger.md)).

### Re-implementation note

Another client reproduces this unit from: two kinds distinguished by _what they may buy_; `SMALL` = a fixed daily
amount minus the amount spent inside the current window, where the window is the user's **local** calendar date and a
later local date derives a fresh full grant with no carry; `GENERAL` = a stored carrying counter; spend order
`SMALL` → `GENERAL` for the recall family and `GENERAL` only for everything else; an unresolvable zone reads as UTC; and
a window anchor that never moves backward.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The domain names the two tiers by purpose: `twinkle.TwinkleKind` exists with exactly `SMALL` and `GENERAL`, and
   `Balance{Small, General}`, `BalanceRecord{General, SmallSpentThisWindow, SmallResetWindow}` and
   `SpendPlan{FromSmall, FromGeneral, OK}` carry no `Basic`/`Additional` identifier anywhere in
   `apps/api/internal/twinkle` outside `pg/` ([G2][G2a], §2.9 #1).
2. `SmallRemaining` and `DeriveBalance` take the user's zone; **no zone-free variant and no `utcDay` helper remains**,
   so a caller cannot silently read a UTC day ([G2][U7]).
3. `ResetWindowOf(now, zone)` returns the user's **local** calendar date, and `SmallRemaining` derives a fresh full
   grant when that date is later than the stored anchor and `grant − spentThisWindow` otherwise ([G2]).
4. An empty, blank or unknown IANA zone resolves to UTC on both the Go and TS sides, returns no error, and never
   suppresses a refill ([G5]).
5. `twinkle.UserZoneReader` is declared in `apps/api/internal/twinkle/ports.go`, returns an IANA name as a scalar, is
   bound only at `apps/api/cmd/api/twinkle.go`, and `NewService` fails with `ErrZoneReaderRequired` when it is nil;
   `apps/api/internal/twinkle` imports neither `internal/account` nor any `users` query (§2.2/§2.4).
6. The stored window anchor never moves backward: after a spend under an eastward zone change and then a westward one,
   the derived SMALL tier for the same calendar date is never a second full grant ([G2]).
7. `PlanSpend(smallRemaining, general, cost, kind)` returns `FromSmall: 0` for every kind that is not in the recall
   family, draws SMALL first for `RECALL`/`GIST_VIEW`/`DIARY_RECALL`, sets `OK` from the GENERAL remainder, and never
   produces a negative tier ([G2][G5][P9]).
8. `SmallEligible` is a closed switch whose default arm returns **false**, proven by a test that an unlisted
   `SpendKind` value is ineligible ([P9][I11], [00.overview](00.overview.md) §3).
9. `QuoteSpend`'s shortfall is tier-aware — `ShortfallFor` counts SMALL only for an eligible kind — so a quote for a

    exist in `twinkle.v1.SpendKind`, and `quoteCost` has no purchase arm — a purchase cannot be quoted over the wire
