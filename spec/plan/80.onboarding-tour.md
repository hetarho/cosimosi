# 80. onboarding tour

> Onboarding tour — the same sequence engine the demo runs, pointed at the **real** universe and fed by the user's own
> first diary ([O1]). It opens once, at the instant signup completes and before the universe is anything but a gray
> field, and it is replayable forever after from `/me` ([O3][O5]); a skip is on screen at every step ([O4]). Unlike the
> demo, nothing here is exempt: the tour highlights and narrates while every write is the user's own press through the
> shipped flow, so [I10], [I2], [I11] and [G1] hold untouched — enforced by closed anchor/signal unions and a
> report-only signal seam, not by care. Scope: FE | Status: planning
>
> Layer P. Home: a new pure package `packages/onboarding` (the script, the two closed unions, the start policy) plus
> anchor wrappers at two composition sites and one `features/replay-onboarding` row on `/me`, in **both** apps
> ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4).
> It closes the exposure seam [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md) deliberately left open and opens none of its
> own.

## Purpose

[53.auth-universe-gate](53.auth-universe-gate.md) put a new account down in an empty universe with one affordance and
one quiet line, and that was the right answer for v1: empty is a welcome, not an error. But a gray latent field with a
일기 쓰기 button explains nothing about why the button matters. The product's whole proposition — a diary splits into
stars, the stars find each other through shared neurons, they dim when you never come back, and a recall rewrites one —
is a sequence of consequences, and the first of them only happens after the user has already committed to writing.
[O3] therefore places the tour at the one moment where the cost of explaining is lowest and the payoff is immediate:
after the nickname, before the universe.

[O1] then makes a specific and unusual demand. Onboarding is not a second tutorial system: it is
[78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md) again, with the user's real first diary substituted for
[79.demo-page](79.demo-page.md)'s fixtures — "다른 것은 주입 데이터뿐". That single sentence is what makes this unit
delicate. The demo's engine runs inside a sandbox that [I13] exempts from every invariant and [Z2] hands free time
travel and unlimited stardust. Here the very same engine runs on a live account where nothing is exempt. If the tour
could seed a star to show the visitor what one looks like, [U2] would be false. If it could nudge the clock so the
first star visibly dims, [I10] would be false. If it could fire a recall so the user sees a reconsolidation, [G1] would
be false and the user would be poorer for it. Each of those is a plausible, well-meant shortcut, and each one would
convert the demo's licensed exemptions into a real bypass — the exact failure [I13] names.

So this plan is subtractive in the same way plan 78 was. The engine already cannot act ([78]: no action field, no data
payload, no host-kind flag). This unit adds the second half: the tour's **vocabulary** is two closed string-literal
unions — five anchors it may highlight and three signals it may wait for — and neither contains a paid, destructive,
decorative or clock-moving control. A tour step that spends Twinkle is not forbidden here; it is unspellable. Exposure
is answered by refusing to own a fact: there is no "already seen" record anywhere, because the moment signup completes
is already a once-per-account event and [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md) already publishes it.

## Scope / Non-goals

**In scope**

- **The onboarding script** — nine steps over the real writing flow and the real universe, expressed as
  [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md)'s `SequenceScript` with i18n caption accessors ([O1][O2]).
- **The two closed unions** — `OnboardingAnchor` (5 members) and `OnboardingSignal` (3 members), host-owned per
  [78](78.sequence-engine.md)'s generic step model, and the _absence_ of every other control as the guard ([I10][I11][G1][I1]).
- **Anchor registration at two composition sites** — `pages/universe` and `widgets/writing-flow` in both apps, each
  wrapping an existing child in `SequenceAnchor` and passing nothing down ([O2]).
- **The signal seam** — `reportSequenceSignal(signal): void`, a write-only report from those same composition sites
  into a Zustand channel, translated to the run machine's `ADVANCE` by the page that owns the actor ([O2]).
- **Exposure policy** ([O3]) — the tour starts on exactly two triggers, both take-once: the signup-completion signal
  [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md) publishes, and an explicit replay request. No durable "seen" record.
- **The run host** — `pages/universe` owns the run actor (`useActorRef`, the shipped `universeNavigationMachine`
  precedent) and mounts `widgets/sequence-guide` over the live canvas ([O2][O4]).
- **`features/replay-onboarding`** — the last row of `/me`'s profile tab, the position
  [64.my-page](64.my-page.md) reserves ([O5]).
- **User-scope reset registration** — `resetOnboardingUserState()` in both apps' inventories ([U1]).
- **Caption copy** under the `sequence_*` prefix in both catalogues, restrained and literary.
- **web + mobile parity**, no waiver ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4).

**Non-goals**

- **No engine.** The step model, the run machine, the echo guard, the spotlight, the caption band, the skip control,
  the anchor registry and the reduced-motion contract are [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md).
- **No fixtures and no demo surface.** `DemoScenario` / `DemoDiarySet` and the ten beats are
  [77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md) and [79.demo-page](79.demo-page.md); this unit imports
  neither ([Z4][Z7]).
- **No writing flow.** The sheet, the split preview, the natural-language revise, the optimistic launch and the
  past-date notice are [27.writing-flow-ui](27.writing-flow-ui.md). This unit points at them and waits.
- **No signup surface and no completion signal.** The nickname step, the profile gate and the take-once signal are
  [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md); this unit consumes the signal and adds no arm to it.
- **No `/me` page, no route, no tab.** The page, the five tabs, the `?tab=` vocabulary and the profile tab's other rows
  are [64.my-page](64.my-page.md); this unit fills one reserved row.
- **No server change.** No proto field, no `users` column, no RPC, no migration —
  [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md) fixes `users` to nickname/timezone/locale and closes the

- **No first-run welcome redesign.** [53.auth-universe-gate](53.auth-universe-gate.md)'s empty-universe welcome line
  and single affordance stay exactly as shipped; the tour runs over them ([U2][V7]).
- **No new tuning scalar** — the v2 values-group enumeration has no onboarding group and groups are closed
  ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4; see Policy).

## Design

### Placement (FSD §3.1)

```
packages/onboarding/src/          @cosimosi/onboarding — pure, shared verbatim by both apps
├── anchors.ts        OnboardingAnchor · OnboardingSignal — the two closed unions
├── script.ts         ONBOARDING_SCRIPT = defineScript<OnboardingAnchor, OnboardingSignal>(…)
├── start.ts          requestOnboardingReplay() · takeOnboardingStart() — the whole exposure policy
├── signal-channel.ts useOnboardingSignalStore · reportSequenceSignal(signal): void
├── user-state-reset.ts  resetOnboardingUserState()
└── index.ts

apps/{web,mobile}/src/features/replay-onboarding/ui/   the /me profile-tab row ([O5])
apps/{web,mobile}/src/pages/universe/ui/               owns the run actor · mounts SequenceGuide · 2 anchor wrappers
apps/{web,mobile}/src/widgets/writing-flow/ui/         3 anchor wrappers + 3 signal reports
apps/{web,mobile}/src/app/model/reset-user-state.ts    + 'onboarding' in the inventory
```

The script is a package and not app-local `model` for the reason lint R4 exists: it is byte-identical on both apps
(onboarding has no parity waiver), so an app-local copy fails the gate. It cannot live in `packages/sequence` either —
[78](78.sequence-engine.md) pins that package's dependency list to `xstate` + `zustand` precisely so the demo's import
closure can admit it, and a caption accessor needs `@cosimosi/i18n`. `@cosimosi/onboarding` is its home — the fifth of
v2's five new FE packages — and it depends on `@cosimosi/sequence` + `@cosimosi/i18n` and nothing else.
`features/replay-onboarding` gets a scoped `'fsd/insignificant-slice': 'off'` block in `apps/web/steiger.config.js`
with a stated reason, as every single-reference slice does. Each app hand-writes its own `ui`; no `*.native.tsx`
sibling is added (the
[45.stardust-ui](45.stardust-ui.md) convention).

### The two closed unions — the whole safety argument

```ts
type OnboardingAnchor =
  | 'universe-write-entry' // pages/universe: the 일기 쓰기 affordance ([W1])
  | 'writing-draft' // widgets/writing-flow: body + date + 별 쪼개기 ([W2][W5])
  | 'writing-proposal' // widgets/writing-flow: the proposed-memory list ([W2a][W4])
  | 'writing-confirm' // widgets/writing-flow: 별 띄우기 ([W3])
  | 'universe-clock' // pages/universe: the universe-time HUD ([T6])

type OnboardingSignal = 'writing-flow-opened' | 'split-succeeded' | 'launch-succeeded'
```

[78](78.sequence-engine.md) makes `SequenceStep<Anchor, Signal>` generic over host-owned unions, so a step naming
anything outside these eight strings is a compile error in this package. What the unions omit is the design:

| Absent member                          | What it would allow                                              | Requirement protected                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| a recall / 회고하기 anchor or signal   | a step that waits for a paid reconsolidation                     | [G1] paid recall; [R1a]/[T2]-2 consent                          |
| a 놓아주기 / delete anchor             | a tutorial that destroys a memory to demonstrate deletion        | [I1]; [X]                                                       |
| a clock or sync anchor/signal          | a step that moves universe time to make dimming visible          | [I10]; [T2][T3]                                                 |
| a palette / ornament anchor            | a step that changes a render parameter for effect                | [I11][V10]                                                      |
| a star anchor                          | pointing at an emergent coordinate as if it were a fixed control | [I5]; positions are rendered, never modeled                     |
| any anchor inside a `features/*` slice | a product slice learning that a tour exists                      | [I13]; [79](79.demo-page.md); [78](78.sequence-engine.md) AC 11 |

The star row is not pedantry: a star's screen position comes out of `packages/force-sim` and changes every frame, so
there is no composition site to register and no rect to measure. Anchors are static ids on layout containers, which is
exactly why the tour cannot address a domain object.

### The nine steps

Every step that represents progress the user must make is a `signal` step; `dwell` is used only for reading time,
never to fake progress ([78](78.sequence-engine.md)'s rule).

| #   | id         | anchor                 | advance                      | what the caption says                                                                 | PRD          |
| --- | ---------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| 1   | `welcome`  | —                      | dwell                        | this is your universe; stars are born only from diaries                               | [U2][V7]     |
| 2   | `entry`    | `universe-write-entry` | signal `writing-flow-opened` | start here                                                                            | [W1]         |
| 3   | `draft`    | `writing-draft`        | signal `split-succeeded`     | write the day, then split it into stars                                               | [W2][W5]     |
| 4   | `proposal` | `writing-proposal`     | dwell                        | the names and the emotions are yours to correct                                       | [W2a][W4]    |
| 5   | `confirm`  | `writing-confirm`      | signal `launch-succeeded`    | send them up                                                                          | [W3]         |
| 6   | `arrival`  | —                      | dwell                        | your first record now lives here                                                      | [W3][V7]     |
| 7   | `clock`    | `universe-clock`       | dwell                        | that date is the universe's time; watching does not move it                           | [T2][T3][T6] |
| 8   | `revisit`  | —                      | dwell                        | a star holds what you wrote; remembering it again is its own act, and it costs 별가루 | [R1][G1]     |
| 9   | `closing`  | —                      | dwell                        | you can play this again whenever you like                                             | [O5]         |

Three consequences worth stating because they are what make the script safe against reality:

- **The tour cannot get ahead of the product.** Steps 2, 3 and 5 wait on signals the real flow emits. A failed
  `SplitDiary` returns the shipped machine to `writing` and emits nothing, so step 3 simply holds its caption; a failed
  `LaunchStars` holds step 5. There is no retry logic, no timeout, no error surface of the tour's own.
- **No caption interpolates a domain value.** Every onboarding message is a zero-parameter i18n accessor, so a star
  name, a memory count, a date or a Twinkle amount cannot appear in a caption — which is also what lets one script be
  true for a first-run account _and_ for a replay over a full universe. This is the structural form of the rule "a
  script carries no data payload" ([78](78.sequence-engine.md)).
- **A past-dated first diary needs no branch.** Step 5 advances when the launch returns, and steps 6–7 are worded to
  hold whether or not a star was launched — the [T1] outcome and its one-time notice belong to
  [27.writing-flow-ui](27.writing-flow-ui.md) and are surfaced by it, not by the tour.

Step 8 is the deliberate stopping point. [G1] makes a recall cost Twinkle and [T2] makes it require consent, so the
tour _names_ recall and stops; it does not walk the user into a spend on their first minute. The demo does show the
recall beat — it can, because [Z2] exempts it and no real balance exists there ([79.demo-page](79.demo-page.md) beat 6).
That divergence is the single legitimate difference between the two feeds, and it is expressed as a missing union
member rather than as a conditional step.

### Anchors and signals — the tour observes and reports, it never intercepts

Anchors are registered by wrapping an existing child at a composition site, per
[78](78.sequence-engine.md): `pages/universe` wraps the 일기 쓰기 cluster and the `UniverseTimeOverlay`;
`widgets/writing-flow`'s sheet wraps its three inner panels around the shipped `features/write-diary`,
`features/split-diary` / `features/revise-split` and `features/launch-stars` children. A widget is a composition site,
not a product slice, so [78](78.sequence-engine.md) AC 11 holds unchanged: **no `features/*` or `entities/*` slice
imports `@cosimosi/sequence` or `@cosimosi/onboarding`**, and nothing is passed down through the wrapper — no prop, no
flag, no callback.

Signals are reported from those same sites. `reportSequenceSignal(signal: OnboardingSignal): void` pushes
`{ signal, nonce }` into a Zustand channel, the shipped decoupling shape (`useRecallTargetStore` /
`useDeletionTargetStore`, the collection/mirror side of ARCHITECTURE §3.2's state split); `pages/universe` subscribes
and translates a matching
signal into the run machine's echo-guarded `ADVANCE`. The signature is the guard: **it takes one id and returns
nothing**, so a reporting site cannot learn whether a tour is running, cannot read a step index, and cannot branch on
either. The three call sites sit on the writing-flow sheet's existing `OPEN`, `SPLIT_OK` and `LAUNCH_OK` transitions
(`packages/universe/src/writing-flow.machine.ts`), which is why the sheet's own behavior is unchanged with or without a
tour: with no run active the channel write is inert, and [78](78.sequence-engine.md)'s index echo makes a stale or
duplicated report a no-op.

### Exposure ([O3]) — two triggers, no memory

`takeOnboardingStart(): 'signup' | 'replay' | null` is the entire policy, and it is a pure take-once read run by
`pages/universe` on mount:

1. **`signup`** — [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md)'s one-shot completion signal is pending. It is set at the
   instant `SignUp` succeeds, and consumed here.
2. **`replay`** — a request placed by `features/replay-onboarding`.
3. Otherwise `null`, and no tour runs.

**There is no "already seen" record, on the server or the client, and adding one is out of scope by decision.**
[60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md) fixes `users` to nickname/timezone/locale and closes the
`AccountService` RPC inventory, so a durable server fact is
not available; a `localStorage` flag would be per-device, which turns "가입 직후 최초 1회" into "once per browser,
possibly a year later on a new phone" — the wrong behavior, not merely an approximation. The signup signal is already
exactly once-per-account, because [63](63.signup-flow-ui.md)'s profile gate makes `SignUp` reachable only while no
`users` row exists: after it succeeds, the row exists forever and the signal has no second occasion to be set. So the
guarantee [O3] asks for is a property of the trigger, not of a flag that could be wrong.

The consequences are accepted, not overlooked. A reload during the first tour ends it — the signal was consumed and
lives in module memory (registered for reset per [63](63.signup-flow-ui.md)) — and the tour is one tap away in `/me`
forever after ([O5]). "가입 직후 … 우주로 들어가기 전" is read as: the tour's first caption is the first thing rendered
over the universe on the commit after the profile gate opens, not as a blocking interstitial route. A route would
contradict [O2] ("on the real screen") and would have to unmount the canvas that step 1 is about.

### The run host and the outcome ([O2][O4][O5])

`pages/universe` owns the run actor with `useActorRef`, exactly as it already owns `universeNavigationMachine`, and
mounts `widgets/sequence-guide` above the HUD so the caption band, the spotlight and the always-visible skip render
over the live scene without remounting the renderer ([A1]/plan 14). All three of
[78](78.sequence-engine.md)'s outcomes are handled the same way — `completed`, `skipped` ([O4]) and `abandoned` (the
route unmounted) all end with the chrome gone and nothing recorded. The tour deliberately draws **no** distinction
between finishing and skipping: there is no "are you sure", no re-offer, and no follow-up prompt, because replay makes
a mis-skip cheap and a nagging tutorial is worse than a missed one.

Replay is `START` with a fresh run id ([78](78.sequence-engine.md)), so `features/replay-onboarding` needs no teardown:
the row calls `requestOnboardingReplay()` and then the `/me` page's existing exit callback (supplied by the app layer —
[64.my-page](64.my-page.md)'s page imports no router), the universe page mounts, `takeOnboardingStart()` returns
`'replay'`, and the run begins from step 1. A replay over a populated universe behaves identically; step 2 points at
the same affordance and steps 3–5 wait for a genuine second diary, which is a welcome act, not a cost ([G3] earns on
write). If the user does not want to write, the skip is on screen.

### Copy ([O2]) — nine sentences, restrained

Captions resolve through [08.i18n-seam](08.i18n-seam.md) under the `sequence_*` prefix (v2 adds no `onboarding_*`
prefix, and [78](78.sequence-engine.md) assigns onboarding's script copy to `sequence_*`), in a `sequence_tour_*`
sub-namespace naming a UI role: `sequence_tour_welcome_caption`, `_entry_`, `_draft_`, `_proposal_`, `_confirm_`,
`_arrival_`, `_clock_`, `_revisit_`, `_closing_`, plus `sequence_tour_replay_action` and
`sequence_tour_replay_description` for the `/me` row. Both `packages/i18n/messages/en.json` and `ko.json` carry every
key; `pnpm gen:messages` and `pnpm check:gen` gate freshness, `pnpm lint:raw-strings` gates the surfaces. The voice is
the shipped one: unhurried, second-person, no exclamation, no decorative emoji, no translation-ese — an invitation, not
an instruction manual. Mood labels, if a caption ever wants one, come from `packages/emotion`'s `moodLabel`, never new
keys. Although the tour is behind auth, [I12]'s honesty rule is applied to it anyway: it may say the universe is
inspired by engram theory, never that it works like a brain, and it makes no therapeutic claim.

### Golden-parity stance

**This unit defines no pure function over the domain and therefore carries no parity obligation.** Its only logic is a
nine-row table, a take-once read of two booleans, and a string match between a reported signal and the current step's
expected one — no Go counterpart, no fixture. The stronger statement is structural: because the tour performs nothing,
every number the user sees during a run (the split's 2–5 memories, the star's size and brightness, the universe date)
comes from the shipped path that is _already_ golden-pinned against the Go domain
([77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md) enumerates the pinned mirrors).
A drifting duplicate cannot appear here, because the two unions admit no numeric field
and `packages/onboarding`'s dependency list admits no domain-logic package.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The tour runs on [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md)'s engine, unmodified, over the **real** universe and
   the user's own first diary; the only difference from [79.demo-page](79.demo-page.md) is the injected script, and no
   branch, flag or host-kind field is added to `packages/sequence` ([O1]; [79.demo-page](79.demo-page.md)'s isolation
   rule).
2. The tour performs **no** product action: it issues no RPC, writes no read model, seeds no star, advances no clock
   and spends no Twinkle. Every state change during a run is the user's own press through the shipped
   [27.writing-flow-ui](27.writing-flow-ui.md) slices ([O2][I10][I2][G1][U2]).
3. `OnboardingAnchor` and `OnboardingSignal` are closed string-literal unions of exactly five and three members; no
   recall, deletion, clock, palette, ornament, purchase or star member exists, so such a step is a compile error rather
   than a review finding ([G1][I1][I10][I11][I5]).
4. `packages/onboarding` depends only on `@cosimosi/sequence` and `@cosimosi/i18n`; it imports no `@cosimosi/demo`, no
   `@cosimosi/api-client`, no domain-logic package, and none of the universe read-model store symbols, enforced by an
   import rule on the package ([I13][U2]).
5. Anchors are registered only by wrapping existing children at `pages/universe` and `widgets/writing-flow` in both
   apps; **no `features/*` or `entities/*` slice imports `@cosimosi/onboarding` or `@cosimosi/sequence`**, and no prop,
   flag or callback is threaded into any product slice ([I13], [78](78.sequence-engine.md) AC 11).
6. `reportSequenceSignal(signal): void` returns nothing and accepts nothing but a signal id, so no reporting site can
   read whether a tour is running or branch on it; with no run active the report is inert, and a duplicate or stale
   report cannot advance the run ([O2], [78](78.sequence-engine.md) AC 6).
7. The script is the nine steps above in that order, each with an i18n caption accessor that takes **no parameters**,
   so no domain value can appear in a caption and one script is true for both a first-run account and a replay over a
   populated universe ([O1][O2]).
8. Every step that represents user progress advances on a signal, never on a dwell timer; a failed split or launch
   leaves the tour on its current step with no error surface, no timeout and no retry of its own ([O2]).
9. The tour starts on exactly two triggers, each consumed once — [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md)'s
   signup-completion signal, and an explicit replay request — and on no other; entering the universe on any later
   session starts nothing ([O3]).
10. **No "already seen" record exists** on the server or the client: no `users` column, no `AccountService` field, no
    `localStorage` key. "최초 1회만" holds because `SignUp` succeeds at most once per account ([O3];
    [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md)).
11. After signup completes, the tour's first caption is the first thing rendered over the universe, without a blocking
    interstitial route and without remounting the canvas; the shipped first-run welcome and single 일기 쓰기 affordance
    are untouched beneath it ([O3][U2][V7]).
12. A skip is visible on every step and ends the run immediately with no confirmation and no re-offer; `completed`,
    `skipped` and `abandoned` all leave nothing behind ([O4]).
13. `/me`'s profile tab carries the onboarding replay row as its last row; activating it returns the user to the
    universe and starts a fresh run from step 1 with no teardown and no residue from the previous pass ([O5]).
14. `resetOnboardingUserState()` clears the start request and the signal channel and is registered in both apps'
    user-scope reset inventories, so a run and a pending replay cannot cross an account boundary ([U1]).
15. The unit ships on **web and mobile** with no waiver: the script, the unions and the start policy shared verbatim
    from `packages/onboarding`, each app hand-writing its own `ui`, `lint:fsd:layout` R4 clean
    ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4, ARCHITECTURE §3.5).
16. All tour copy resolves through the i18n seam under `sequence_*` in both catalogues in the restrained literary
    voice, with `pnpm lint:raw-strings`, `pnpm check:gen`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm typecheck:mobile`,
    `pnpm test:web` and `pnpm build:web` green and **no** BE change.

## Policy / Values Impact

- **policy / ux:** the exposure and conduct rules land as an `## Onboarding` section appended to
  `spec/policy/ux/sequence.md`, the doc [78.sequence-engine](78.sequence-engine.md) creates — written at
  `/implement-job`; **do not** pre-create or pre-edit it. The rules: the tour runs once, at the moment signup

  named at step 8 and not performed ([G1]); every caption must be true both for an empty first universe and for a
  replay over a full one, which is why no caption quotes a value; a failed split or launch never produces a tour-owned
  error, the tour simply waits.
