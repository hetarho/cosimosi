# 72. decorate usecase

> Decorate — the save that makes 우주 꾸미기 real: one transaction that buys the unowned members of the submitted
> selection and applies the whole selection, priced from the rows it actually inserted, paid in `GENERAL` Twinkle only,
> refused as a whole (naming the short item) when the balance does not cover it. It also retires the shipped per-user
> palette preference: `ornament_selections` becomes the single home of the chosen palette and `palette_preferences` is
> backfilled and dropped. The catalog, the tables and the two reads are [71.ornament-catalog-model](71.ornament-catalog-model.md);
> the panel that calls this is [73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md).
> Scope: BE | Status: planning
>
> Layer U. Home: the `store` context — `apps/api/internal/store` (`service.go`, `ports.go`, `errors.go`, `pg/`, `rpc/`)
> plus `apps/api/cmd/api/store.go`, and the `Decorate` RPC on `cosimosi.store.v1.StoreService`. It fills the write half
> of the store context and leaves every render decision, preview and copy to the panel.

## Purpose

v1 shipped exactly one piece of customization: a per-user palette id in `internal/account`, written on every click by
`packages/emotion/src/preference/change-palette.ts` through `AccountService.SetPalettePreference`, with the color table
itself living entirely in `packages/emotion`. v2 promotes that single preference into three ornament kinds bought with
the product's only currency ([P4] 개정, [P9], [G2a]). [P8] is the whole point of this unit: the floating save button is
**one atomic operation** — "(1) 미소유분 구매 + (2) `OrnamentSelection` 갱신이 **하나의 트랜잭션**으로 처리된다" — and
"잔액 부족이면 저장 불가(어떤 아이템이 모자란지 표시)". Nothing in the shipped system can express that: the palette
write is a single-scalar upsert with no price, no balance check and no notion of ownership.

Two failure modes make this a use-case plan rather than a handler. First, **the charge must be derived from what was
actually acquired, never from what the client says it is buying.** A client-supplied total would let a save buy a
5000-Twinkle background for nothing, and a pre-read of ownership followed by an insert would double-charge two
concurrent saves. So the request carries no amount at all, and the price is summed over the rows the ownership insert
**returned** inside the transaction. Second, **the palette cannot live in two places.** [P10] folds the palette into
`OrnamentKind.PALETTE`, so if `palette_preferences` survives alongside `ornament_selections` the boot gate
(`apps/{web,mobile}/src/app/providers/palette-bootstrap.tsx`) reads one table while the panel writes the other and the
universe flashes the wrong palette. This unit is where that second store is retired, in the same commit that removes
the write RPC that fed it.

The economy side is already built and is only consumed here: the append-only ledger, the balance derivation and the
tier rules are [43.stardust-ledger](43.stardust-ledger.md) / [44.earn-spend-usecase](44.earn-spend-usecase.md), and
`ornament_purchase` joins the closed `EntryReason` set as a `GENERAL`-only spend in
[66.earn-and-purchase-spend](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md) ([G7] one ledger, [G4] a fourth 소모처). This unit adds no
reason, no ledger column and no currency.

## Scope / Non-goals

**In scope**

- **`store.Service.Decorate(ctx, scope, selection)`** — validate, buy, apply, record, in one transaction over `store`'s
  own tables; the complete selection is submitted every time and the result is the confirmed selection plus the amount
  actually spent ([P8]).
- **The single cross-context edge** — a consumer-owned `store.SpendGate` bound at `apps/api/cmd/api/store.go` onto
  `twinkle.Service.CheckAndSpend`, carrying `store`'s open transaction so the purchase and the debit commit or roll back
  together ([P8][G7]).
- **`GENERAL`-only payment** ([P9]) — `store`'s spend port has no tier field, so a `SMALL` draw is not expressible
  here; the tier rule itself is enforced by plan 65's `PlanSpend(kind)` and plan 66's ledger `CHECK`.
- **A refusal that names the item** ([P8]) — a new `STORE_INSUFFICIENT_TWINKLE` reason carrying the blocking ornament id
  and the shortfall as error metadata, plus its i18n fallback copy.
- **[P11] achievement-only ornaments are unbuyable** — a distinct refusal when an unowned achievement-granted ornament
  appears in a save; an already-owned one is free to select.
- **The palette migration** ([P10]) — backfill `palette_preferences` → `ornament_selections(kind='PALETTE')`,
  `DROP TABLE palette_preferences`, delete `apps/api/db/queries/account/palette_preference.sql`, remove
  `account.v1.SetPalettePreference` from the wire, and re-point `account.v1.GetPalettePreference`† at `store`'s
  published selection read through a consumer-owned `account.OrnamentSelectionReader` port.
- **Achievement progress facts** ([A2] 꾸미기 · 첫 경험) — a consumer-owned counter-only `store.AchievementRecorder`
  called inside the same transaction, with a shipped no-op default; plan 75 binds the concrete.
- Per-user scoping on every statement (`pnpm lint:persistence`, [U1]).

**Non-goals**

- **No table, no column, no catalog.** `ornament_ownerships`, `ornament_selections`, the `Ornament`/`OrnamentKind`/
  `StarVariant` models, every price and every acquisition path are [71.ornament-catalog-model](71.ornament-catalog-model.md)
  (catalogs are Go tables in code; 71 is the sole owner of the `store` DDL).
- **No `GetCatalog` / `GetSelection`.** The two `NO_SIDE_EFFECTS` reads and their `owned`/`selected` flags are plan 71.
  This unit adds exactly one RPC to `StoreService` and no fourth one.
- **No `EntryReason`, no ledger DDL, no balance math.** `ornament_purchase`, the `from_basic = 0` `CHECK`, the
  tier-aware `PlanSpend` and `QuoteSpend`'s purchase shortfall are [65.twinkle-kind-redefinition](65.twinkle-kind-redefinition.md)
  / [66.earn-and-purchase-spend](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md).
- **No panel, no preview, no save button.** The over-universe sheet, the in-memory preview store, the revert-on-close
  boundary, the unowned-total display and the item-specific refusal copy are
  [73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md). This unit ships no UI and no XState machine.
- **No achievement definitions, conditions, rewards or claim.** [74.achievement-catalog-model](74.achievement-catalog-model.md)
  / [75.achievement-tracking-usecase](75.achievement-tracking-usecase.md); this unit only emits counter deltas.
- **No render change.** Applying a selection to the universe (`applyPalette`, `setSkinKey`, the star-variant remount)
  is 71/73; `store` returns ids and never a color, size, brightness or coordinate ([I11][V10]).
- **No refund, expiry or re-purchase** ([P9]) — the transaction surface exposes no delete of `ornament_ownerships`.
- **No payment.** Twinkle is the only payment method ([P9]); the store-receipt path is removed in v2 and returns
  in v3.

## Design

### Placement (domain-first Go — ARCHITECTURE §2.2/§2.3)

`Decorate` is an application use-case on `store.Service`, in `apps/api/internal/store/service.go`, beside plan 71's
catalog reads. New files this unit adds: `apps/api/db/queries/store/decorate.sql` (a new file, so it never edits plan
71's query file), the `Decorate` method on `store/pg/store.go`, the `Decorate` handler on `store/rpc/server.go`, its
reason constants in `store/rpc/reasons.go`, and the spend/recorder adapters in `apps/api/cmd/api/store.go`. No
`domain/` or `app/` package is created (§2.9 #2).

`store` imports no other context. The two edges it needs are consumer-owned ports in `store/ports.go`, bound at the
composition root, following `cmd/api/twinkle.go`'s shipped comment — "twinkle never imports memory and memory never
imports twinkle — the two meet only here… they share the transaction, never the queries":

```go
// store/ports.go
type EconomyTx any                      // opaque; only the composition root knows the concrete

type PurchaseSpend struct {             // no Kind, no tier, no reason, no price curve
    Amount   int
    DedupKey string
}

type SpendGate interface {              // consumer-owned; the concrete is twinkle.Service
    CheckAndSpend(ctx context.Context, scope platform.UserScope, tx EconomyTx, spend PurchaseSpend) error
}

type AchievementRecorder interface {    // counters only
    RecordProgress(ctx context.Context, scope platform.UserScope, tx EconomyTx, counterKey string, delta int) error
}
type NoAchievementRecorder struct{}     // shipped no-op default (tests, economy-less roots)
```

`PurchaseSpend` deliberately has **no kind and no tier field**: `store` cannot ask for a `SMALL` draw and cannot claim
to be a recall, so [P9] holds at the type level before plan 65's `PlanSpend(kind)` and plan 66's `CHECK` ever run.
`storepg.Store` exposes `DB() dbgen.DBTX` (the `memory/pg` accessor pattern) so `cmd/api/store.go` can bind
`twinklepg.NewStore(carrier.DB())` onto the same pgx transaction.

### The `Decorate` transaction ([P8])

`Decorate(ctx, scope, selection Selection) (Selection, int, error)` where `Selection` holds one optional
`OrnamentID` per `OrnamentKind` and an empty id means "the free default for that kind". The submitted selection is
always **complete** — `Decorate` is a full-state write, not a patch — which is what makes "half-applied selection"
unrepresentable and matches the panel's revert-to-confirmed model ([73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md)).

1. **Validate, before any write.** Every non-empty id must resolve in plan 71's in-code catalog **and** its kind must
   equal the field it arrived on. An unknown id or a kind mismatch is `ErrOrnamentUnknown`.
2. **Open one `store` transaction** (`InDecorateTx`). Its surface exposes the ownership insert, the selection
   upsert/delete and nothing else — no ownership delete, no catalog write, no ledger query.
3. **Classify.** `ListOwnedOrnamentIDs(user_id, ids)` reads ownership for exactly the submitted ids. An unowned
   **achievement-only** ornament is refused here (`ErrOrnamentNotPurchasable`, [P11]); an owned one is free to select.
   This read classifies and refuses — it never prices.
4. **Acquire.** One `INSERT INTO ornament_ownerships … ON CONFLICT (user_id, ornament_id) DO NOTHING RETURNING
ornament_id` over the unowned purchasable candidates. The **returned rows are the authoritative newly-acquired
   set**: two concurrent identical saves serialize on that unique index, and the loser inserts zero rows and is charged
   zero. This is why the charge is derived from `RETURNING` and not from step 3.
5. **Price.** `chargeTotal` = sum of the catalog prices of the returned rows. Zero returned rows ⇒ zero charge and the
   spend leg is skipped entirely, so re-selecting things you already own writes no ledger row ([P8]).
6. **Spend** (only when `chargeTotal > 0`) — `SpendGate.CheckAndSpend(ctx, scope, tx, PurchaseSpend{…})`. A refusal
   aborts the transaction: no ownership row, no selection change, nothing partially charged ([P8] 저장 불가).
7. **Apply.** Per kind: upsert `ornament_selections(user_id, kind, ornament_id)` for a non-empty id, **delete** the row
   for an empty one. Absence is the single representation of the free default, so a row can never hold the
   default id and the two representations cannot drift apart.
8. **Record** the achievement counter facts (below), still inside the transaction.
9. **Return** the confirmed selection and `chargeTotal`.

**Idempotency.** The purchase's ledger dedup key is `"ornament_purchase:" + hex(sha256(<length-prefixed sorted newly
acquired ornament ids>))`, mirroring `spendDedupKey` in `cmd/api/twinkle.go`. It is single-use _by construction_:
ownership is permanent and never refunded ([P9]), so the same set can never be newly acquired twice. A retried
transaction folds onto the same key; a later purchase of a different item hashes differently.

### The spend leg — the only cross-context edge

`cmd/api/store.go` binds a `storeSpendGate` that maps `store.PurchaseSpend` onto plan 66's purchase intent
(`twinkle.SpendIntent` with `Reason: ornament_purchase` and a caller-supplied amount — the one spend reason whose
amount is not derived from a cost curve, because only the catalog knows an ornament's price) and translates
`twinkle.ErrInsufficientTwinkle` back into `store`'s vocabulary. It resolves the ledger store from the `EconomyTx`
carrier exactly as `economyLedger` does today. Consequences pinned here so no sibling re-decides them:

- **One ledger row per save**, amount = `chargeTotal`, not one row per ornament. `twinkle_ledger_entries` carries no
  item reference and plan 66 owns its DDL, so the /me history renders a purchase as a single 꾸미기 line
  ([67.twinkle-relabel-and-ledger-ui](67.twinkle-relabel-and-ledger-ui.md) must not expect per-item rows).
- **`store` never reads a balance.** No `DecorateResponse` field carries one; the panel invalidates the shipped
  `GetBalance` query after a successful save (invalidation only, ARCHITECTURE §3.2).
- **`twinkle` gains no knowledge of ornaments** — it receives an amount and a dedup key.

### The refusal that names the item ([P8])

`ErrInsufficientTwinkle` in `store` maps to a **new** reason `STORE_INSUFFICIENT_TWINKLE` (`ResourceExhausted`),
distinct from `TWINKLE_INSUFFICIENT`, whose copy cannot name an item. The handler attaches
`apperr.Domain(..., map[string]string{"ornament_id": …, "shortfall": …})` — the shipped metadata channel
(`apperr.Domain`, surfaced as `AppError.metadata` in `packages/errors/src/core.ts`), so no new transport mechanism is
introduced.

Which item is named: sort the newly-acquired set by price ascending, tie-broken by ornament id, and fill against the
covered amount; the **first item that does not fit** is `ornament_id`. Deterministic, and with at most three kinds it is
in practice the single item the user cannot afford. The ordering is **BE-only** — it is a refusal detail, not shared
math (see the parity stance).

Copy: the generic toast keeps a non-parameterised fallback, `error_store_insufficient` —
ko `"별가루가 모자라 저장하지 못했어요."` / en `"Not enough stardust — nothing was saved."` (it states the atomicity, which
is the honest thing to say). The **item-specific** message and the row it points at are rendered by
[73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md) from the metadata, because only the panel can resolve an id to a display
name and only it has a row to point at — so `reasonMessage()` in `packages/errors/src/presentation.ts` is not widened.

Full reason set added to `store/rpc/reasons.go` and `packages/errors/src/core.ts`: `STORE_INSUFFICIENT_TWINKLE`
(`ResourceExhausted`), `STORE_ORNAMENT_UNKNOWN` (`InvalidArgument`), `STORE_ORNAMENT_NOT_PURCHASABLE`
(`FailedPrecondition`), `STORE_SCOPE_REQUIRED` (`Unauthenticated`).

### One home for the chosen palette ([P10])

Migration **`00021_ornament_selection_palette_migration.sql`** (claimed in plan order from `00016` —
[00.overview](00.overview.md) §5.6; ahead of it plans 60, 62, 66, 68 and 71 each carry one), both directions:

- **Up** — `INSERT INTO ornament_selections (user_id, kind, ornament_id) SELECT user_id, 'PALETTE', palette_id FROM
palette_preferences WHERE palette_id <> '<default id>' ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, then `DROP TABLE
palette_preferences`. Rows holding the default id are **not** copied — absence is the default — and the copy is
  otherwise unfiltered: an id retired from the registry coerces to the default at read time, exactly as it does today,
  so no registry list is hardcoded in SQL. The PALETTE ornament's id **is** the `packages/emotion` registry palette id
  verbatim, which is what makes this a straight column copy and keeps `packages/emotion/src/registry.ts` the sole
  color-table authority ([I11]).
- **Down** — recreate `palette_preferences` and copy the `PALETTE` selections back.
- Dropping in one migration is safe: `.github/workflows/deploy-backend.yml` stops `api` and `worker` **before** goose
  runs and starts the new image after, so no binary is ever live against the dropped table.

  rollback-to-confirmed, and its tests. **Renaming it and flipping write-on-change to preview-then-save is
  [73.decoration-panel](73.decoration-panel.md)'s job**; this unit only changes which RPC it calls, so the
  tree is green at every commit.

### Achievement progress facts ([A2])

`Decorate` calls `AchievementRecorder.RecordProgress` inside its transaction, with counter keys declared as string
constants in `internal/store` (the port takes a plain `string`, so neither context imports the other):

The keys themselves — their spelling, their accumulate/reach mode and their membership in the closed set — are
**owned by [74.achievement-catalog-model](74.achievement-catalog-model.md)**; this unit only emits them:

| counter key (74)                 | mode                           | delta                                          | the [A2] fact it feeds         |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `decoration_saved`               | accumulate                     | 1, when the save changed anything              | 첫 경험 · 첫 꾸미기 저장       |
| `ornament_owned`                 | reach                          | the ownership count after the save             | 꾸미기 · 오너먼트 N종 보유     |
| `ornament_kind_decorated:<KIND>` | accumulate (family, 3 members) | 1, when that kind's stored id actually changed | 꾸미기 · 종류별 전부 교체 경험 |

A save that changes nothing records nothing. The family member is built from the `OrnamentKind` enum, never from user
input, and the distinct-kind count `ornament_kind_variety` is derived by `achievement` from first touch of a family
member — `store` never computes a set size (74). Plan 74 may only gate a family key at `≥ 1`, since a
toggle-back-and-forth would otherwise be farmable and a repetition-inducing condition is forbidden outright ([A1]). No memory id, strength, mood or text can ride along — the port has no field for
one, which is how [A6]/[I11] holds structurally. The no-op default ships here for tests and economy-less roots; plan 75
binds the concrete and makes production fail to boot unbound. An ornament granted **by** an achievement is
recorded by plan 75 at its own grant leg, not here.

### RPC contract (`cosimosi.store.v1.StoreService`, Connect unary — §2.7)

```proto
// Buy the unowned members of the submitted selection and apply the selection, atomically ([P8]).
// Mutates ownership, selection and the Twinkle ledger — NOT NO_SIDE_EFFECTS.
rpc Decorate(DecorateRequest) returns (DecorateResponse);

// The complete selection, one field per OrnamentKind. An empty id means the kind's free default
// ([P10]). Three named fields rather than a repeated/map shape: a duplicate kind and an unknown
// kind are unrepresentable. There is deliberately no user_id field and no price, amount
// or total field — the charge is derived server-side from the rows actually acquired.
message DecorateRequest {
  string background_ornament_id   = 1;
  string star_shader_ornament_id  = 2;
  string palette_ornament_id      = 3;
}

// selection reuses plan 71's OrnamentSelection message so the client renders exactly one selection
// shape; spent_twinkle is what this save charged (0 for a free re-select).
message DecorateResponse {
  repeated OrnamentSelection selection = 1;
  int64 spent_twinkle = 2;
}
```

The handler is thin (§2.9 #7): map proto → `store.Selection`, call `Decorate`, map the domain error to a reason +
metadata. `Decorate` is a mutation, so it needs no `packages/client-cache/src/http-policy.ts` entry; `GetCatalog`/
`GetSelection` classifications are plan 71's. `store` remains free of any `Equip`, `ListInventory` or `ListOwned…`
message ([P7]).

### Golden-parity stance

**No pure function in this unit is mirrored TS↔Go, and none may be.** The only number the client shows is the unowned
total on the save button — a display sum over plan 71's price mirror, advisory by design: `Decorate` re-derives the
total server-side from the rows its own insert returned, and the request has no amount field, so a stale, wrong or
tampered client total can never change what is charged. The cheapest-first affordability ordering that names the
blocking item is a BE-only refusal detail. This unit is headless and therefore carries no parity question
([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4); the balance and cost math it leans on is already golden-parity-pinned by
[43.stardust-ledger](43.stardust-ledger.md).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `store.Service.Decorate` inserts the missing `ornament_ownerships` rows, upserts/deletes all three
   `ornament_selections` rows and debits Twinkle in **one transaction**; a failure at any step leaves no ownership row,
   no selection change and no ledger row. ([P8])
2. The charged amount is the sum of the catalog prices of the ornaments the ownership insert **returned** inside that
   transaction. Two concurrent identical saves charge the full price exactly once and zero the second time, and
   `DecorateRequest` carries **no** amount, price or total field. ([P8][G7])
3. Already-owned members of the selection cost nothing and re-selecting them writes **no** ledger row; a save that
   changes nothing is a successful no-op. ([P8])
4. A purchase is paid from `GENERAL` only: `store`'s spend port has no tier field, `PlanSpend` returns
   `FromSmall: 0` for the purchase kind, and the ledger `CHECK` rejects `ornament_purchase` with a non-zero small draw.
   ([P9][G2a])
5. Insufficient balance **refuses the whole save** with `STORE_INSUFFICIENT_TWINKLE` — a reason distinct from
   `TWINKLE_INSUFFICIENT` — carrying the blocking `ornament_id` and the `shortfall` as error metadata; nothing is
   bought, nothing is applied, nothing is deleted. ([P8][I1])
6. An **unowned achievement-only** ornament in a save is refused with `STORE_ORNAMENT_NOT_PURCHASABLE` and is never
   purchasable at any price; the same ornament, once owned, is free to select. ([P11])
7. An unknown ornament id, or an id whose kind does not match the field it arrived on, is refused with
   `STORE_ORNAMENT_UNKNOWN` before any write. ([P8])
8. A purchase writes exactly **one** `ornament_purchase` earn/spend row per save, amount = the charged total, into the
   one append-only ledger; the balance stays derived from that ledger. ([G4][G7])
9. The purchase spend is idempotent: a replay of the same save charges once, keyed by the newly-acquired ornament set,
   and no ownership is ever refunded, expired or re-purchased (the transaction surface exposes no ownership delete).
   ([P9])
10. After the migration, `ornament_selections` is the **only** store of the chosen palette: `palette_preferences` is
    gone, its queries and its `account` read/write code are gone, and a user who had chosen a non-default palette in v1
    still sees that palette after deploy. ([P10])
11. `account.v1.SetPalettePreference` no longer exists on the wire; `account.v1.GetPalettePreference`† is unchanged for
    clients and is served by a read-through onto `store`'s published selection read. The shipped palette boot gate and
    its cache classification are untouched. ([P10])
12. `account` contains no palette id, no default palette id and no registry allow-list after this unit; the
    color table remains solely `packages/emotion/src/registry.ts` and an `Ornament` row carries an id, never a color.
    ([I11][V10])
13. The free default palette costs nothing and requires no purchase and no ownership row: an absent
    `ornament_selections` row **is** the default, and a selection row can never hold the default id. ([P10])
14. `Decorate` reports achievement progress through a counter-only port whose payload is `(scope, tx, counterKey,
delta)` — no memory id, strength, mood or text field exists on it — inside the same transaction, with a shipped
    no-op default. ([A2][A6][I11])
15. `store` imports no other context and no other context imports `store`; the spend gate, the achievement recorder and
    the decorate store are consumer-owned interfaces bound in `apps/api/cmd/api/store.go`; the RPC handler contains no
    policy. (§2.2/§2.4/§2.9 #6/#7)
16. Every new statement is scoped to the authenticated user, `pnpm lint:persistence` passes with no new
    `globalQueries`/`platformTables` entry, and no request message in `cosimosi.store.v1` carries a `user_id`.
    ([U1])
