# 61. signup and invite usecase

> Signup & invite use-cases — the account context's write path: `SignUp` (nickname-only input, client-detected
> timezone, link-bound invite binding) and the **deferred settlement**
> that pays the invite reward and the one-time signup bonus only once the new user has actually launched a star. It owns
> the [G6] abuse gate — what "a valid signup" concretely means and when the reward settles — and it turns on the invite
> earn path that has been fail-closed since plan 44.
> Scope: BE | Status: planning
>
> Layer U. Home: the existing `apps/api/internal/account` context — use-cases in `account/service.go` (the file
> [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md) splits out of the shipped `account.go`), invite
> queries in a new `apps/api/db/queries/account/invites.sql`, RPCs on `cosimosi.account.v1.AccountService`. It fills the
> shipped `twinkle.InviteResolver` seam with a real resolver and leaves the profile/timezone _model_ to
> [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md) and every signup screen to
> [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md).

## Purpose

v1 ships an authenticated universe with no account of its own. Identity is entirely Supabase: `platform.AuthInterceptor`
→ `SupabaseJWTVerifier` → `UserIdentity{UserID}`, and the only per-user row the product owns is a palette preference.
There is no nickname, no timezone, and no way to enter through a friend's link. [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md)
introduces the `users` / `auth_providers` / `invites` tables; **this unit is the write path that fills them** — the once-
per-account `SignUp` ([U6][U7]), the link-bound invite ([U8]), and the moment a reward becomes real.

The second gap is economic. The invite earn path exists in code and **does not work**: `cmd/api/twinkle.go` binds
`twinkle.UnavailableInviteResolver{}`, so `ClaimInvite` returns `ErrInviteResolutionUnavailable` for every caller. Plan
44 deliberately left that fail-closed and reserved [G6] behind the resolver seam. With payment deferred to v3 (PRD §8.3),
[G3]'s remaining supply is the daily grant, writing, achievements, invites, and the signup bonus — so the invite path
must be turned on, and it must be turned on **gated**. The arithmetic of leaving it ungated is explicit with the shipped
numbers: `earn_invite_inviter = 500` + `earn_invite_invitee = 500` + `earn_write = 100` + a signup bonus, for one
throwaway email, against a signup that asks for a nickname and nothing else ([U6]).

So this unit answers one product question with a structure: **when has a signup earned its reward?** The answer is a
four-part conjunction, evaluated at settlement rather than at signup, and settlement is deferred to the
invitee's **first star launch** — which forces an LLM `Encode` round-trip per fake account and is the real cost floor
([G6][I10]). `SignUp` itself binds and pays nothing; the one-time signup bonus ([U11]) sits behind the same gate, or it
would be farmable independently of the invite check.

## Scope / Non-goals

**In scope**

- **`SignUp` use-case + RPC** ([U6][U7][U8]) — the once-per-account first write: validated nickname (the only user
  input), the client-detected IANA timezone, the negotiated locale, the initial `auth_providers` row read from the
  directory, and — when present — the invite-link token. Idempotent: a replay returns the existing profile and
  re-binds nothing.
- **`AcceptInvite`** ([U8]) — invite binding as an **internal use-case with no RPC of its own**, invoked only by
  `SignUp`. Sets `invites.invitee_user_id` + `bound_at`; pays nothing. Best-effort: an unknown, expired, self-, or
  withdrawn-inviter token binds nothing and never fails the signup.
- **The bind-time `invites` row** — `AcceptInvite` writes it from the token it verifies. Issuance itself
  (`GetInviteLink†`, the HMAC-signed stateless token, `account.VerifyInviteToken`, `account.InviteSigner` and
  `account.invite_link_ttl_days`) is **owned by [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md)**; this unit
  consumes the verifier and writes the row.
- **The [G6] valid-signup gate** — the concrete `twinkle.InviteResolver` at `cmd/api/account.go`, backed by
  `account.Service.ResolveInviteSettlement`: the four-part conjunction (distinct live inviter · ≥1 launched star ·
  email-verified invitee · per-inviter rewarded cap), plus the `invites.rewarded_at` bookkeeping mark.
- **Deferred settlement** ([U11][G3][G6]) — a new consumer-owned `memory.SignupSettlementPort` fired from
  `PersistEncoded` after the launch commits, whose concrete drives `account.Service.SettleSignup`: the invite reward
  through the shipped `twinkle.Service.ClaimInvite`, and the signup bonus through a new `twinkle.Service.EarnSignupBonus`
  keyed `signup_bonus:<userID>`.
- **`twinkle.ReasonSignupBonus`** — the one `EntryReason` constant [U11] forces, plus its dedup key. No ledger DDL
  change (see the reason-set handling in _Design_).
- Per-user scoping on every statement, and the two invitee-side statements the isolation gate cannot express
  conjunctively, allowlisted with stated reasons.

**Non-goals**

- **No `users` / `auth_providers` / `invites` DDL, no profile reads, no `UpdateProfile`.** The three tables, migration
  `00016`, the `User`/`AuthProvider`/`Invite` types, `GetProfile`, `ListAuthProviders`, `GetInviteLink†` and the whole
  invite-token model, the promoted Supabase directory adapter, and the nickname/timezone/locale _model_ belong to
  [60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md), the sole owner of those tables and of the account read
  surface ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §5.6).
- **No withdrawal, restore, or withdrawn-scope interceptor** — [62.withdrawal-usecase](62.withdrawal-usecase.md)
  owns all of it. This unit only _reads_ `users.deleted_at` to make a withdrawn inviter's link inert.
- **No UI.** Signup/sign-in surfaces, the nickname step, invite-link capture across the OAuth redirect, and the URL
  shape of the link are [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md); the `/me` profile tab is
  [64.my-page](64.my-page.md). No i18n key is added here.
- **No tier rename, no reason-set expansion, no ledger DDL.** `SMALL`/`GENERAL` is
  [65.twinkle-kind-redefinition](65.twinkle-kind-redefinition.md); the full v2 `EntryReason` set (including splitting
  `invite_signup` out of `invite`), the `ornament_purchase` CHECK, and `GetLedger` are
  [66.earn-and-purchase-spend](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md).
- **No achievement recording.** The `account` invite-settlement recorder call site belongs to
  [75.achievement-tracking-usecase](75.achievement-tracking-usecase.md); that plan declares the port in
  `account/ports.go` and adds the call inside `SettleSignup`. This unit declares no counter port.
- **No provider linking after signup.** `SignUp` seeds the first `auth_providers` row; reconciling a second linked
  provider is a read-through concern of plan 60's `ListAuthProviders` ([U5] is retroactive documentation, not new
  behavior).
- **No reward reversal, ever.** Withdrawal does not claw back a settled invite ([62.withdrawal-usecase](62.withdrawal-usecase.md)); the ledger is
  append-only ([I1][G7]).

## Design

### Placement (domain-first Go, §2.2/§2.3)

Everything lands in the **existing** `apps/api/internal/account` package — `SignUp`, `AcceptInvite`,
`ResolveInviteSettlement`, `SettleSignup` as methods on `account.Service`; consumer-owned ports in a new
`account/ports.go`; sqlc mapping in `account/pg/store.go`; thin handlers in `account/rpc/server.go`. The context stays
one package (§2.3) — the `{app,pg,rpc}` split is not earned by five use-cases. `account` imports neither `memory` nor
`twinkle`; `cmd/api/account.go` grows the adapters.

`account.NewService(store)` becomes `account.NewService(account.ServiceDeps{...})` (the `twinkle.ServiceDeps` /
`admin.ServiceDeps` form, since there is now more than one dependency): the preference store, the invite store, the
directory port, the two granter ports, and an id minter. **Queries** go in a new `apps/api/db/queries/account/invites.sql`
(the directory name must equal the context name — `check-persistence-isolation.mjs` keys its allowlists on
`<dir>/<file>#<sqlcName>`), leaving plan 60's user/provider queries in their own file. **This unit adds no migration**;
`00016` is plan 60's.

### `SignUp` — one field, one row, no money ([U6][U7][U11])

`SignUp(ctx, scope, in SignUpInput) (Profile, bool /*inviteBound*/, error)` with
`SignUpInput{Nickname, Timezone, Locale, InviteToken}`:

1. **Nickname** — trim, then require a rune length within `account.nickname_min_length`..`account.nickname_max_length`
   and no control characters or line breaks. **Nicknames are not unique** — they are display copy, and a uniqueness
   check would put a second field ("that one is taken") into a one-field signup ([U6]). Violations →
   `ACCOUNT_NICKNAME_INVALID`.
2. **Timezone** — must resolve as an IANA location. An unresolvable zone is **rejected** (`ACCOUNT_TIMEZONE_INVALID`)
   rather than silently defaulting to UTC: the zone is the authority for three boundaries ([U7]), and a silent
   default breaks all three invisibly. The client detects it and falls back to `UTC` explicitly, so the server never
   needs to guess. Resolution requires the IANA database inside a `distroless/static` image, so this unit adds the
   blank `import _ "time/tzdata"` to the api entrypoint — a one-line embed, idempotent if plan 60 landed it first.
3. **Locale** — an unknown or empty tag **coerces** to the default (`en`) instead of failing: a locale is a
   preference, not a boundary.
4. **Provider** — the initial `auth_providers` row records what the **directory** reports for this user id, never a
   client-supplied claim (a wire-supplied provider is spoofable, and `platform.UserIdentity` carries only the id).
   A directory failure is not fatal: the row is left for plan 60's read-through to reconcile.
5. **Insert** — `INSERT INTO users … ON CONFLICT (user_id) DO NOTHING`, then read back. So `SignUp` is **idempotent**:
   a concurrent or retried call returns the existing profile, does not overwrite a nickname, and — critically — does
   **not** re-run step 6.
6. **Bind the invite** (only when the row was actually created, and only when `InviteToken` is non-empty) — `AcceptInvite`.

There is no step 7: the caller's own link needs no row, because [60](60.account-profile-model.md)'s `GetInviteLink†`
derives a signed token from the scope and touches no table.

**Structural guard for [U11]:** `account.SignUpInput` has no amount, `SignUpResponse` has no balance field, and the
`Service` reachable from the RPC handler holds the granter ports only behind `SettleSignup`. A signup literally cannot
credit anything.

An authenticated user with no `users` row is the pre-signup state: every other account RPC answers
`ACCOUNT_SIGNUP_REQUIRED` (FailedPrecondition), which is [63.signup-flow-ui](63.signup-flow-ui.md)'s cue to show the
nickname step.

### `AcceptInvite` — link-bound, with no wire surface ([U8])

`AcceptInvite` is a **use-case with no RPC**. There is no `AcceptInviteRequest`, and the only field on the whole account
surface that accepts a token is `SignUpRequest.invite_token`, which the link carries. [U8]'s "초대 코드를 사용자가
입력하지 않는다" is therefore unexpressible rather than merely discouraged — the v2 style of enforcing a rule by the
absence of a message rather than by copy review.

Binding follows [60](60.account-profile-model.md)'s token model: `account.VerifyInviteToken(token)` recomputes the MAC
and checks `issuedAt + account.invite_link_ttl_days ≥ now`, yielding the inviter id **out of the verified payload** —
so there is no cross-user `SELECT … WHERE token = $1`. The bind is then one **INSERT** of the row 60's DDL pins
(`id`, `user_id` = the verified inviter, `invitee_user_id`, `token`, `created_at` = the payload's `issuedAt`,
`bound_at` = now), guarded by `UNIQUE (token)` (single use) and `UNIQUE (invitee_user_id)` (an invitee binds at most
once, ever). A failed verification, a self-invite, a withdrawn inviter (`users.deleted_at IS NOT NULL`) or a lost
uniqueness race → **not an error**: `SignUp` succeeds and reports `invite_bound = false`. A pending link from a
withdrawn inviter is **inert** — it binds nothing, so it can never settle.

### Issuance is not this unit's — `GetInviteLink†` is owned by [60](60.account-profile-model.md)

Each `Invite` row is a **single-use, expiring link**: the pinned row shape carries one `invitee_user_id` and one
`bound_at`, so one token reaches one friend. But the token is a stateless HMAC capability derived from the
caller's scope, so issuing one writes nothing — which is exactly why `GetInviteLink` stays `NO_SIDE_EFFECTS`
and why this unit adds neither the RPC nor a mint query. The row is born at **bind** time, above.

### The [G6] gate: what a valid signup is

`account.Service.ResolveInviteSettlement(ctx, in InviteSettlementRequest{Token, InviteeUserID}) (SettledInvite, error)`
is the whole gate. All four must hold, evaluated **in this order** so a permanently-failing invite never makes a
network call:

1. **Distinct, live inviter** — `invites.user_id != invitee`, and the inviter's `users.deleted_at IS NULL`. (SQL)
2. **≥1 launched star** — satisfied by construction: the only caller is the launch hook. This is why the condition is a
   _count of `episodic_memories`_, not of `diaries` — [T1] lets a past-dated diary exist with no star, so a diary count
   is farmable with zero engagement, while a launched star forces an LLM `Encode` round-trip.
3. **Per-inviter rewarded cap** — `COUNT(invites WHERE user_id = inviter AND rewarded_at IS NOT NULL) <
values.TwinkleInviteRewardMaxPerInviter`, lifetime. (SQL)
4. **Email-verified invitee** — the directory port reports Supabase `email_confirmed_at`; a linked `GOOGLE`
   `auth_providers` row also satisfies it — verification is implicit for `GOOGLE`. (network)

Failing 1 or 3 is permanent and costs one indexed read. Failing 4 is transient by design — the invitee may verify
later, and the next launch re-evaluates. Failing any of them means **neither side is rewarded**; the invitee still
receives the signup bonus, so their onboarding is never empty.

`SettledInvite{InviteID, InviterUserID, InviteeUserID}` maps at `cmd/api/account.go` onto
`twinkle.ResolvedSignup{SignupID: InviteID, …}` — **`SignupID` is `invites.id`**, so one invite row is one settlement and
one pair of dedup keys.

### Deferred settlement: the trigger, the transaction boundary, the keys

**The trigger** is a new consumer-owned port declared by its caller, `internal/memory` (§2.4), beside `EarnPort`:

```go
// SignupSettlementPort is the deferred account-settlement seam ([U11][G6]): fired by
// PersistEncoded AFTER the launch transaction commits, once per launched diary, and only
// when the monotonic guard admitted the diary ([I10]). It carries the scope and nothing
// else — no diary id, no memory, no count — so a settlement can neither read nor alter
// the meaning layer ([I11]), and it returns nothing, so it can never fail a committed launch.
type SignupSettlementPort interface {
    OnStarsLaunched(ctx context.Context, scope platform.UserScope)
}
type NoSignupSettlement struct{} // tests and economy-less roots
```

The **no-error, no-payload signature** is the structural guard: memory cannot make the launch depend on settlement, and
settlement cannot see what was written. The composition root owns error handling (log + observability); production
**fails to boot** with the port unbound, mirroring `TestProductionTwinkleExternalEarnsFailClosedWithoutAdapters` —
`memory.NoEarnOnWrite`'s silent-nothing default is deliberately not copied for a reward path.

**Transaction boundary.** Settlement is an **idempotent pairing**, not a shared transaction: `invites` belongs
to `account` and the ledger belongs to `twinkle`, so neither may own the other's tables, and the pairing is the
sanctioned shape for exactly that case (the shipped `admin.GrantStardust` pairing is the precedent). It also keeps the directory HTTP call out of any database
transaction. `account.Service.SettleSignup(ctx, scope)`:

1. Probe: the one bound, unrewarded invite for this invitee (at most one row).
2. If present, call `account.InviteRewardGranter.Grant(ctx, inviteeUserID, token)`. Its concrete at
   `cmd/api/account.go` is a one-line adapter over the shipped `twinkle.Service.ClaimInvite(inviteeScope, token)` —
   translation only, no orchestration. Twinkle re-enters `account` through the resolver **before** it opens its ledger
   transaction (the shipped `ClaimInvite` calls `Resolve` first, then `InLedgerTx`), so the re-entry is a plain
   completed read, never a nested transaction.
3. On success, stamp `invites.rewarded_at`.
4. Unconditionally, call `account.SignupBonusGranter.Grant(ctx, scope)` → `twinkle.Service.EarnSignupBonus`.

**Credits first, mark second** — the reverse of `admin.GrantStardust`'s "records first, credits second", and
deliberately so: twinkle's `UNIQUE (user_id, dedup_key)` is the real lock and `invites.id` is known before crediting, so
nothing needs minting inside the marking write. A crash between 2 and 3 leaves `rewarded_at` NULL with the credits
already written; the next launch re-probes, the dedup keys make both legs no-ops, and the mark lands. The failure mode
is a repeated no-op, never a lost or doubled reward.

**Dedup keys** (all shipped shapes, drawn from the closed `EntryReason` set [66](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md) owns): `invite:<inviteID>` (inviter), `invite_signup:<inviteID>` (invitee),
`signup_bonus:<userID>` (bonus). The steady-state cost of the hook once everything is settled is one indexed read that
returns nothing plus one insert that conflicts — negligible beside the launch it follows.

### Reason set: the one constant this unit adds

[66.earn-and-purchase-spend](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md) is the sole owner of `twinkle.EntryReason` and of every
ledger DDL change ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §5.6), but the pinned sprint order puts 61 **before** 66 and [U11] is
61's requirement. Resolved narrowly: this unit adds **exactly one**
constant, `twinkle.ReasonSignupBonus = "signup_bonus"`, plus `twinkle.Service.EarnSignupBonus(ctx, scope) (Balance,
error)` and the extra case in `TestEarnReasonsAreAClosedSetWithNoLoginBonus`. It changes **no DDL** — `reason` is a plain
`TEXT` column with no CHECK (`00007_twinkle_ledger.sql`) — and adds no other reason. Splitting `invite_signup` out of
the shipped single `ReasonInvite` stays plan 66's, since the two legs are already distinguished by their dedup keys.

### Persistence isolation: four scoped statements and one stated exception

`invites.user_id` is the **inviter**, so the inviter-side statements are conjunctively scoped and pass
`pnpm lint:persistence` unchanged: mint (`INSERT … (id, user_id, token, created_at)` with a parameter), current-link
read (`WHERE user_id = $1 AND bound_at IS NULL AND created_at > …`), rewarded-cap count, and the `rewarded_at` stamp
(`WHERE user_id = $1 AND id = $2`).

The bind is also conjunctive under [60](60.account-profile-model.md)'s token model: it is an `INSERT … (id, user_id,
invitee_user_id, token, created_at, bound_at)` whose `user_id` is the inviter id read out of the verified token, so the
gate's `INSERT` rule ("`user_id` among the inserted columns with a parameter value") is satisfied without an exception.

The one **invitee-side** statement cannot be: an invite is inherently a two-user relationship, and the gate's grammar
recognizes only the literal `user_id` column, so `invitee_user_id = $1` reads as "no `user_id` predicate reaching a
parameter". It is therefore added to `globalQueries` in `scripts/check-persistence-isolation.mjs` **with the reason
stated in the SQL comment**, following the shipped `twinkle/ledger.sql#TwinkleLedgerDedupExists` precedent (a per-user
product table with one deliberately non-conjunctive statement):

- `account/invites.sql#FindSettleableInviteForInvitee` — `WHERE invitee_user_id = $1 AND rewarded_at IS NULL`, returning
  `(id, user_id AS inviter_user_id, token)` for at most one row and no other product data.

No second exception, no `platformTables` entry, and every other statement in the file stays inviter-scoped.

### RPC contract (`cosimosi.account.v1.AccountService`, Connect unary — §2.7)

```proto
// SignUp is the once-per-account first write: nickname-only input ([U6]), the client-detected
// timezone ([U7]), the negotiated locale, and — when the user arrived through an invite link —
// the opaque link token ([U8]). There is deliberately NO field a user could type a code into,
// and NO balance field: a signup binds, it never pays ([U11] settles later).
rpc SignUp(SignUpRequest) returns (SignUpResponse);

message SignUpRequest {
  string nickname = 1;      // trimmed; account.nickname_min_length..max_length
  string timezone = 2;      // IANA name, client-detected ([U7])
  string locale = 3;        // short tag; unknown coerces to the default
  string invite_token = 4;  // opaque link token; empty = no invite
}
message SignUpResponse {
  string nickname = 1;
  string timezone = 2;
  string locale = 3;
  bool invite_bound = 4;    // acknowledgment only — a refused token is never an error
}

```

`GetInviteLink†` / `GetInviteLinkRequest` / `GetInviteLinkResponse` are declared by
[60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md) and are not restated here. No request carries a `user_id` —
scope comes from `platform.UserScope`, never the wire. `SignUp` is a mutation, so it appears in no `http-policy.ts` entry. Handlers stay thin (§2.9 #7): validation, the [G6] conjunction, and idempotency live in the use-case.
New reason codes, mapped in `account/rpc/reasons.go` and mirrored in `packages/errors/src/core.ts`:
`ACCOUNT_NICKNAME_INVALID` (InvalidArgument), `ACCOUNT_TIMEZONE_INVALID` (InvalidArgument), `ACCOUNT_SIGNUP_REQUIRED`
(FailedPrecondition). Settlement errors never reach the wire — settlement has no RPC.

### Golden-parity stance

**No pure function is mirrored TS↔Go, and none may be.** Nickname validation, timezone resolution, the invite lifecycle,
and the [G6] conjunction are BE-only orchestration over IO (a directory call, a ledger write, four SQL predicates);
there is no formula for a client to reproduce and no golden fixture. The client shares only the two generated nickname
bounds (`values` constants, for instant field feedback) — a shared **constant**, not a shared function, so there is
nothing to drift. Eligibility is never computed client-side: it is not derivable from anything the client holds
(§2.9 #8).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `SignUp` accepts exactly four inputs — nickname, timezone, locale, invite token — and creates the `users` row plus
   the initial `auth_providers` row read from the directory. No other information is requested, and a client-supplied
   provider claim is ignored. ([U6][U5])
2. A nickname is accepted only when its trimmed rune length is within
   `account.nickname_min_length`..`account.nickname_max_length` and it carries no control characters or line breaks;
   nicknames are **not** unique. Violations return `ACCOUNT_NICKNAME_INVALID`. ([U6])
3. A timezone that does not resolve as an IANA location is **rejected** with `ACCOUNT_TIMEZONE_INVALID`; it is never
   silently defaulted. An unknown locale **coerces** to the default. Zone resolution works in the production
   `distroless/static` image. ([U7])
4. `SignUp` is idempotent: a retried or concurrent call returns the existing profile, overwrites no field, and re-binds
   no invite. ([U6])
5. Arriving through an invite link binds the invite automatically; there is **no request field and no RPC** through
   which a user could type an invite code. An unknown, expired, self-, already-bound, or withdrawn-inviter token binds
   nothing, returns `invite_bound = false`, and does **not** fail the signup. ([U8])
6. `SignUp` credits **nothing** — no ledger row is written on any signup path, and the request/response carry no amount
   or balance field. ([U11][G6])
7. The `invites` row is created **at bind time** by `AcceptInvite`, from the inviter id inside the token
   [60](60.account-profile-model.md) verified; this unit adds no mint query, no `GetInviteLink` RPC, and no statement
   that looks a token up across users. ([U8][U1])
8. The invite reward and the signup bonus settle only from the launch path: the reward fires from
   `memory.SignupSettlementPort`, once per launched diary, after the monotonic guard admits the diary — a past-dated
   diary that launches no star settles nothing, and no wire method can trigger a settlement. ([G6][I10])
9. A settlement pays both sides only when all four [G6] conditions hold: distinct live inviter, ≥1 launched
   `EpisodicMemory` (structural, via the trigger), per-inviter rewarded count below
   `twinkle.invite_reward_max_per_inviter`, and an email-verified invitee (implicit for `GOOGLE`). Failing any one pays
   neither side. ([G6])
10. Settlement is exactly-once and crash-recoverable: credits are written before `invites.rewarded_at` is stamped, both
    legs are keyed `invite:<inviteID>` / `invite_signup:<inviteID>`, the bonus is keyed `signup_bonus:<userID>`, and a
    replay writes nothing new. No reward is ever reversed. ([G3][G7][I1])
11. The one-time signup bonus grants `twinkle.earn_signup_bonus` to `GENERAL` with reason `signup_bonus`, at most once
    per account, and only behind the same launched-star condition. There is no login or attendance earn. ([U11][G3])
12. `twinkle.UnavailableInviteResolver` is replaced at `cmd/api/account.go` by the resolver backed by
    `account.Service.ResolveInviteSettlement`; production **fails to boot** with the settlement port or the resolver
    unbound. ([G6])
13. The settlement ports carry only `(scope)` and `(scope, token)`; no memory id, mood, strength, coordinate, or diary
    body is expressible on them, and `account` imports neither `memory` nor `twinkle`. ([I11], §2.2/§2.4)
14. Every `invites` statement is inviter-scoped except `BindInviteToInvitee` and `FindSettleableInviteForInvitee`, which
    are single-row, authenticated-invitee-keyed, and allowlisted in `check-persistence-isolation.mjs` with the reason
    stated in the SQL comment; `pnpm lint:persistence` passes. ([U1])
15. Handlers are thin — proto↔domain mapping plus a use-case call; no proto or sqlc type reaches the use-case; the
    directory, invite store, and both granters are consumer-owned interfaces. (§2.7/§2.9 #4/#6/#7)
16. This unit adds no migration, no ledger DDL change, and exactly one new `EntryReason` constant
    (`signup_bonus`); the closed-set test is extended rather than replaced
    ([66.earn-and-purchase-spend](66.earn-and-purchase-spend.md) owns the reason set and the ledger DDL).
