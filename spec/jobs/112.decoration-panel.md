## Acceptance Criteria (from plan/73.decoration-panel)

<!-- The source spec's acceptance criteria. After building, verify each is true in the running code. -->

- [ ] A1 An affordance on the universe HUD opens `widgets/decoration-panel` over the running canvas through `useDecorationRequestStore`; the panel renders **no scrim and traps no focus**, the universe stays visible and camera-interactive beside/behind it, and the renderer is **not remounted** ([P5], [A1], [14]).
- [ ] A2 The panel lists the **entire catalog** from a single `GetCatalog`† read — all three `OrnamentKind`s, every row selectable **regardless of ownership** ([P6]) — as three labeled groups in one scroll, with no tab, no ownership filter and no separate owned/unowned view.
- [ ] A3 Selecting any row — owned or not — applies **immediately to the user's real universe**: a palette through `applyPalette`, a background through the `3d-renderer` skin seam, a star variant through the star-layer remount key. Only the affected layer remounts ([P6], [V10]).
- [ ] A4 Ownership is visible **only** as the presence or absence of a price: the item view model carries a cost discriminant (`none | price | condition`) and **no `owned` boolean**; no screen, badge, section, verb or i18n key uses inventory/보관함/equip/장착 vocabulary, and no `ListInventory` or `Equip` method exists to call ([P7]).
- [ ] A5 A **floating save button** shows the **sum of the previewed unowned items' prices** and calls `Decorate` **once**; already-owned and unchanged rows contribute nothing, so re-selecting something owned reads as a free save ([P8]).
- [ ] A6 `DecorateRequest` carries **only the three selected ids** — no total, no price, no ownership claim, no ornament attribute — so the client cannot influence what it is charged; the authoritative total is the server's ([P8], [P9]).
- [ ] A7 When the previewed unowned total exceeds the **`GENERAL`** balance the save is **blocked**, and the copy names both the shortfall and the item that is short; recovery points at the achievements/earn path, never a purchase. A stale balance is caught by `STORE_INSUFFICIENT_TWINKLE` and rendered through the same copy ([P8], [P9]).
- [ ] A8 The panel reads and displays **`GENERAL` only** — `SMALL` appears nowhere in it, and it mounts no second `GetBalance` query ([P9]).
- [ ] A9 An **achievement-only, unachieved** ornament shows its **condition instead of a price**, **previews normally**, and **cannot be saved**; the server refuses it independently with its own reason ([P11]).
- [ ] A10 Closing the panel without saving **restores the confirmed selection completely** — palette, background and star variant — and the machine has **no transition from an open state to `closed`** that bypasses the transient revert/commit states ([P8]).
- [ ] A11 The preview survives **nothing**: navigating away, reloading, process death, sign-out and user switch all restore the confirmed selection. There is **no `persist` middleware, no URL parameter and no server write** in the preview path, and `resetStoreUserState()` is listed in the shared user-state reset inventory.
- [ ] A12 A recall, a diary write, or any other mutation while a preview is live **neither cancels the preview nor captures it**: no `memory.v1` or `twinkle.v1` request carries an ornament id, and a `GetUniverse` invalidation re-renders with the previewed render parameters intact.
- [ ] A13 A successful `Decorate` promotes the previewed ids to confirmed **from the response**, invalidates `GetCatalog`/`GetSelection`/`GetBalance`, and closes the panel; a failed `Decorate` writes nothing, keeps the preview and returns to a retriable state; the sheet cannot be closed while the call is in flight ([P8]).
- [ ] A14 The panel exposes **no control over any meaning-layer or scene-level value**: no color, size, brightness, seed, position, mood, bloom or camera control exists in its UI, its stores, its machine context or the request, and `widgets/decoration-panel` is banned from importing `three` / `@react-three/fiber` / `@cosimosi/3d-renderer` ([I11], [V10]).
- [ ] A15 The palette write path is **preview-then-save**: `changePalette`'s write-on-change is gone and the orphaned `features/change-palette` slice is removed (the `/settings` page and staging placeholder are already gone — job 93); the palette is one of the panel's three groups, **every palette row is free and priceless** ([P10] as amended), and the default's encoding stays absence-of-a-row.
- [ ] A16 **web + mobile parity:** the same widget and both features run on both apps, sharing `model`/`api`/`config` through `@cosimosi/store`; the only fork is the new `Sheet` primitive pair, and the mobile host gates on `useIsFocused()` (§3.5, [00.overview](00.overview.md) §4).
- [ ] A17 **All** user-visible copy resolves through the i18n seam under `store_*` (`lint:raw-strings` passes), in restrained literary Korean, with no ownership vocabulary and no sales pressure.

## Implementation Checklist

<!-- How to build it, top to bottom. [P] = parallel (different files, no dependency). Flag (gen)/(migrate)/(gen:values). -->

> **Lands after [job 110](110.ornament-catalog-model.md) and [job 111](111.decorate-usecase.md)** — it defines no proto
> and adds no values key; it consumes `GetCatalog` / `GetSelection` / `Decorate` as they are. Invoke
> `/fe-architecture` **and** `/mobile-architecture` before creating a file: this unit adds two features, one widget and
> a primitive, on both apps.

**The scrim-less host (the reason `Dialog` is refused)**

- [ ] T001 `packages/ui/src/primitives/sheet.tsx` + `sheet.native.tsx` — the parity pair. It renders **no backdrop**,
      traps no focus, and leaves the camera interactive outside its own bounds, so **`Sheet` has no `scrim` / `overlay` /
      `modal` prop at all**: the forbidden thing has no field. Cover it in the design showcase's primitives group.

**The shared model (`packages/store`, the package job 110 created)**

- [ ] T002 `ornament-preview-store.ts` — `{ previewActive, previewed, confirmed }` + `preview()` / `revert()` /
      `commit()` / `resetStoreUserState()`. `preview(kind, id)` is a **no-op while `previewActive` is false**, so nothing
      outside an open panel can install a preview. `resetStoreUserState()` joins `@cosimosi/auth/user-state`'s shared
      reset registry once, so both apps get sign-out revert for free. No `persist` middleware, no URL parameter — reload
      reverts by construction.
- [ ] T003 `decoration.machine.ts` (XState v5) — phase and last-failure reason only: `closed → browsing → saving →
committing|browsing`, plus `reverting`. `committing` and `reverting` are **transient states, not transition
      actions**, so there is no path from an open panel to `closed` that skips a commit or a revert; `saving` carries
      **no `CLOSE`** (the deletion-flow precedent) so a stale completion cannot land on a reopened panel.
- [ ] T004 [P] `decoration-request-store.ts` — the one-slot open signal (the shipped `recall-target-store` precedent), so
      the HUD affordance can open a widget without a widget importing a widget.
- [ ] T005 [P] `decorate.ts` — `requestDecorate(transport, selection)` carrying the **auth-epoch guard** moved unchanged
      in shape from the palette session helpers, so a `Decorate` that resolves after a user switch cannot commit into the
      new scope.
- [ ] T006 [P] `save-eligibility.ts` — pure, shared: `unownedTotal(catalog, preview)` (sum of `cost.amount` over
      previewed rows that are priced) and `saveVerdict(...)` → `ready | free | shortfall(amount) | locked(achievementId) |
unchanged`, compared against the **`GENERAL` balance only**, read from the shared twinkle mirror (the panel mounts
      no second `GetBalance`). Display arithmetic only — the authoritative total is derived inside `Decorate`.

**The item view model — where [P7] stops being a copy review**

- [ ] T007 `features/preview-ornament/api` maps the wire row to a **cost discriminant**, so the UI has no ownership
      boolean to render: `{kind:'none'}` (owned) | `{kind:'price', amount}` | `{kind:'condition', achievementId}`. The
      `achievementId` is joined from `ListAchievements`' `reward_ornament_id` — `store` never names an achievement — and
      an unmatched achievement-only row degrades to `{kind:'none'}` and stays unbuyable. No `owned` field, no 보관함
      group, no ownership filter, no equip verb, no sort by ownership.

**The panel (both apps; `model`/`api`/`config` shared, only `ui` forks)**

- [ ] T008 `apps/{web,mobile}/src/widgets/decoration-panel/` — `ui/DecorationPanelSheet.tsx` (three labelled groups + the
      floating save button), `config/panel.ts` (slice-local layout constants), `index.ts` exporting the sheet. Web renders
      a right-hand `<aside>` at `md` and a bottom sheet below it; mobile renders the bottom sheet and gates consumption on
      `useIsFocused()`. Scoped `'fsd/insignificant-slice': 'off'` per new slice in `apps/web/steiger.config.js`.
- [ ] T009 `apps/{web,mobile}/src/features/preview-ornament/` (`ui` rows per kind · `api` the catalog read) and
      `features/buy-ornament/` (`ui` the floating button + refusal line · `api` `Decorate` + invalidation). The button
      reads a bare 저장 when nothing is bought and `저장 · 별가루 {amount}` when something is; `shortfall` disables it and
      names the item, pointing recovery at the achievements tab / earn explainer — **never** a purchase; `locked` disables
      it, states the condition, and leaves the preview applied.
- [ ] T010 Mount the panel from `pages/universe/ui/UniverseHomePage.tsx` beside the shipped panels, opened through
      `useDecorationRequestStore` from a restrained HUD affordance.

**The preview bridge — one translation point (§3.4)**

- [ ] T011 `widgets/universe-canvas` gains a single `OrnamentPreviewBridge`, mounted **inside** `<SkinProvider>`, that
      subscribes to `previewActive ? previewed : confirmed` and drives the three existing seams: PALETTE →
      `applyPalette(id)` (the one [M6] entry point) with the shipped `usePaletteVersion()` remount keys; BACKGROUND →
      the per-user `SkyEffectKey` feeding the `emotion` skin's `sky.effect`, re-pointing only the **two production**
      `<SkinProvider defaultSkin={…}>` call sites (the four `/test`-surface providers stay); STAR_SHADER → folded into
      the existing star remount key (`star-${paletteVersion}` → `star-${paletteVersion}-${shape}`), leaving the sibling
      nebula/gist keys untouched. The renderer itself is never remounted — only the affected layer. `widgets/decoration-panel`
      never imports the renderer; keep that an eslint import ban rather than a convention.

**The palette's one mechanism, and the boot gate**

- [ ] T012 The palette reuses `usePalettePreferenceStore` (`paletteId` display vs `confirmedPaletteId`): preview is
      `applyPalette`, commit is `applyConfirmedPalette`. Delete `change-palette.ts`'s write-on-change
      (`changePalette` / `useChangePalette`) and remove the `features/change-palette` slice (`PaletteSection`,
      `settings_palette_selected`); keep `initializePaletteSession` / `resetPaletteSession` / `paletteSessionMatches` in
      their own module with the module-level session state, and re-scope `change-palette.test.ts`. The canvas widget
      imports `usePaletteVersion` from `@cosimosi/emotion/react` directly.
- [ ] T013 Rename `apps/{web,mobile}/src/app/providers/palette-bootstrap.tsx` (+ its test) to
      `decoration-bootstrap.tsx` and gate on a **single** `GetSelection` read that serves all three confirmed ids — do
      not also await the surviving `GetPalettePreference` read-through (two reads of one fact is a wasted round trip).
      Gating on it is what stops a default sky from painting and then jumping.

**Copy, tests, SSOT**

- [ ] T014 i18n under `store_*` naming a **UI role** (`store_panel_title`, `store_group_*_title`, `store_price_amount`,
      `store_condition_locked`, `store_save_action`, `store_save_action_priced`, `store_shortfall_notice`,
      `store_locked_notice`, `store_saving_notice`, `store_open_action`, `store_panel_close`) — **no `inventory_*` /
      `equip_*` key may exist** ([P7]); mood labels keep coming from `packages/emotion`'s `moodLabel`. Voice: restrained
      and literary, no sales pressure. `pnpm lint:raw-strings` gates it; final wording is authored against
      `policy/ux/design-review.md`.
- [ ] T015 Tests: `preview()` is inert while closed; every exit from an open panel reverts (close, route change, mobile
      blur, sign-out) and reload reverts by construction; a live preview survives a `GetUniverse` invalidation; a failed
      `Decorate` keeps the preview and leaves `confirmed` untouched; a success commits from the **response's** selection
      and invalidates catalog/selection/balance; `saveVerdict` covers all five arms; a `Decorate` resolving after a user
      switch does not commit.
- [ ] T016 SSOT at implement: complete the panel half of `spec/policy/ux/decoration.md` (job 110 creates it); record the
      preview lifecycle + the bridge in `spec/tech/store-ornaments.md`; note the `Sheet` primitive in
      `spec/tech/design-system.md` and its specimen in `tech/design-language.md` §10; update plan 51 for the retired
      palette section.
