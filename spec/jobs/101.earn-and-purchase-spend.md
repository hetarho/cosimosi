([G1][P8])
- [ ] A6 `EarnAchievementReward(scope, claimID, amount)` credits `GENERAL` exactly once per `claimID` under dedup key `achievement:<claimID>` in its own ledger transaction; a replay returns the current balance with no second credit. ([A3])

- [ ] A8 `ClaimInvite` credits the inviter as reason `invite` (`invite:<signupID>`) and the invitee as reason `invite_signup` (`invite_signup:<signupID>`), atomically and exactly once per signup; an unavailable or refusing resolver credits neither side. ([G3][G6])
- [ ] A9 No earn path writes a `daily_grant` row, no scheduled job or cron is added, and the wire enum has **no** `DAILY_GRANT` member; the `SMALL` tier stays a lazy derivation from the stored anchor. ([G7][T4])
- [ ] A10 The `SMALL` reset boundary is the user's IANA zone read through the consumer-owned `UserZoneReader` bound at `cmd/api`; an unknown or unparseable zone resolves to `UTC`, and no zone change or fallback ever produces a second grant within one local day. ([U7][I10])
