## Acceptance Criteria (from changes/10.free-play-demo-with-a-step-gated-tutoria)

<!-- The source spec's acceptance criteria. After building, verify each is true in the running code. -->

- [ ] A1 While the tutorial runs, only the current beat's anchored control is interactive; every other control is non-interactive **and** rendered dimmed + blurred; the skip affordance stays fully visible and interactive throughout ([O4]).
- [ ] A2 Completing the last beat or pressing skip moves the run to free play permanently: all controls become interactive and undimmed, and no tutorial chrome lingers.
- [ ] A3 The gating derives from one explicit finite-state machine that owns the run's phase (tutorial step ↔ free play); a control's availability cannot be set anywhere else, and pressing a gated control does nothing — a state-machine test walks the script and asserts per-state availability.
- [ ] A4 In free play, a control with the product's writing verb draws a random prepared diary from the fixture pool and presents it through the same UI shapes as the product's writing flow: the entry appears, 별 쪼개기 reveals its precomputed split, 별 띄우기 launches it — repeatable at least as many times as the pool is deep, with no real write and no LLM call ([Z1][Z4][Z5]).
- [ ] A5 The fixture pool is extended (authored, both locales, precomputed splits) and its integrity tests cover every added diary; the same diary always splits identically ([Z5]).
- [ ] A6 Time travel is three controls — +1일 · +1주 · +1달 — advancing the demo clock by one day, seven days and one month respectively (month length per `spec/values.yaml`), and the advance is presented like the product's universe-time passing, not as a bare date-string swap; `UniverseTimeOverlay` itself is still never mounted.
- [ ] A7 Forgetting is experienceable: as the clock advances, unrecalled stars dim by `effectiveBrightness` and their words erode by decay stage, and opening a launched star's entry shows the eroded current text while the diary body stays verbatim ([I2]).
- [ ] A8 Recall is per-star and repeatable: each 회고 re-brightens that star, increments its recall count, reshapes its seed and restores its words — with no cost, balance or spend surface anywhere ([Z2][Z8]).
- [ ] A9 Semanticization is experienceable per memory: memories left unrecalled while time advances climb the semantic stages and their gist bodies rise into the neocortex band; the sky keeps mirroring the strength-weighted emotions as the universe grows ([M5]).
- [ ] A10 The isolation boundary is unchanged: a full session issues zero RPCs, DB writes and LLM calls; the demo-scoped lint block still bans every transport path; no `isDemo` appears in shared code; leaving `/demo` clears everything and nothing persists ([Z1][Z7][I13]).
- [ ] A11 All `demo_*` copy is re-voiced in 해요체 with the product's verbs — "시간 밀기" and "일기 두 편 더" no longer exist in either catalogue — key sets match across `en.json`/`ko.json`, `pnpm gen:messages` output is committed, `lint:raw-strings` passes, and every sentence stays [I12]-honest with beat 8 carrying the [M5] definition.
- [ ] A12 `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, `pnpm test:guards`, `pnpm lint:fsd:layout` and the web build pass; the demo adds no `steiger` exemption and no new shared-code seam.
