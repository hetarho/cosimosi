> Implementation work doc. Source spec: [code-review/10.jobs-94-115-achievement,-decorate-and-sh](../code-review/10.jobs-94-115-achievement,-decorate-and-sh.md).

Covers **R004** (P2) — review 10's _enforcement asymmetry between siblings_ theme, at the anti-corruption boundary
(§2.4) of the newest context.

Five contexts test their RPC adapter (`account`, `admin` ×2, `memory`, `store`, `twinkle`); `achievement` is the
exception. `apps/api/internal/achievement/rpc/` holds only `reasons.go` and `server.go`, and the gate says so out loud:
`pnpm test:api` reports `? github.com/cosimosi/api/internal/achievement/rpc [no test files]`. The epic is otherwise
well covered — `catalog_test.go`, `counters_test.go`, `record_test.go`, `claim_test.go`, `service_test.go`, a real-DB
`pg/store_integration_test.go` and `cmd/api/achievement_integration_test.go` — which makes the handler gap read as an
oversight rather than a decision.

What is untested happens to be the two mappings that are **total only by convention**:

- `rpc/server.go:103-126` `protoAxis` — a 9-arm switch whose `default:` falls through to
  `ACHIEVEMENT_AXIS_UNSPECIFIED`. A tenth axis added to `catalog.go:24-33` without a proto arm serializes silently as
  UNSPECIFIED, and the FE groups by the wire value (`packages/achievement/src/achievement.ts:45-59`, deliberately
  response-driven), so the rows render under an unresolvable heading rather than failing.
- `rpc/server.go:131-144` `domainError` — the error→reason classification, including the
  `ErrRewardUnavailable → CodeUnavailable/reasonRewardUnavailable` arm that job 122's entire recovery story depends on
  and that the FE branches on at `AchievementList.tsx:83-88`.

Plus a wire-type asymmetry that sits unexercised here: `rpc/server.go:60` maps the reward as
`int32(entry.Reward.Twinkle())` while `rpc/server.go:89` maps the same quantity as `int64(result.TwinkleAmount)` —
which is why `packages/achievement/src/achievement.ts:30` reads `rewardTwinkle` directly but `react/claim.ts:30` has to
wrap `grantedTwinkle` in `Number()`.

**Settle the wire width now** (review 10 _Questions / Tradeoffs_): harmless today at a max of 400
(`values.yaml:463`), but it is a proto edit plus a regen either way, and the proto is still young. Cheap now, never
cheaper.

Decisions taken while building:

- **T005 — the settled width is `int64`, not `int32`.** The checklist preferred `int32` unless another service already
  carries a Twinkle amount at `int64`; `twinkle.v1` does, on every one of them (`small`/`general`/`total`, `cost`,
  the ledger `amount`). Matching it means `AchievementEntry.reward_twinkle` widens rather than
  `ClaimAchievementResponse.granted_twinkle` narrowing. The regenerated diff is that field's type and its comment,
  nothing else — field number 5 unchanged (A8).
- **T007 — the FE symmetry landed the other way round.** Both reward reads now go through `Number()`, which is what
  `target` and `progress` (also `int64`) already did in the same function — so the file has one convention instead of
  an exception.
- **`achievedAtWire` extracted from the loop.** It is the handler's own proto mapping, not a decision moved out of the
  context, and it is what lets A4 be tested without standing up a service.
- **T008 — the axis test bites.** Adding a tenth axis to `catalog.go` makes it fail with
  `axis "STREAK_DAYS" has no wire value`; reverted after.
- The wire-width assertion reads the two field **descriptors** rather than an example value, so the two surfaces cannot
  drift apart again without failing here.
