**Found and fixed during the pass, before Codex:**

- **`not_found_home_action` read "Back to your universe"** while the not-found screen navigates to `/`, which is now the
  public root. Correct target (the root is the one decision point, so it lands a visitor on the front door and a
  signed-in user in their universe) but wrong copy for half the audience — neutralized to "Back to the start" in both
  catalogues, with the reasoning noted at the call site.
- **The copy-honesty guard rejected its own disclaimer.** "It is a diary, not a model of anyone's brain" is the sentence
  [I12] wants on the page, and the first version of the matcher flagged it. Fixed by scanning per sentence and skipping
  negations, with a test asserting the disclaimer passes and the affirmative claim still fails. The Korean case is the
  sharper one: `아닙니다` contains no `아니` substring (the final consonant is baked into the syllable block), so the
  negation set lists the inflected forms.

**Codex findings — 4 of 5 applied, 1 recorded:**

1. **`/blog/` is a live destination this unit does not serve** — six links (five theory cards + the blog line) resolve to
   the SPA's not-found screen until plan 82 ships, as does the `/blog/sitemap.xml` line. **Recorded, not changed**: the
   links and the `Sitemap:` naming are what the ACs require and what 82 depends on, and the ordering is the plans' own (81
   owns the origin root, so it lands first). But it is a real user-visible window, so it is now named in plan 81 and
   `tech/landing-page.md` §7 rather than implied.
2. **The root canonical is served for `/demo` too**, while the sitemap listed `/demo` as its own URL — a contradiction
   published on purpose. Fixed by listing only `/` in the sitemap (a deviation from AC 14's wording, reasoned in the plan);
   `/demo` stays `Allow`ed in robots so a shared link is never blocked, and it has no crawlable content anyway.
3. **The poster was not actually the default**, and an async `renderer.init()` rejection never reaches the error boundary
   (it does not throw during render), so a browser without WebGPU would have shown an empty canvas rather than the hero.
   Fixed by putting the poster **under** the canvas instead of swapping for it: it is the first paint, the canvas clears
   over it when the renderer is up, and every way the renderer can fail to arrive needs no detection at all.
4. **The copy-honesty guard had a hole its own design could not close.** _"Not just a diary — it works like your brain."_
   carries a negation and the forbidden claim in one breath, so the negation skip let it through. Replaced with an
   exact-sentence allowlist of reviewed denials, plus two tests: the overclaim-with-negation must still fail, and every
   allowlist entry must appear verbatim in the catalogue so a rewrite retires its exemption.
5. **`robots.txt`'s comment claimed more than its directives do** ("only the two public surfaces are indexable" — an
   unknown path is unlisted, not blocked). Comment corrected to say what it does and why `Disallow: /` + an allowlist is
   not an option: it would block `/blog/`, the tier the theory cards lead to.

Codex also confirmed no route-move or auth defect: product routes sit under `/universe`/`/diary`/`/me` behind the guard,
`/login?from=/` falls back to `/universe`, the OAuth root callback stays coherent, `trailingSlash: 'never'` does not change
matching for any existing route (the router trims before matching), and every consumer that gated on access uses
`requiresSignIn`.

## Deviations from the checklist (as-built)

- **The section→component record lives in `ui/LandingPage.tsx`, not `model/sections.ts`** (T008). The tuple and its types
  stay pure in `model`; putting the record there would have made `model` import `ui`.
- **A second config file, `config/illustration.ts`** (T010/T012) — the invented moods and revisit weights the hero's sky
  ramp and the mirror's swatches share, so neither section owns the other's data.
- **The renderer layer is `LatentStarField`** (`@cosimosi/universe-render`), the shipped name for what the plan called
  `LatentField`; the inner primitive is the one the plan named.
- **The two rasters are procedurally generated, not designed** (T011/T019): the active skin's bare-night base lit by the
  same illustrative mood weights the hero ramp uses, latent points seeded so a regeneration reproduces the same bytes.
  They read as the sky before anything is in it, which is what they stand in for — but a **designed OG card is still
  open**, and that is a design call rather than something this job could invent.
- **`sitemap.xml` lists `/` only, not `/` and `/demo`** (T018) — see Codex finding 2.
- **`copy-honesty.test.ts` reads its inputs through Vite `?raw`**, not `node:fs` (T015): the web app's tsconfig carries
  `types: ["vite/client"]` and no Node types, and widening that for one test would have let node globals into `src`.
