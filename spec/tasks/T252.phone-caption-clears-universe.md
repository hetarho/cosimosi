## goal
On a narrow screen with no bottom surface, the demo's overview framing places the universe below the eyeline caption band, so a caption never sits on the memories it describes.

## acceptance
- [v] A pure page-local helper turns the viewport, the caption room in use and the caption band's measured height into the framing top inset. While a caption shows on a narrow screen in the eyeline room, the inset clears the band plus a gap. Otherwise it returns the existing insets unchanged. Wide layouts, recall-focus insets and free play are unchanged.
- [v] Caption room rules (`resolveCaptionPosition`, `CAPTION_EYELINE`), the engine and the widget's behaviour stay unchanged. At most, export an existing caption-band constant from the widget's public index. No shared type widens for the sandbox (DEMO-9).
- [v] `framing-insets.test.ts` covers narrow+eyeline, narrow with a bottom surface, wide and no caption. Existing demo tests stay green.
- [v] UI-15 round at 390x844 (ko), with before/after shots of beats 4, 5 and 7:
  - the caption rect intersects no memory body
  - the universe stays fully in frame
  - UI-18 dimensions are scored in this task's result ledger (UI-20)
- [v] `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck:affected`, `pnpm test:affected` against the assignment start.

## result
- outcome: On a narrow screen with no bottom surface, the demo overview now starts one `CAPTION_SURFACE_GAP_PX` below the eyeline caption band. Beats 4, 5 and 7 at 390x844 frame every memory body and the rising gists below the band. `framing-insets.ts` holds two pure functions. `guideCaptionRoom` reads the guide's default room (ONB-12) from caption-showing, narrow and bottom-surface. `framingTopInset` turns the viewport height, that room and the band's rendered height into the top inset. `DemoPage` reads the band's height from the guide's live region with a `ResizeObserver` and keeps the last reading while a scene moment holds the line, so the frame does not bob during a reveal. `DemoUniverseScene` applies the inset only to the overview. Recall focus keeps top 96. Wide layouts (`edge`), bottom surfaces (`aboveSurface`) and free play (no run) return the existing inset.
