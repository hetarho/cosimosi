## goal
On the landing walkthrough's fixed camera, the visitor sees the feeling a step talks about. At `color` and `fade` the frame is led by the heaviest slice of the sky. At `recall` and `mirror` it is led by the recalled memory's feeling and visibly widens from `recall` to `mirror`.

## acceptance
- [ ] `SkySphere` takes an optional facing slice index and turns its mesh so that slice's `emotionAnchors(count)` direction faces the camera's view direction (−Z). With the prop absent, the mesh transform and every existing render are unchanged (universe, demo, sign-in and design benches).
- [ ] The faced slice stays centred while the effect spins its own frame. Each spinning effect publishes its existing frame spin (axis and rate per sky second) as one constant, and both its shader and its registry entry read it, so no effect looks different. Facing follows the spun anchor every frame, and an effect without spin publishes none. Turns between facings ease at the universe camera's pace through an existing renderer timing constant (VIS-0 camera feel), not a 2D UI token. Under reduced motion the turn is instant.
- [ ] `WalkthroughSceneFacts` gains the facing index, derived only from the step's existing `skyStops` and the authored recall target's mood (MKT-19: no local formula). It is the heaviest slice at `color`/`fade`, and the recall target's mood slice at `recall`/`mirror`. Steps with no sky leave it undefined.
- [ ] Tests:
  - `SkySphere.test.ts`: the absent prop leaves rotation at identity, and a facing index maps its anchor onto −Z within tolerance.
  - `walkthrough.test.ts`: the facing index per step, and the recall target's slice share rising from `recall` to `mirror`.
  - `LandingWalkthroughScene.test.tsx`: the prop is passed through.
  - All existing landing and renderer tests stay green.
