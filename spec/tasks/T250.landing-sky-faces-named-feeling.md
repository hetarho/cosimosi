## goal
On the landing walkthrough's fixed camera, the visitor sees the feeling a step talks about. At `color` and `fade` the frame is led by the heaviest slice of the sky. At `recall` and `mirror` it is led by the recalled memory's feeling and visibly widens from `recall` to `mirror`.

## acceptance
- [v] `SkySphere` takes an optional facing slice index and turns its mesh so that slice's `emotionAnchors(count)` direction faces the camera's view direction (−Z). With the prop absent, the mesh transform and every existing render are unchanged (universe, demo, sign-in and design benches).
- [v] The faced slice stays centred while the effect spins its own frame. Each spinning effect publishes its existing frame spin (axis and rate per sky second) as one constant, and both its shader and its registry entry read it, so no effect looks different. Facing follows the spun anchor every frame, and an effect without spin publishes none. Turns between facings ease at the universe camera's pace through an existing renderer timing constant (VIS-0 camera feel), not a 2D UI token. Under reduced motion the turn is instant.
- [v] `WalkthroughSceneFacts` gains the facing index, derived only from the step's existing `skyStops` and the authored recall target's mood (MKT-19: no local formula). It is the heaviest slice at `color`/`fade`, and the recall target's mood slice at `recall`/`mirror`. Steps with no sky leave it undefined.
- [v] Tests:
  - `SkySphere.test.ts`: the absent prop leaves rotation at identity, and a facing index maps its anchor onto −Z within tolerance.
  - `walkthrough.test.ts`: the facing index per step, and the recall target's slice share rising from `recall` to `mirror`.
  - `LandingWalkthroughScene.test.tsx`: the prop is passed through.
  - All existing landing and renderer tests stay green.
- [v] UI-15 round on the running `/about` (desktop 1440x900 and mobile 390x844, ko):
  - Before/after shots of walkthrough presses 5, 9, 10 and 11, saved under the assignment's artifact dir.
  - UI-18 dimensions scored in this task's result as a ledger (UI-20).

## result

- outcome: The landing walkthrough sky faces the feeling its step names. `SkySphere` takes an optional `facing` slice index and an optional `facingLambda`. It turns the mesh so that slice's `emotionAnchors(count)` direction sits on −Z, composed every frame with the effect's own published frame spin, so the faced territory stays centred however long the sky has run. Absent, the mesh is never touched. Grainient, Grainstorm and Liquid Ether publish the spin their shaders already turned by (`GRAINIENT_SPIN`, `GRAINSTORM_SPIN`, `LIQUID_ETHER_SPIN`: axis [0.25, 1, 0.2] at 0.15×0.25, 0.15×0.25 and 0.1×0.08 rad per sky second) as one `SkyFrameSpin` constant that the shader and the `SkyEffect.spin` registry row both read. The other nine effects publish none. A turn between facings is an exponential damp at the camera rig's convention. The renderer cannot import `@cosimosi/universe`, so the landing host passes `UNIVERSE_CAMERA_RIG.glideLambda.focusing` (4/s), the universe camera's focus glide; that is the closest existing camera transition constant to "the view comes round to one chosen thing". Reduced motion takes the facing at once, and a facing that appears or disappears is taken at once. `WalkthroughSceneFacts.skyFacing` is undefined without a sky. Before the recall it is slice 0, the heaviest, since slices are primary-first. From the recall on it is the authored recall target's mood slice. The recall prompt (press 8) therefore still faces the heaviest slice, and the sky turns when the memory is recalled. The section forwards `skyFacing` to the scene.
