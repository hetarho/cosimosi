### 13. Change: Origin-centered lens universe with z-offset gist copies

> A **change proposal** (a WHAT delta) that modifies the already-shipped behavior of [plan/19.force-sim-layout](../../plan/19.force-sim-layout.md).
> Filled by /cosimosi:create-change through a user interview — a reviewable doc, not a one-line prompt.
> The STEPS live in the job that /cosimosi:create-change-job creates. Write it in English.

#### As-is

The simulation is fully 3D (stride 3, 3D springs / Barnes-Hut / integrator), but three compounding choices make the
universe read as an x,y-only disc:

- **The hippocampus band floats above the origin.** `force_sim.hippocampus_z_min..max` = 0..18, so the cloud's actual
  attractor is (0, 0, 9): `applyCenterForces` (`packages/force-sim/src/forces.ts`) pulls x,y toward 0 but z toward the
  band **mid**, the integrator hard-clamps z into the band every tick (`clampToHippocampusZ`,
  `packages/force-sim/src/simulation.ts`), and seeding compresses the z component of every cluster seed to ×0.25 around
  that mid (`packages/force-sim/src/seed.ts`). x,y are unbounded (`latent_field_radius` 56, spacing from
  `link_distance`), so the settled layout is an 18-unit-thick slab whose bottom face sits on the world origin — z depth
  exists but is nearly invisible, and "radius from center = connectivity" ([I7][V1]) is realized only in the x,y plane.
- **A gist star's z is an absolute, stage-only ladder.** `gistCoordinate(x, y, stage)`
  (`packages/memory-logic/src/semanticization.ts`, Go mirror `apps/api/internal/memory/semanticization.go`) copies the
  hippocampal x,y verbatim and derives z stage-linearly into the **absolute** neocortex band
  `force_sim.neocortex_z_min..max` (27..45). `GistStarLayer` (`packages/universe-render`) reads x,y live from the sim
  slot each frame and eases z to the ladder rung — absolute z alone encodes a gist star's stage, and the hippocampal z
  a memory settled at is discarded ([C6] as currently worded: "copy x,y, raise only z").
- **The rest of the stack is tuned to the slab.** The latent field scatters a uniform x,y disc × uniform band z (a
  cylinder, `packages/universe/src/latent-field.ts`); the default camera opens at [0, 0, 90] looking straight down the
  z axis at the slab face; pinned mode orbits world +z, opens dead-level (elevation 0), and clamps tilt to +20°/−10°
  (`packages/universe/src/camera-rig.ts`).

#### To-be

**One lens, and a z-shifted copy of it.** The episodic universe becomes an origin-centered oblate lens; a risen
memory's gist star is the **same point translated up the z axis** — the neocortex layer is a literal, progressively
filled copy of the hippocampal map, which is the CLS claim itself (McClelland 1995, PRD §9) made visible.

1. **Origin-centered lens.** The hippocampus band re-centers on z = 0: `hippocampus_z_min/max` = −9..+9 (thickness 18
   preserved; values-tunable). The center force targets the true origin (0, 0, 0) on all three axes; seeding stops
   flattening z beyond lens shaping. A new anisotropic z gain on the center pull (`force_sim.z_center_gain`) shapes the
   settled cloud into an oblate lens — z density tapers toward the band bounds instead of piling on the clamp faces;
   the hard clamp remains as the outer guarantee. "Radius from center = connectivity" ([I7][V1]) now reads in three
   dimensions: low-connectivity nodes drift outward **radially from (0,0,0)**, still purely as a structure phenomenon.
2. **Gist star = full-xyz copy + z offset.** The neocortex contract becomes: gist position = live episodic
   **(x, y, z)** + `gistZOffset(stage)`. `memory-logic` replaces the absolute `gistCoordinate` with a pure,
   stage-monotonic `gistZOffset(stage)` (Go-mirrored, golden-parity); composing the absolute position moves entirely
   into the renderer — `GistStarLayer` adds the offset to the live sim slot and eases only the offset change on a rise.
   No absolute gist coordinate is stored or reverse-projected ([I5] unchanged, and now the server derives no absolute
   coordinate at all).
3. **Stage interleaving accepted (decided).** Because a gist star inherits its memory's z (±half-thickness), absolute z
   no longer totally orders stages across memories. Stage stays encoded by diffuse softness ([V5] ladder) and the
   upward rise animation — nothing may read a stage back out of z (already the as-built rule).
4. **Separation by construction.** The offset ladder reuses today's numbers as **offsets**:
   `Δz(s) = gist_z_offset_min + (s / SEMANTIC_MAX_STAGE) · (gist_z_offset_max − gist_z_offset_min)`, 27..45. The lowest
   possible gist z is `hippocampus_z_min + Δz(1)` = 22.5 > lens top (+9) — a 13.5-unit visible gap, guarded by test:
   `hippocampus_z_min + Δz(1) > hippocampus_z_max`. BandFog moves into that gap (between lens top and the gist layer's
   lowest reach). [C6a]'s one-way rule is preserved: `Δz` is stage-monotonic, so a rise only ever moves the body up.
5. **Latent field fills the lens.** The gray field samples the same oblate volume (density tapering with the lens
   silhouette) instead of a cylinder, so the visible hippocampus universe _is_ the lens; still rendering-only, no DB
   rows ([E7a][V7]).
6. **The camera reads the depth.** The default pose and the pinned opening pose gain a non-zero elevation (no more
   straight-down view onto the slab face); the pinned orbit axis stays world +z; tilt bounds and gestures are retuned
   scalars, not redesigned modes.
7. **PRD wording amendment ([C5][C6][V9]) — meaning-preserving.** Everything [C5] actually asserts survives:
   hippocampus below, neocortex above, a gap between, the neocortex derived with no force-sim, positions never stored.
   What changes is the letter: the hippocampus band is origin-centered (numbers stay values-owned), and [C6]'s
   "copy x,y, raise only z" becomes "copy x,y,z, raise by a stage offset".

#### Scope / Non-goals (regression boundary)

**In scope**

- `packages/force-sim` — seed z, center-force target + anisotropic z gain, band re-centering, golden fixtures re-baked.
- `packages/memory-logic` `gistZOffset(stage)` + its Go mirror (`apps/api/internal/memory/semanticization.go`) — a
  pure derivation function only; no API, proto, or schema surface moves.
- `packages/universe-render` `GistStarLayer` (offset composition + rise easing), BandFog placement,
  `packages/universe/src/latent-field.ts` (lens-shaped sampling), camera-rig opening scalars.
- `spec/values.yaml` `force_sim.*` (see below) + regenerated config.
- SSOT reflection: PRD [C5][C6][V9], plan 19 + plan 42 as-built notes, [policy/ux/layout.md](../../policy/ux/layout.md),
  [tech/force-sim.md](../../tech/force-sim.md), [tech/rendering.md](../../tech/rendering.md).

**Non-goals — what must NOT change**

- **No position persistence, proto, RPC, or DB change of any kind** ([I5]); the server stays facts-only.
- **No emotion or time input into layout** ([I3][V1]); the forbidden-input guard stays; outward drift remains a
  connectivity phenomenon (forgetting → weaker pull → outward), never a function of age.
- **No memory↔memory force** ([I4]); springs, centroid placement, repulsion, and determinism discipline (seeded PRNG,
  no `Math.random`/trig in the deterministic path) are untouched beyond the center-force z re-target.
- **Semanticization is untouched** — timing, stages, texts, provenance ([C6a][C7]); only where the gist body sits
  changes. One transforming gist body per memory (change 11) unchanged.
- **No camera mode or gesture redesign** — scalar retune only; free/pinned machine and zoom envelope stay.
- **Web↔mobile parity** — every behavior change lives in shared packages; no app-side forks; golden fixtures pin
  byte-identical coordinates on both apps after the one re-bake.
- Demo/staged scenes (landing walkthrough, design bench, showcase) keep hand-authored buffers — re-checked visually
  against the new bands, not rewritten.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. The hippocampus band is origin-centered and values-owned: `force_sim.hippocampus_z_min = −force_sim.hippocampus_z_max`
   (default ±9), and the center force targets (0, 0, 0) on all three axes.
2. z is a genuinely spread force output: on the golden fixture graph the settled z distribution fills the band interior
   as an oblate lens (fixtures assert spread; no pile-up at the clamp faces), while every coordinate stays in-band.
3. Every risen memory's gist body renders at live episodic (x, y, z) + `gistZOffset(stage)`; `gistZOffset` is pure,
   stage-monotonic, Go/TS golden-parity-pinned; no absolute gist coordinate is stored or reverse-projected ([C6][I5]).
4. Layer separation holds by construction: `hippocampus_z_min + gistZOffset(1) > hippocampus_z_max` (guard test), so no
   episodic star can ever sit above any gist star, and BandFog sits inside that gap.
5. The latent field samples the lens volume (matching silhouette) and remains rendering-only with no DB rows
   ([E7a][V7]).
6. The default camera pose and the pinned opening pose have non-zero elevation so the lens depth reads; the pinned
   orbit axis remains world +z.
7. Sim inputs remain structure-only (forbidden-field guard passes); positions are never persisted or sent
   ([I3][I5][V1]).
8. Web and mobile produce byte-identical coordinates for the fixture graph after the one fixture re-bake
   (golden-parity preserved).
9. PRD [C5][C6][V9], plan 19 and plan 42 as-built notes, policy/ux/layout.md, tech/force-sim.md, and tech/rendering.md
   state the lens + offset-copy contract.
