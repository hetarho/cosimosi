### 82. blog site

> Blog site — the long-form theory tier of the public funnel. `apps/blog` (Astro, static) becomes a real content site
> with one file per post, served at the **`/blog` subpath of the product origin** so one domain accumulates authority and
> every landing↔post link stays same-origin ([K5]). Two builds share one Cloudflare Worker asset directory via a staged
> copy — no second Worker, no second zone route, no subdomain. It owns the depth (papers, DOIs); PRD §9 stays the
> requirement↔evidence SSOT, and the lay summary stays on the landing page ([K2]).
> Scope: FE | Status: built (job 121)
>
> Layer P. Home: `apps/blog` (an app root **outside** FSD and outside the i18n gates), plus exactly one FSD slice in
> `apps/web` — `pages/blog-not-found` — which fills the seam the SPA not-found handler opens under `/blog/**`.

#### Purpose

The product has a marketing page and a theory story, but the theory has nowhere deep to live. PRD §9 is a
requirement→evidence map written for implementers: it names 엔그램, 재공고화, 침묵 엔그램 and their DOIs in one line
each, which is exactly right for tracing a rule to its paper and exactly wrong for a curious visitor. [K2] resolves this
with two tiers — the landing page carries a lay summary a non-specialist reads ([K1] step 5; §1.5 says neuroscience
knowledge is never required of a user), and **the blog carries the depth**: the papers, the DOIs, the "why this rule and
not another" reasoning. This unit builds that second tier.

It also closes a real hosting gap. `apps/blog` exists today — an Astro 7 app in the pnpm workspace, gated by
`pnpm typecheck:blog` and `pnpm build:blog`, with nine finished Korean essays inside a single `src/blog.md` rendered as
one long anchored page. **Nothing deploys it.** The Cloudflare Workers Builds command is
`pnpm --filter @cosimosi/web build` and `wrangler.jsonc` uploads `./apps/web/dist` alone (`DEPLOY.md` §2, §5 step 9), so
`apps/blog/dist` has never reached the origin. Worse, its output is shaped for the origin _root_: it claims
`/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/rss.xml`, `/favicon.svg` and `/index.html`, three of which collide with the app the
moment both dists share a directory. [K5] fixes the address (`/blog`, a subpath — not a subdomain); this plan fixes the
mechanism, and states both as one arrangement instead of discovering them at deploy time.

Finally, the existing content is not yet publishable under [I12]/[I3]. Post 8 ("비슷한 감정의 기억끼리 끌어당깁니다")
tells the reader that cosimosi binds stars whose **emotion coordinates are close** more tightly, that strong arousal
keeps a memory "가까이", and that memories **attract each other** — three claims the product must never implement ([I3]
emotion never touches position or links; [I4] there is no memory↔memory edge or attraction; [I7] radius is
self-relevance). Public copy that describes forbidden mechanics is the [I12] failure mode in its purest form: not
overselling the brain metaphor, but describing a _different, wronger_ product. The closing note also links
`spec/tech/neuroscience.md`, a file that does not exist, from public copy. Publishing is therefore gated on a content
correction, not just a pipeline.

#### Scope / Non-goals

**In scope**

- **The serving arrangement** ([K5]) — `apps/blog` builds to `apps/blog/dist`, which is staged into `apps/web/dist/blog/`
  **after** the web build, so the single Worker (`wrangler.jsonc` `name: "cosimosi"`, `assets.directory:
"./apps/web/dist"`) serves both from one asset directory with no route, zone or DNS change. One root script
  owns the sequence; the Workers Builds command becomes that one script.
- **`base: '/blog'` + `trailingSlash: 'always'` in `apps/blog/astro.config.mjs`** ([K5]) — without `base`, every page
  404s its own `_astro/*` stylesheet; `trailingSlash` makes dev and production agree on the canonical `/blog/` form.
- **Origin-root ownership** ([K5]) — the blog stops shipping `robots.txt` and `favicon.svg`; the origin root owns them.
  The blog's `sitemap.xml` moves under `/blog/` and is referenced from the root one.
- **A content model: one post = one file** — an Astro content collection at `apps/blog/src/content/posts/` with a
  schema-validated frontmatter (`title` · `description` · `pubDate` · `lang` · `pillar` · `sources[]` · `draft`), routed
  to `/blog/<slug>/`, indexed at `/blog/`, and syndicated at `/blog/rss.xml`. The nine essays in `src/blog.md` migrate
  to nine files.
- **The five-pillar link to the landing page** ([K2]) — `pillar` is a closed enum of exactly the five ids
  [81.landing-page](81.landing-page.md)'s theory cards link to: `engram` (엔그램), `spatial-representation` (공간 표상),
  `synapse-time` (시냅스·시간), `reconstructive-recall` (재구성적 회상), `forgetting-accessibility` (망각=접근성).
  The `/blog/` index groups posts under their pillar and **emits each group heading with `id="<pillar>"`**, so the
  landing card's `/blog/#<id>` resolves to a real anchor. Each landing card therefore has a stable blog destination and
  PRD §9 traceability is checkable at build time.
- **The `/blog/**` miss behavior** — a `/blog/$` splat route in `apps/web` rendering blog-shaped not-found chrome, plus
  the honest statement of what the SPA asset handler can and cannot do about the status code.
- **The [I12] content gate** — a repository lint over blog content that rejects "works like a brain"-class claims,
  therapeutic claims, and any sentence presenting the 3D coordinates as the brain's real coordinates; plus the content
  correction of post 8 and the dead `spec/tech/neuroscience.md` link.
- **The three blog build constants stay module constants in the Astro app** — no `spec/values.yaml` key, no group, no
  `@cosimosi/config` dependency (`apps/blog` keeps `astro` as its only dependency). The Policy section carries the
  reasoning: groups are closed and v2 sanctions no blog group, and no other surface reads these numbers.

```
└── index.ts
```

`apps/blog` is listed in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` and already wired into `pnpm check` (`build:blog`) and `pnpm typecheck`
(`typecheck:blog`); this unit adds no new gate wiring for the app itself. Note that `.astro` files have **no Prettier
parser** in this repo (`prettier .` skips them; naming one explicitly errors), so `format:check` does not cover them —
markdown content _is_ covered, and that asymmetry is stated rather than silently relied on.

##### The serving arrangement — two builds, one origin, one asset directory

```
pnpm build:site                                                     ← the single Workers Builds command
  1. pnpm --filter @cosimosi/blog build   → apps/blog/dist          (base '/blog' baked into every asset URL)
  2. pnpm --filter @cosimosi/web  build   → apps/web/dist           (vite empties outDir — must run before 3)
  3. node scripts/stage-blog.mjs          → apps/web/dist/blog/**   (copy, then assert index.html + _astro/ exist)
  4. npx wrangler deploy                  → uploads ./apps/web/dist (deploy command, unchanged)
```

Step 2 must precede step 3 because Vite empties `outDir` on build; a copy staged before the web build is silently
deleted. `scripts/stage-blog.mjs` therefore _fails loudly_ if `apps/web/dist` is missing or if the staged tree lacks
`blog/index.html` — the ordering bug becomes a build error instead of a 404 in production. `wrangler.jsonc` changes in
exactly one way: `assets.html_handling` is declared **explicitly** (`"auto-trailing-slash"` — the value that resolves a
directory index for both `/blog` and `/blog/`; confirmed against the Cloudflare assets reference at implement) so the
behavior is pinned rather than inherited from an undeclared default. `assets.directory` and
`assets.not_found_handling` are untouched. `DEPLOY.md` §2 and §5 step 9 are amended to name `pnpm build:site`.

**Rejected: a second Worker with a `…/blog*` zone route.** It costs a second Workers Builds project on the same
repository, a second wrangler config (the dashboard reads only the root `wrangler.jsonc`; a second needs
`-c apps/blog/wrangler.jsonc`), a second build-variable set, and a second deployment to keep in step — for zero benefit
over one copy, since both apps already build in the same pnpm workspace on the same push. **Also rejected: a
`blog.` subdomain** — it splits domain authority and makes every landing↔post link cross-origin, the exact thing [K5]
forbids.

##### The `/blog/**` miss, stated rather than discovered

`assets.not_found_handling: "single-page-application"` returns the app's `index.html` with **HTTP 200** for any path
matching no asset. Consequences, accepted deliberately:

1. A typo under `/blog/**` renders the _app's_ client not-found screen — wrong chrome, no way back to the blog. Fixed
   by a public splat route `path: '/blog/$'` under `rootRoute` (sibling of `/login`, `/test`, `/design`), **outside**
   `authenticatedRoute`, with **no `beforeLoad`** — it must never require a session or the diagnostics flag. It renders
   blog-shaped chrome and a link back to `/blog/`. This route can never shadow a real post: static assets resolve
   first, so the SPA only executes on a genuine miss — a structural guarantee, not a precedence rule to maintain.
2. The status code stays 200, so a crawler sees a soft-404. Mitigations: the blog's sitemap lists only real, non-draft
   URLs, and the not-found route sets `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` on mount. A true 404 would require a
   Worker _script_ in front of the assets, which this plan's one-Worker, assets-only arrangement pins out — recorded
   here as the honest limitation, not silently absorbed.
3. Trailing slash: the canonical form is **`/blog/`** (Astro `trailingSlash: 'always'` + `build.format: 'directory'`),
   every internal link is authored in that form, and the declared `assets.html_handling` makes the slashless `/blog`
   resolve too. This plan owns the trailing-slash policy for the route it introduces and touches no other route's —
   the origin's is [81.landing-page](81.landing-page.md)'s.
4. The web slice needs **no** steiger exemption block — every shipped block targets `entities|features|widgets` and
   no `pages/*` slice carries one (plan 81 states the rule this plan follows) — and its three strings go through the
   i18n seam — `lint:raw-strings`
   scans `apps/web/src`, so a raw Korean literal there fails the gate. Keys: `blog_not_found_title` ·
   `blog_not_found_description` · `blog_not_found_back_action`, in **both** `en.json` and `ko.json`, followed by
   `pnpm gen:messages` and a committed generated output (`spec/tech/i18n.md` §2). This adds `blog_*` as one more
   surface prefix in the per-surface key scheme without altering any of its rules. **The blog's own content is not
   i18n'd** ([K5], and `spec/tech/i18n.md` §2: "no marketing/blog localization — the Astro blog keeps its own
   pipeline"); only this `apps/web` route is, because of where it lives.

##### The content model — one post is one file

`apps/blog/src/content.config.ts` declares a `posts` collection whose schema is the build-time gate:

| Field         | Type                                                                                                                                      | Why it is schema-enforced                                                                                                                      |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `title`       | string                                                                                                                                    | the `<h1>`, the OG title, the index label, the feed item title                                                                                 |
| `description` | string                                                                                                                                    | the meta description and the index/feed summary — authored, never truncated from the body                                                      |
| `pubDate`     | date                                                                                                                                      | index ordering (newest first), feed `pubDate`, sitemap `lastmod`                                                                               |
| `lang`        | `'ko' \| 'en'`                                                                                                                            | drives `<html lang>` and the index chip; **this is the whole of [K5]'s per-language story**                                                    |
| `pillar`      | enum of the five §9 pillars — `engram` · `spatial-representation` · `synapse-time` · `reconstructive-recall` · `forgetting-accessibility` | a landing theory card ([K1] step 5) resolves to `/blog/#<pillar>`, the id the index's group heading carries; an unknown pillar fails the build |
| `sources`     | array of `{ label, doi }`, min 1                                                                                                          | [K2]'s promise that the depth tier _cites_; a theory post with no source cannot ship                                                           |
| `updated`     | date, optional                                                                                                                            | `lastmod` when a post is corrected                                                                                                             |
| `draft`       | boolean, default false                                                                                                                    | excluded from index, feed and sitemap at once — the only publication switch                                                                    |

Routing: `/blog/` (index — non-draft posts newest first, grouped under their pillar under a heading carrying
`id="<pillar>"`, each with a `lang` chip),
`/blog/<slug>/` (the post, `slug` = filename), `/blog/rss.xml` (the newest N non-draft posts, N a module constant in
the endpoint),
`/blog/sitemap.xml` (every non-draft post). The feed endpoint stays hand-rolled — it already is, and a dependency to
emit thirty lines of XML is not earned. Each post's foot shows up to a fixed number of other posts sharing its
`pillar`, plus one restrained CTA pair — `/demo` and `/` — closing the funnel the landing page opens.

`<head>` per post: `<link rel="canonical">` at the absolute `/blog/<slug>/` URL, OG/Twitter tags with the absolute
`og-blog.png`, `<link rel="alternate" type="application/rss+xml">` at `/blog/rss.xml`, and `BlogPosting` JSON-LD whose
`citation` entries come from `sources[]` — so the DOIs the schema requires are machine-readable too, which is what a
theory-depth tier should look like to a crawler.

##### Content migration and the [I3]/[I12] correction

`src/blog.md`'s nine `##` sections become nine files and `src/blog.md` is retired. Slugs are English kebab-case (the URL
is code, [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) §5) while the prose stays Korean with `lang: ko`. The existing per-post
"Description(작성자·심화 학습용)" block splits: the 논문·DOI list becomes structured `sources[]` rendered as a citations
section; the "LLM에 물어보기" prompts stay body content, since inviting a reader to interrogate the theory is exactly the
depth tier's job.

Two corrections are conditions of publication, not follow-ups:

- **Post 8 is rewritten.** The claims "감정 좌표가 가까운 별끼리 살짝 더 단단하게 묶습니다", "비슷한 감정의 기억끼리
  끌어당기는 거예요" and "강렬했던 기억일수록 오래, **가까이**, 밝게 남아요" describe emotion driving links and
  distance, and memories attracting one another — forbidden by [I3], [I4] and [I7]. The rewrite keeps the science
  (Russell's circumplex; mood-congruent recall; McGaugh's amygdala modulation) and states what cosimosi actually does:
  valence becomes **color**, arousal modulates **strength and the forgetting rate**, links emerge from **shared neurons

##### The [I12] copy gate

##### Parity waiver

`blog-site` ships **web only**. A native app has no marketing or long-form-article route, and Astro has no React Native
analogue. The waiver is recorded here in full because `scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` does **not** enforce web↔mobile page
peering — an unstated waiver is silently broken rather than caught — following the admin-console precedent
(`spec/policy/ops/admin.md` §6: "the web↔mobile parity rule is deliberately waived for it"). The one `apps/web` slice
this unit adds (`pages/blog-not-found`) is likewise web-only: it repairs a _URL_ miss, and mobile has no URLs.

##### Golden-parity stance

**Zero parity obligation, guaranteed by dependency shape rather than by discipline.** This unit re-implements no pure
function, mirrors none, and reads no domain value: it renders authored prose. Its only workspace dependency is
`@cosimosi/config` (generated `spec/values.yaml` constants), itself a generated artifact whose freshness
`pnpm check:gen` already gates. It imports none of the golden-parity-pinned packages (`@cosimosi/memory-logic`,
`@cosimosi/twinkle-logic`, `packages/emotion`, `packages/force-sim`) and adds no fixture, constant or formula — so
`scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` R4 (byte-identical pure modules) has nothing to catch here. Where the prose _describes_ a
shipped formula, no number is restated: the post describes the behavior and the authoritative figures stay in
`spec/values.yaml` and PRD §9. The real risk in a copy unit is not numeric drift but **semantic drift** — prose
describing mechanics the code does not have — and that is what the [I12] gate above guards.
[77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror](77.demo-fixtures-and-mirror.md)'s TypeScript mirrors are irrelevant here; the blog never
runs domain logic.

#### As built — what moved

- **`build.inlineStylesheets: 'never'`** was added, which the plan did not anticipate. Astro inlines a small stylesheet by
  default, and with everything inlined there is no `_astro/*` request left — so `base` is never exercised and the staged
  assertion has nothing to assert. Emitting the file makes the guard real, and one cacheable stylesheet beats the same
  6 KB duplicated into ten pages.
- **A `BaseLayout.astro` sits under `PostLayout.astro`.** The head, the header, the footer and the one stylesheet are
  shared by the index and every post rather than duplicated; `PostLayout` adds the article chrome, the citations and the
  JSON-LD. `src/site.ts` is the single URL helper the plan asked for.
- **The forgetting post needed the same correction as the emotion post.** The plan named post 8 as the condition of
  publication; post 4 also said a star sits near the centre when it is "often recalled, emotionally deep and well
  connected". Emotional depth does not enter the radius — it is connectivity, never emotion and never recency — so that
  clause is gone too. Two rewrites, not one.
- **The copy gate's negation rule was a correction, not a design.** Its first version flagged every denial, including
  "기억끼리 서로를 끌어당기지 않습니다" — the exact sentence the rule exists to produce. Its own `--probe` caught it. A
  match now counts only when the clause it starts is not negated, and the window is deliberately **after** the match:
  Korean negation is post-verbal, so a tail check catches every honest denial without the hole a whole-sentence check has
  (an English-style "Not just a diary — it works like your brain" puts the negation before the claim).
- **Two sources were dropped rather than substituted.** The migrated essays cited a book chapter (Bjork & Bjork 1992) and
  a 1949 monograph (Hebb), neither of which has a DOI the schema will accept. The first instinct was to swap in a
  DOI-bearing paper on the same ground — and that was wrong, because a DOI written from memory is exactly the kind of
  citation a page about source honesty must not carry. Both entries are gone from the lists; the remaining sources on those
  two posts are the DOI-bearing ones the essays already had, and the prose still credits Hebb and the
  storage-versus-retrieval distinction **by name**. The schema governs the citation list, not the argument.
- **One prompt lost its clinical framing.** The migrated post 3 asked about reconsolidation "applied to PTSD treatment" — a
  question rather than a claim, but the therapeutic class cannot tell the difference and should not try to. It now asks
  about clinical research generally, with no condition named. The rule is blunt on purpose.
