#### Purpose

Today the product has no front door. A stranger who opens `https://cosimosi.haeram.me` is redirected to a sign-in form
by [53.auth-universe-gate](53.auth-universe-gate.md)'s gate — a password field for a product they have never heard of.
[U4] calls the marketing page a MUST for exactly this reason: it is the first contact for everyone who is not already a
user, and every other v2 acquisition surface (the demo, the blog, the invite link) assumes it exists to send people
onward.

The harder problem is what the page must say. cosimosi's pitch is unusually easy to overclaim: it is built on memory
neuroscience, it moves, and it looks like a brain if you squint. [I12] forbids all three temptations — the copy says
"inspired by engram theory", never "works like a brain"; it never presents the 3D coordinates as the brain's real
coordinates ([I5] — they are emergent visualization); it claims no therapeutic or clinical effect. [K2] adds the
discipline that makes honesty easy to keep: the landing carries only the summary a non-specialist reads (§1.5 says
neuroscience knowledge is never required of a user), and every paper and DOI lives on the blog. A landing page with a
citation list is a landing page that will eventually cite something it does not implement.

And one thing the page must say, whether or not it sells: **[M5]**. The universe's color is not the average of your
emotions — it is a mirror of the ones you keep returning to. §1.4 makes that definition the core of the product
message, and [K1] step 4 requires it on the page, because a user who believes the sky averages their feelings will read
their own universe wrong for months. It is the one place where the honest sentence is also the interesting one.

Finally, this is where the app's routing becomes an information architecture: `/` has meant "the universe, or a redirect
to login" since v1, and now means "the universe if you are signed in, the landing if you are not" — the single reserved
gate change, made so that a visitor, a returning user mid-refresh and a deep link each resolve without a flash.

The section→component record ended up in `ui/LandingPage.tsx` rather than in `model/sections.ts`: the tuple and its types

created** — which is also why `apps/web/steiger.config.js` needs **no** new `fsd/insignificant-slice` exemption block

may not import `app`, so the locale-persistence seam (`app/providers/i18n-config.ts`) reaches the page as an injected

##### The route change — `/` becomes public, the universe becomes `/universe`

`packages/auth/src/gate-decision.ts` adds `'landing'` to the `GateDecision` union, and **exactly one arm changes**:
settled `signedOut` → `'landing'`. `signingIn`/`expired`/`failed` stay `'login'` (a returning user whose token died is
not a marketing arrival); `bootstrapping`/`refreshing` stay `'hold'`, which is what stops the landing flashing for a
returning user mid-refresh; `authenticated` stays `'universe'`, so a signed-in user never sees the landing.

Widening the union would silently pass every `=== 'login'` comparison in the consumers, so the same file exports
**`requiresSignIn(decision: GateDecision): boolean`** — an exhaustive `switch` true for `'login'` and `'landing'`. Every
consumer asks that question instead of comparing strings, so a future fifth arm becomes a compile error inside one pure
function rather than a silent behavior change in four files. That is the structural guard for the widening.

| Consumer                                            | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `app/routes/route-tree.tsx`                         | `landingRoute` at `/` under `rootRoute`, no `beforeLoad`; `universeRoute` re-parented to `/universe` under `authenticatedRoute` (the other three product routes untouched)                                                                                                     |
| `app/routes/guards/auth-gate.ts`                    | the guard redirects on `requiresSignIn(...)` — a deep link into a product route still goes to `/login?from=…`, never to the marketing page, so the return target survives; `loginReturnTarget`'s fallback becomes `/universe` and `/` joins `/login` in the never-replayed set |
| `app/routes/route-screens.tsx`                      | `AuthenticatedLayout` navigates away on `requiresSignIn(...)`; a new `LandingRoute` wrapper supplies `onTryDemo` / `onSignUp` / `locale` / `onSelectLocale`; `LoginRoute` needs no change (`'landing'` never reaches it)                                                       |
| `apps/mobile/src/app/navigation/NavigationRoot.tsx` | the stack selector uses `requiresSignIn(gateDecision(status))`, so `'landing'` maps to the login stack — mobile has no landing (the web-only waiver)                                                                                                                           |

`LandingRoute` reads the live snapshot and resolves by decision: `'universe'` → navigate to `/universe`; `'hold'` → the
shipped neutral `AuthHold`; `'login'` → navigate to `/login`; `'landing'` → the page. So `/` is the app's single
decision point, and no URL means two things at once.

> **As-built divergence — the root is the door, and the landing is `/about`.** The address the section above assigns
> is the one thing that did not hold. `/` now renders the entry screen (`EntryRoute` → `LoginPage`) and the landing
> moved to its own public route, `/about`, reached from the door's side doors, from the blog and from search. Three
> consequences, all live:
>
> - **`gateDecision` lost its `'landing'` arm.** Settled `signedOut` maps to `'login'` with every other signed-out
>   status, so the union is back to three (`universe` · `login` · `hold`). The arm answered "which of two surfaces
>   does `/` mean for a signed-out visitor", and the root means one thing again. `requiresSignIn` stays, and every
>   consumer still asks through it — the hazard is a future FOURTH arm, not this one.
> - **`/about` carries no gate at all.** It is a plain public page like `/demo`: an authenticated visitor following a
>   shared link reads it rather than being forwarded to their universe. `EntryDoor` (shared by `/` and `/login`)
>   renders the form only when `requiresSignIn` is true, which is what keeps the no-flash rule at the root.
> - **The SEO root followed the content.** The shell's canonical, `og:url` and `public/sitemap.xml` name `/about`;
>   `/` and `/demo` stay `Allow`ed but unlisted. The blog's two "look around cosimosi" links point at `/about` too.

> This delta has no change number: it was made directly rather than through `spec/changes/`. The current shape is

##### The hero — the empty universe

The hero mounts the real renderer through `@cosimosi/3d-renderer`'s `UniverseCanvas` with exactly two layers:
`SkySphere` (the shipped `rendering.active_skin` emotion sky, fed an authored illustrative stop set built from
`packages/emotion`'s `moodColor`) and `LatentStarField` (`rendering.latent_star_count` / `latent_field_radius` /
`latent_star_size`), at `rendering.max_pixel_ratio`. `SkySphere` self-animates through `useFrame`, so the omitted frame
pump costs the hero nothing. There is **no** `StarField`, cell-star or filament layer, no
`NebulaField`, no `CameraControls`, no sim bridge and no frame pump: the hero is not navigable, and a marketing page is
not where the frame budget the demo needs one click later should go.

It reads as the sky before any memory is in it — honest twice over: it is literally what a new account looks like
([U2], "the first universe is empty"), and it puts nothing on screen that copy could mis-describe as anatomy ([I5]).
**Rejected:** a fixture-backed hero showing real stars and a constellation. It would duplicate
[79.demo-page](79.demo-page.md)'s `DemoUniverseScene`, pull `@cosimosi/demo` and `packages/force-sim` into the first
paint of the page a stranger opens on mobile data, and preempt the very thing [K1] step 2's CTA exists to deliver.

`useReducedMotion()` from `@cosimosi/ui` and a failed renderer init both fall back to a committed raster poster in
`apps/web/public/`; the poster is the default and the canvas replaces it once initialized, so a visitor never waits on
WebGPU to see the hero. Reduced motion never mounts the canvas at all — the hero's whole motion is the sky's drift — and
the renderer's boundary carries no `resetKeys`, so a failed init stays fallen back for the visit rather than retrying
WebGPU behind a headline.

**The two rasters are procedurally generated, not designed** (`landing-hero.png` 1600×900, `landing-og.png` 1200×630):
the active skin's bare-night base lit by the same illustrative mood weights the hero ramp uses, with the latent field as
faint seeded points, deterministic so a regeneration reproduces the same bytes. They read as the sky before anything is
in it, which is what they stand in for. A designed OG card is a later call, not a blocker
([tech/landing-page.md](../tech/landing-page.md) §2).

  product's whole arc on one canvas, six fixed steps (`split → launch → color → fade → recall → mirror`), each a short
  caption and the change it describes, walked with back/next and nothing else — no per-step verb, because the visitor is
  reading a story rather than operating an app. Fully hardcoded and advance-only: an authored diary and precomputed split
  in both locales, no free-text input, no mood picker, no time slider — every run is deterministic, and anyone who wants
  to steer is one click from the demo. Every
  visual is the shipped behaviour, not an animation of it: the split reveal, the launched stars over `starChannels`,
  the `effectiveBrightness` dimming with the persisted `decayStageText` word loss, the recall recovery with the
  authored reconsolidated reading and a `reshape`d seed-form, and the sky as `effectiveStrength`-weighted emotion
  slices — [M5]'s stated mechanism. The mirror step states the definition plainly (우주의 색은 내 감정의 평균이
  아니라, 내가 자주 떠올리는 감정의 거울이에요) and is the tuple's guarded final step.
- **Theory cards ([K1] step 5, [K2])** — five cards, one per §9 strand, ids `engram`, `spatial-representation`,
  `synapse-time`, `reconstructive-recall`, `forgetting-accessibility`. Each is a lay paragraph whose type has **no
  citation field**, and each links to `/blog/#<id>` — the same five strings **owned by
  [82.blog-site](82.blog-site.md)** as its closed `pillar` enum, which the blog index emits as `id="<pillar>"` group
  headings. A landing unit test asserts the tuple is exactly those five ids; an unknown pillar fails the blog's own
  build. Both blog links are plain anchors with an absolute path, never
  router `Link`s: `/blog/` is Worker-served static HTML, and a client navigation would land in the SPA fallback.
- **Blog link ([K1] step 6)** — one restrained line to `/blog/`, for the reader who wants the depth. Nothing else on
  the page competes with it.

##### Locale — auto-detect plus a manual switch ([K4])

Detection is the shipped negotiation, unchanged: `WebI18nProvider` resolves `resolveWebLocale({ stored, languages })`
(stored choice → `navigator.languages` → `defaultLocale`) and syncs `document.documentElement.lang`. A visitor has no
session, so there is no server `users.locale` in the precedence
([60.account-profile-model](60.account-profile-model.md)) — `localStorage["cosimosi.locale"]` is the whole
of persistence. Note the seam is **not empty**: `writeStoredLocale()` is fully implemented and a manual switch
already ships on `/me` (the signed-in `AccountProfile` control, which also writes `UpdateProfile`). What this unit
adds is the **public, signed-out** switch — the first writer reachable without a session.

The switch is a two-option control in the landing's header. It reads `useActiveLocale()` from
`apps/web/src/shared/i18n` and writes through the `onSelectLocale` callback `LandingRoute` injects, which calls
`setActiveLocale` and persists the choice — keeping `pages → app` off the import graph. It is deliberately **not** a
shared slice: [64.my-page](64.my-page.md)'s language control writes `UpdateProfile` for a signed-in user ([K6]), a
different operation with a different authority, and the only genuinely common part (the negotiation) already lives
below both.

All copy is `landing_*` in both `packages/i18n/messages/{en,ko}.json`, regenerated with `pnpm gen:messages`; keys name a
UI role, never a domain term (`spec/tech/i18n.md` §2). `pnpm lint:raw-strings` covers every rendered string on the page.

##### Copy honesty ([I12], [K2]) — and where the gate lives

Beyond the citation-free card type, this unit ships `pages/landing/model/copy-honesty.test.ts`, whose inputs are (a)
every `landing_*` message in **both** catalogues and (b) `apps/web/index.html` — the two places public prose reaches a
visitor from this unit. It fails on:

1. brain-equivalence claims (뇌처럼 작동 · 뇌와 동일 · "works like a brain" · "like your brain does");
2. therapeutic or clinical claims (치료 · 치유 · 효능 · therapy · cure · clinically);
3. any sentence presenting the 3D coordinates as the brain's real coordinates;
4. mechanics the product does not have — emotion driving position or link strength, memories attracting one another,
   radius meaning recency ([I3][I4][I7]);
5. **academic-citation shapes** — a DOI (`10.\d{4,9}/…`), "et al.", or a parenthesized author-year — which is [K2]'s
   rule made mechanical;

and it asserts the `landing_*` key sets in `en.json` and `ko.json` are identical, that the positive framing is actually
present, and — via one deliberate offender per class — that each matcher can fail.

**As built, the scan is per sentence and skips negations**, which the plan had not anticipated and the implementation
forced: "It is a diary, not a model of anyone's brain" is the sentence [I12] wants on the page, and the first version of
the guard rejected it. A rule that punishes the disclaimer pushes the copy into saying nothing at all, and quiet is not
the same as honest. (The Korean case is sharper than it looks: `아닙니다` contains no `아니` substring, because the final
consonant is baked into the syllable block — so the negation set lists the inflected forms, with a test of its own.)

A test rather than a lint script, deliberately: [82.blog-site](82.blog-site.md) owns `scripts/lint-public-copy.mjs` and
lands **after** this plan ([00.overview](00.overview.md) §5.6), so a script here would collide with the one that plan
already specifies. The hand-off
is stated in both directions — 82's root list gains `packages/i18n/messages/*.json` (scoped to `landing_*`) and
`apps/web/index.html`, while this test stays as the unit's own regression guard. The positive framing is fixed too: the
page says the product is **inspired by engram theory**, and describes the 3D layout as a visualization that emerges
from shared neurons and synapse strength.

**Store-listing copy** ([I12]) is authored here as policy text — the same five rules applied to the app-store
description that stands in for a mobile marketing route.

##### The origin's SEO root, and two stale deployment claims

This unit takes ownership of the origin root — it belongs to the app, not to the blog served beneath it

##### Parity waiver

`landing-page` ships **web only**. A native app has no marketing route — a person who installed the app has already
converted — and mobile's entry mapping instead sends `'landing'` to the login stack. The waiver is written out in full
here because `scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` does **not** enforce web↔mobile page peering, so an unstated waiver is
silently broken rather than caught; it follows the admin-console precedent (`spec/policy/ops/admin.md` §6: "the
web↔mobile parity rule is deliberately waived for it", echoed in `spec/tech/admin-console.md` §8 and
[58.admin-console](58.admin-console.md)). The parity matrix pairs the waiver with an obligation
([00.overview](00.overview.md) §4): the [I12]-reviewed **store-listing copy** is this plan's deliverable in place of
the route.

##### Golden-parity stance

**No parity obligation, by dependency shape.** This unit defines no pure domain function and mirrors none. Since
change 09 the walkthrough **consumes** golden-parity-pinned functions read-only — `effectiveStrength`,
`decayStageText` and `reshape` from `@cosimosi/memory-logic`, `starChannels`/`currentDecayText` from
`@cosimosi/universe`, `toEmotionSlices`/`arousalToInitialStrength` from `packages/emotion` — over its authored fixture,
exactly as the demo does; it re-implements nothing, so the parity pins themselves are the drift guard.
`scripts/lint-fsd-layout.mjs` R4 has nothing to catch: the slice is web-only and has no mobile twin.

The one shared module it edits, `packages/auth/src/gate-decision.ts`, is pure, unit-tested and consumed by both apps,
and it has **no Go counterpart** — server-side gating is `platform.AuthInterceptor`, a different mechanism with a
different authority — so there is no fixture to align. Its drift guard is the exhaustive switch in `requiresSignIn`.
The hero's five numbers are generated `@cosimosi/config` constants whose freshness `pnpm check:gen` already gates.
