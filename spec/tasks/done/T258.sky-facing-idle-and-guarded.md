## result

- outcome: `SkySphere`'s facing frame callback hands its work to the pure `frameSkyFacing(orientation, held, plan, seconds, delta)`. While nothing is faced and nothing is held, it returns before composing a spin, computing a step or touching the quaternion. The per-render inputs (static facing, spin, lambda, instant, scratch) sit in a memoized `SkyFacingPlan`, so a frame allocates nothing. Unfaced skies (universe, demo, sign-in, empty sky, `/design`) do no facing work per frame and render as before. The walkthrough scene's `skyFacing` prop is required as `number | undefined`. Removing the section's `skyFacing={facts.skyFacing}` forward now fails `tsc` with TS2741 (checked, then restored); the section still passes `facts.skyFacing` unchanged. The `VIEW_DIRECTION` comment now says that −Z is the frame centre only for a canvas camera looking down −Z, as the walkthrough's staged camera does. The two test comments now state what their tests pin. `SkySphere.test.ts` adds two cases. One checks that a no-facing frame returns false, leaves the quaternion and the scratch at identity, and never reads the spin (counting getters). The other checks that a faced frame holds the spun anchor on −Z and that dropping the facing returns to identity.
